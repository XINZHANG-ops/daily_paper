SLA: Scaling Spatial-Language-Action Model via Continual Pre-training

Published: 2026-11-04

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08373

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents SLA, a spatially aware vision-language-action foundation model for general robotic manipulation, situated in robot learning, embodied AI, 3D perception, and continual pre-training. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Prior 2D-pretrained VLAs and post-training-only Spatial Forcing provide limited transferable spatial grounding, whereas SLA proposes aligning intermediate VLA visual tokens with a frozen 3D perception teacher during both continual pre-training and post-training. 3. **❓ Problem:** It addresses how to develop scalable, transferable spatial understanding and precise 3D control across heterogeneous robots, tasks, and scenes without altering the original inference architecture or requiring depth and calibration at deployment. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** SLA initializes from π0.5, trains on a unified corpus drawn from six sources, aligns OmniVGGT-derived spatial targets through adaptive multi-layer gating and cosine loss, applies state dropout and rotation-vector end-effector actions, and discards all auxiliary modules after training. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** SLA reports state-of-the-art results of 87.4% on LIBERO-Plus, 72.3% Clean2Random and 79.2% average on RoboTwin 2.0, 68.4% with 50 demonstrations per task on RoboCasa-Panda, and 33.9% success on RoboDojo-Real, plus 83.8% average across eight real-world conditions, 24.0 percentage points above π0.5.

SplatAnything: Gaussian Spacing Normalization for Feed-forward 3DGS across Arbitrary Scenes

Published: 2026-11-05

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.08264

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper addresses feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting for generalizable novel-view synthesis and cross-scene, cross-view 3D reconstruction. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on geometry-foundation-model and feed-forward 3DGS pipelines that use radial-distance normalization, it introduces Gaussian Spacing Normalization (GSNorm), which canonicalizes the median spacing between neighboring Gaussian centers, alongside a systematic extrapolation benchmark. 3. **❓ Problem:** Radial-distance normalization leaves Gaussian spacing highly variable across scene scales, camera configurations, and resolutions, creating ambiguous scale supervision that causes holes, over-smoothing, and weak cross-domain and viewpoint generalization. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The method uses a frozen WorldMirror 2.0 geometry model, rescales predicted depth and camera translations by the median Gaussian spacing, predicts per-pixel Gaussian attributes with a backbone and GSHead, merges redundant primitives in voxels, and trains with MSE and LPIPS losses on diverse datasets and view-sampling patterns. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Across seven datasets, 1–64 input views, multiple resolutions, and controlled extrapolated viewpoints, the method achieves state-of-the-art perceptual results on most benchmarks, while ablations consistently outperform radial-distance normalization and post-hoc scale calibration, although performance remains dependent on reliable geometry and visible input coverage.

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: Harness-S is a reinforcement-learning framework for training generalizable LLM agents that uses agent harnesses—prompts, tool interfaces, context, memory, and execution rules—as complementary sources of experience for policy improvement. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on harness optimization (Meta-Harness, HarnessX, Life-Harness), harness–policy co-evolution (WHALE, HarnessForge, HarnessBandit), and model routing, the paper's new idea is to keep a fixed pool of sibling harnesses and use them only to rescue base-harness failures so their elicited behaviors are internalized into one shared policy that still works under the original harness. 3. **❓ Problem:** It addresses the locality, complementarity, and nonstationarity of harness evolution, which make a universally superior harness hard to build, couple harness search to policy updates at high cost, and cause improvements to vanish when the trained policy returns to the base harness. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The method combines Directional Harness Evolution for building the reusable Sibling Harness Pool, Complementary Harness Rescue Training that replaces all-failure base groups with top-k sibling rollouts for GRPO updates, and an Adaptive Harness Router using behavior profiles, multi-LLM voting, and policy-aware profile re-anchoring. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** On AppWorld, SWE-Gym, and DBBench with Qwen3.5-4B/9B, Harness-S outperforms policy-only training under H0 (e.g., AppWorld 4B Pass@3 67.56% vs. 60.31% for GRPO), yields the highest effective-group (79.38%) and effective-token (80.88%) rates, improves rescue success and final success with adaptive profiles, transfers behaviors to held-out instances and unseen harnesses, and gains further from Rank-1 routing at equal rollout count while requiring fewer search rollouts than joint-adaptation baselines.

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read SLA first: it offers the broadest practical advance, injecting transferable 3D spatial reasoning into general-purpose robotic manipulation while preserving the original inference architecture and requiring no depth sensor at deployment. SLA and GSNorm are complementary geometry-focused papers—the former distills spatial knowledge from a 3D teacher, while the latter normalizes scale ambiguity in feed-forward Gaussian reconstruction. Harness-S also shares a useful training principle: auxiliary scaffolding is used only during learning and then internalized, as sibling harnesses rescue failed rollouts while SLA’s spatial modules are discarded after training.
建议优先阅读 SLA：它在不改变推理架构、部署时也无需深度传感器的前提下，将可迁移的三维空间理解引入通用机器人操作，工程影响最全面。SLA 与 GSNorm 都关注几何先验和尺度一致性，前者从三维教师中蒸馏空间知识，后者则通过高斯间距归一化缓解重建中的尺度歧义。Harness-S 在方法理念上也与 SLA 相通：辅助结构仅用于训练并最终被内化——兄弟 harness 补救失败 rollout，而 SLA 的空间模块在训练后被移除。
