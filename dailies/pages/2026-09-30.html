Paper 1

MaLiang-Harness: A Programmable Path to Image and Video Generation

Published: 2026-09-27

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34309

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on programmable image and video generation using Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) to produce executable visual programs, spanning the fields of computer vision, generative AI, and code-based visual synthesis.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds upon diffusion models, flow matching, executable visual representations (e.g., VISPROG, Design2Code, BlenderAlchemy), and agent harnesses for visual generation. Its new idea is defining the "Program-to-Visual (P2V) Gap" and introducing a stateful, unified framework (MaLiang-Harness) that organizes MLLM-driven visual creation into a persistent process of construction, inspection, and revision through three coordinated mechanisms.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the discrepancy where a program can execute correctly yet produce images or videos that fail to satisfy user intent regarding composition, appearance, or motion—termed the Program-to-Visual (P2V) gap.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes MaLiang-Harness, featuring three designs: (1) Persistent Executable Generation (PEG) state that preserves programs and task context across revisions; (2) Traceable Generation Process (TGP) that links edits to rendered visual evidence; and (3) Revision-aware Editing and Verification (REV) that supports restoration from history and verifies the current revision before completion. The framework supports multiple rendering backends (Canvas, SVG, Scene2d, Three.js) for both image and video generation.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On MaLiang-IBench (50 image tasks) and MaLiang-VBench (13 video tasks), GPT-6-Astra achieved 100% generation success on both benchmarks, with 96.0% of image tasks and 76.9% of video tasks meeting all quality thresholds. Results reveal that similarly scored models on general benchmarks (e.g., AA Intelligence Index) can differ substantially in visual generation performance, indicating that general capability scores are imperfect predictors of visual program generation ability.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文专注于使用多模态大语言模型（MLLM）生成可执行视觉程序来实现可编程的图像与视频生成，涵盖计算机视觉、生成式AI和基于代码的视觉合成领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于扩散模型、流匹配、可执行视觉表示（如VISPROG、Design2Code、BlenderAlchemy）以及智能体工具架构进行研究。其新思路在于定义了"程序到视觉（P2V）差距"，并提出了一个统一的有状态框架（MaLiang-Harness），通过三种协调机制将MLLM驱动的视觉创作组织为构建、检查和修订的持续过程。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了程序能够正确执行但生成的图像或视频却无法在构图、外观或运动方面满足用户意图的差异问题，这一现象被称为程序到视觉（P2V）差距。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了MaLiang-Harness，包含三种设计：(1) 持久可执行生成（PEG）状态，跨修订版本保留程序和任务上下文；(2) 可追溯生成过程（TGP），将编辑操作与渲染的视觉证据相关联；(3) 修订感知编辑与验证（REV），支持从历史版本恢复并在完成前对当前修订进行验证。该框架支持多种渲染后端（Canvas、SVG、Scene2d、Three.js）用于图像和视频生成。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在MaLiang-IBench（50个图像任务）和MaLiang-VBench（13个视频任务）上，GPT-6-Astra在两个基准上均实现了100%的生成成功率，其中96.0%的图像任务和76.9%的视频任务满足所有质量阈值。结果表明，在通用基准（如AA Intelligence Index）上得分相似的模型在视觉生成性能上可能存在显著差异，说明通用能力评分并不能完全预测视觉程序生成能力。

Paper 2

What Makes World Action Models Generalize? An Empirical Study of Test-Time Future Modeling

Published: 2026-09-28

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34981

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on World Action Models (WAMs) for language-conditioned visuomotor robot control, specifically examining inference-time future modeling within the domain of embodied AI and generalizable robot policies.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior WAM research, including the explicit paradigm (Ye et al., Cheang et al., Hu et al.) that denoises future frames at inference and the latent paradigm (Yuan et al., Ni et al.) that skips video generation at inference. The new idea is that fully noised future video tokens processed in a single forward pass are sufficient to recover the generalization benefits, eliminating the need for iterative video denoising.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses whether inference-time video generation is necessary for WAM generalization, resolving the disputed trade-off between explicit WAMs (strong generalization but high computational cost) and latent WAMs (efficient but degraded generalization).
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors conduct a controlled comparison of explicit and latent WAMs along three generalization axes (environmental perturbation, data efficiency, and task generalization), and propose Simple-WAM, which performs a single forward pass over fully noised future video tokens combined with a mixed training-time noise schedule that increases the probability p=0.5 of sampling at τ=1.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Simple-WAM leads the explicit paradigm on 7 of 8 generalization measurements and the latent one on all 8, reaching 79.5% under environmental perturbation (vs. 67.7% and 53.8%), while running at 3.8× the speed of the explicit paradigm and within 1.2× the latency of the latent one.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于面向语言条件视觉运动机器人控制的世界动作模型（WAMs），具体研究测试时未来建模，属于具身智能与可泛化机器人策略领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于先前的WAM研究，包括显式范式（Ye等人、Cheang等人、Hu等人）在推理时去噪未来帧，以及隐式范式（Yuan等人、Ni等人）在推理时跳过视频生成。新思路是：通过单次前向传播处理完全加噪的未来视频标记即可恢复泛化收益，无需迭代式视频去噪。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了推理时视频生成对WAM泛化是否必要的争议问题，化解了显式WAM（泛化强但计算成本高）与隐式WAM（高效但泛化退化）之间被认为存在的权衡矛盾。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者沿三个泛化轴（环境扰动、数据效率、任务泛化）对显式与隐式WAM进行受控对比，并提出Simple-WAM，对完全加噪的未来视频标记执行单次前向传播，同时采用混合训练噪声调度，将τ=1处的采样概率提升至p=0.5。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Simple-WAM在8项泛化指标中有7项领先显式范式，在全部8项上领先隐式范式，环境扰动下达到79.5%（对比67.7%和53.8%），同时运行速度为显式范式的3.8倍，且延迟仅在隐式范式的1.2倍以内。

PanoVLN: Towards Effective Panoramic Vision-and-Language Navigation

Published: 2026-09-27

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34759

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) with panoramic visual observations, situated in the fields of embodied AI, computer vision, and robotics.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds upon prior VLM-based VLN methods using perspective images (e.g., NaVid, StreamVLN, JanusVLN, DualVLN) and panoramic geometry models (e.g., PanoVGGT), and introduces the new idea that exploiting panoramic context requires targeted adaptations to action prediction, training supervision, and visual representation rather than simple input replacement.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the problem that merely swapping perspective images with 360° panoramas yields only limited navigation improvements, and seeks to determine how to fully leverage wider visual context for more accurate and efficient vision-and-language navigation.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes PanoVLN with three key components: (1) longer action-sequence supervision (H=18 horizon) combined with Confidence-Guided Execution (CGE) that adaptively selects execution length based on action uncertainty; (2) a decision-centric training dataset of 98K trajectories across 800 HM3D scenes with frequent branching points and verified instructions; and (3) geometry-aware visual representation that fuses semantic features from a Qwen3.5-4B VLM with aligned geometric features from a frozen PanoVGGT encoder on RGB panoramas without adding visual tokens.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: PanoVLN achieves state-of-the-art success rates of 77.3% on R2R-CE and 78.0% on RxR-CE Val-Unseen, surpassing the previous best by 11.9% and 8.7 percentage points respectively; on a Unitree Go2 quadruped robot, it achieves faster real-world navigation (86.4s vs. 117.8–412.9s) with fewer pauses (5.4 vs. 8.3–95.3) and policy calls (7.4 vs. 29.4–101.7) compared to prior baselines.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文专注于基于全景视觉观察的视觉-语言导航（VLN），属于具身人工智能、计算机视觉和机器人学领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该论文基于此前使用透视图像的VLM-based VLN方法（如NaVid、StreamVLN、JanusVLN、DualVLN）以及全景几何模型（如PanoVGGT），并提出新思路：充分利用全景上下文需要对动作预测、训练监督和视觉表征进行针对性调整，而非简单地替换输入图像。
3. ❓ 问题: 该论文解决了简单地将透视图像替换为360°全景图像只能带来有限导航性能提升的问题，并探索如何充分利用更宽的视觉上下文来实现更准确、更高效的视觉-语言导航。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该论文提出了PanoVLN，包含三个关键组件：(1) 更长的动作序列监督（H=18预测范围）结合置信度引导执行（CGE），根据动作不确定性自适应选择执行长度；(2) 一个以决策为中心的训练数据集，包含跨800个HM3D场景的98K条轨迹，具有频繁的分叉点和经过验证的指令；(3) 几何感知的视觉表征，将Qwen3.5-4B VLM的语义特征与冻结的PanoVGGT编码器从RGB全景图中提取的对齐几何特征相融合，无需增加额外的视觉token。
5. 📊 结果与评估: PanoVLN在R2R-CE和RxR-CE Val-Unseen上分别取得了77.3%和78.0%的最先进成功率，比此前最优结果分别高出11.9和8.7个百分点；在Unitree Go2四足机器人上的真实世界实验中，相比此前基线实现了更快的导航速度（86.4秒 vs. 117.8–412.9秒）、更少的暂停次数（5.4 vs. 8.3–95.3）以及更少的策略调用次数（7.4 vs. 29.4–101.7）。
