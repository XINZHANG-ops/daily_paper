Paper 1

Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs

Published: 2026-10-28

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.39302

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper develops a theoretical and empirical account of cross-modal Platonic representation learning with Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs), spanning self-supervised learning, vision-language alignment, language modeling, and video representation learning. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on the Platonic Representation Hypothesis, spectral self-supervised learning, JEPAs, and earlier limited alignment proofs, it introduces the Platonic Representation Theorem, good-view versus bad-view JEPAs, an interpretation of next-token prediction as a bad-view JEPA, and an alignment scaling law. 3. **❓ Problem:** The paper addresses when separately trained encoders from different modalities provably converge to a shared world representation, why existing objectives often align only through scale, and how positive-pair semantics and modality-specific nuisance govern this process. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The authors analyze finite-world population objectives using injective renderings, whitening, spectral embeddings, kernel-mismatch and nuisance bounds, and Davis–Kahan-style reasoning, then evaluate LeJEPA variants across Flickr, COCO, MSR-VTT, and web-text datasets using retrieval, mutual-kNN, CKA, zero-shot classification, and captioning. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Under idealized shared-positive and rotation-unique-minimizer assumptions, the theorem guarantees agreement up to rotation, while experiments show that good views outperform bad views and next-token prediction, achieve equivalent alignment with roughly 42-fold fewer samples, enable a 109M text JEPA to surpass evaluated language models up to 72B parameters, and yield CIDEr .838 and 64.4% pair-free ImageNet-100 top-5 accuracy, although real modalities and training objectives do not satisfy all theorem assumptions.

OmniShowcase: Omnidirectional Subject-Consistent Video Generation from Multi-View Images

Published: 2026-10-25

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35684

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents OmniShowcase, a generative computer-vision system for subject-driven omnidirectional video synthesis that preserves a target’s identity and 3D structure across viewpoints.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on diffusion-based subject-to-video models and feed-forward multi-view reconstruction, it introduces 3D-asset-based training data and a trajectory-independent surface-normal turntable that conditions generation without framewise alignment or subject-specific optimization.
3. ❓ Problem: Prior single- and multi-view methods leave unseen surfaces underconstrained, causing identity drift, geometric deformation, incomplete camera motion, and inconsistent 3D structure across generated frames.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The method renders four references and 80-frame targets from 3D assets across 88 camera trajectories, composites realistic backgrounds, reconstructs reference-view geometry as 3D Gaussians and normal maps, and injects the references and normal turntable into a LoRA-tuned image-to-video diffusion transformer through temporal in-context conditioning.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On SHOWCASEBENCH and NAVI, OmniShowcase achieves the best DINO, CLIP, MEt3R, and GT-CD scores while retaining competitive text alignment and video quality, and human raters prefer it on 70.9%–79.4% of comparisons, although synthetic-looking backgrounds and non-rigid subjects remain limitations.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文提出 OmniShowcase，一种面向主体驱动全方向视频生成的计算机视觉与生成式人工智能系统，旨在跨视角保持目标主体的身份和三维结构。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在扩散式主体视频生成和前馈多视图重建的基础上，论文引入基于三维资产的训练数据以及与目标轨迹无关的表面法线转盘条件，从而无需逐帧对齐或针对每个主体单独优化。
3. ❓ 问题: 现有单视图和多视图方法难以约束未观测表面，容易在生成过程中出现身份漂移、几何形变、相机轨迹不完整以及跨帧三维结构不一致。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该方法从三维资产生成四个参考视图和覆盖88种相机轨迹的80帧目标视频并合成真实背景，同时由参考视图重建三维高斯与法线图，再通过时间维上下文拼接将参考图和法线转盘注入经LoRA微调的图像到视频扩散Transformer。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在SHOWCASEBENCH和NAVI上，OmniShowcase取得最佳的DINO、CLIP、MEt3R和GT-CD指标，同时保持有竞争力的文本对齐与视频质量，并在人类对比中以70.9%–79.4%的偏好率胜出，但其背景偶显合成感且无法处理非刚性主体。

Look Only When Needed: Certificate-Guided Visual Routing for Efficient Diffusion VLMs

Published: 2026-10-26

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36671

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper addresses efficient inference for diffusion vision-language models, focusing on adaptive visual-token routing during iterative denoising. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Unlike prior visual-token pruning, intermediate-state caching, and fixed parallel-decoding schedules, LOWN introduces image-access scheduling that uses prediction agreement to determine both when the image is needed and how many tokens can be unmasked. 3. **❓ Problem:** DVLMs repeatedly process an unchanged image that occupies 89–97% of the input, while pruning permanently discards visual information and fixed schedules cannot adapt to task- and trajectory-dependent visual demand. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** LOWN stores the latest image-conditioned prediction as an anchor, uses a cheap text-only forward to certify a confidence-ordered prefix through prediction agreement, runs a full forward only when certification is insufficient, and jointly controls image access and unmasking without training or tuned thresholds. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** On ten benchmarks, LOWN preserves 99.6% of LLaDA-V’s baseline score with 0.353× decoding FLOPs and achieves the best matched-budget score on eight tasks, while also transferring to LaViDa and VidLaDA and composing effectively with token pruning and key-value caching.
