Looking Ahead: Modeling Future Evolution in Autoregressive Video Generation

Published: 2026-10-20

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29989

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies future-evolution modeling and physical plausibility in autoregressive video diffusion for generative world modeling.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on teacher-forced flow-matching video generation, it introduces Future Chunk Prediction (FCP), a parallel, text-free auxiliary objective that predicts nonadjacent future chunks from the current chunk’s representation without intervening context.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses the teacher-forcing shortcut whereby strong temporal redundancy lets models reconstruct the current chunk through local continuity without adequately learning scene dynamics.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The method jointly optimizes teacher-forced denoising with offset-specific lightweight DiT predictors conditioned on fused multi-layer clean and noisy features through token-wise AdaLN, while removing all auxiliary modules at inference.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Under matched settings, FCP raises Physics-IQ Verified IQ from 27.6 to 30.9 at 1.3B and 5B I2V/V2V scores from 30.4/40.6 to 33.8/44.4, improves three additional physics benchmarks, requires 42.6–50.7% less training compute than full-context prediction, and yields more structured representations.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究生成式世界建模中自回归视频扩散的未来演化建模与物理合理性。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在教师强制流匹配视频生成的基础上，论文提出未来块预测（FCP），以并行、无文本的辅助目标在不提供中间上下文的情况下，从当前块表征预测非相邻未来块。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决教师强制训练中强时间冗余使模型依赖局部连续性重建当前块、因而未能充分学习场景动态的捷径问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该方法将教师强制去噪与按未来偏移设置的轻量DiT预测器联合训练，通过逐标记AdaLN注入由多层干净和噪声特征融合而成的条件，并在推理时移除全部辅助模块。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在匹配设置下，FCP将1.3B模型的Physics-IQ Verified IQ从27.6提升至30.9，并将5B模型的I2V/V2V得分从30.4/40.6提升至33.8/44.4，同时改善三个额外物理基准，较全上下文预测减少42.6–50.7%的训练计算量并形成更有结构的表征。

Paper 2

S-Agent 2.0: Spatial Reasoning with a General Coding Agent

Published: 2026-10-19

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28354

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on spatial reasoning in AI, specifically building an agentic system that performs 3D spatial reasoning across multiple images and videos, situated at the intersection of multimodal vision-language models, agentic AI systems, and 3D perception.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior agentic VLMs for spatial reasoning like S-Agent, SpatialClaw, SpaceTools, and pySpatial, which all invest heavily in acquisition (perception/geometry libraries). Its new idea is to invert the design: instead of a specialized controller, use a stock general coding agent and focus the design effort on a CLI perception toolkit that closes the "evidence loop" by joining the model's perception with tools' measurements, keeping evidence in a persistent scene memory, and returning readings already interpreted in spatial language.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the gap between model and tools in spatial reasoning—where tools return correct measurements but the VLM still fails to produce correct answers—caused by (1) misreading correct readings (sign/frame errors) and (2) acquired evidence never being used in the final answer because the model falls back on its own perception detached from the measurements.
4. 🛠️ Methods: S-Agent 2.0 combines a general-purpose coding agent (OpenHands/Codex/Claude Code) with a command-line perception toolkit featuring: (i) tool perception (recon, segment) and model perception (add_instance, drop) writes to a shared scene memory; (ii) persistent per-episode scene memory storing frames, metric point clouds, and named instances with geometry/pose; and (iii) readouts that return answer-ready spatial-language conclusions (e.g., side, distance, motion, compass, elevation) instead of bare numbers.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: S-Agent 2.0 achieves 66.5 on MMSI-Bench and 82.3 on ReVSI with Qwen3.8-27B, and 74.1 and 90.5 with Gemini-3.8-Flash, leading SpatialClaw and other agentic baselines on all five benchmarks tested; the toolkit adds +13.9/+20.5 points over the same agent without tools on Qwen3.8-27B, maintains substantial gains on the stronger Gemini backbone where SpatialClaw's gains shrink, and works across OpenHands, Codex, and Claude Code harnesses, with ablations confirming all three interface components contribute.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本论文聚焦于AI的空间推理，具体研究如何构建一个能在多图和视频上执行3D空间推理的智能体系统，处于多模态视觉语言模型、智能体系统与3D感知交叉领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于先前面向空间推理的智能体VLM（如S-Agent、SpatialClaw、SpaceTools、pySpatial），这些系统都在感知获取（感知/几何库）上投入大量精力。论文的新思路是颠倒设计：不使用专用控制器，而是采用现成的通用编码智能体，并将设计精力集中在一个命令行感知工具包上，通过将模型感知与工具测量统一、保持证据于持久场景记忆、并以空间语言返回解读结果来"闭合证据环"。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了空间推理中模型与工具之间的鸿沟——工具返回正确的测量结果，但VLM仍无法给出正确答案，原因有二：（1）误读正确的读数（符号/参考系错误）；（2）已获取的证据从未被用于最终答案，因为模型的推理脱离了测量结果而回退到自身感知。
4. 🛠️ 方法: S-Agent 2.0将通用编码智能体（OpenHands/Codex/Claude Code）与命令行感知工具包相结合，工具包包括：（i）工具感知（recon、segment）与模型感知（add_instance、drop）共同写入场景记忆；（ii）持久化的每集场景记忆，存储帧、度量点云和带几何/姿态的命名实例；（iii）以"即可作答"的空间语言结论返回的读出函数（如side、distance、motion、compass、elevation），而非裸数字。
5. 📊 结果与评估: S-Agent 2.0在Qwen3.8-27B上取得MMSI-Bench 66.5、ReVSI 82.3，在Gemini-3.8-Flash上取得74.1和90.5，在测试的全部五个基准上领先SpatialClaw及其他智能体基线；工具包在Qwen3.8-27B上比无工具时提升+13.9/+20.5分，在更强的Gemini骨干上仍保持显著增益（而SpatialClaw的增益大幅缩小），且在OpenHands、Codex、Claude Code三种编码智能体框架下均有效；消融实验证实三个接口组件均有贡献。

Paper 3

Cache Engineering for Agents

Published: 2026-10-19

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.28577

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents cache engineering for long-horizon LLM agents, focusing on KV-cache management, inference serving, and efficient attention as agent trajectories grow.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on context summarization and trimming, KV-cache compression, eviction, reuse, and prefix-caching systems, it proposes programmable direct control of active KV caches through append, drop, and repos operations without rewriting trajectories or re-prefilling retained states.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses the cost and accuracy dilemma of growing KV caches, where cache I/O and storage become expensive, context limits require history reduction, edits invalidate downstream caches, and history-dependent cache states break ordinary prefix reuse.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors formalize history-sensitive cache-operation sequences and implement AKV with a stateless interface, an extended radix tree, drop- and reposition-aware reuse and eviction, and single-pass segment-batch attention, while evaluating rolling-drop, tool-directed, and recursively self-improved policies.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across three LLMs and four agent benchmarks, cache engineering improves average benchmark scores by about 1.7 points and reduces average cost by 27.7% versus threshold-triggered summarization, while AKV achieves up to 1.2× output throughput and 1.5× faster P95 latency, with RSI reducing costs another 7.1% over expert design.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出面向长程大语言模型智能体的“缓存工程”，聚焦KV缓存管理、推理服务以及轨迹增长条件下高效注意力。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在上下文摘要与裁剪、KV缓存压缩与淘汰复用以及前缀缓存服务系统的基础上，论文提出通过append、drop和repos操作以可编程方式直接控制活动KV缓存，而不重写轨迹或重新预填充保留状态。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决KV缓存持续增长带来的成本与准确率困境，即缓存读写和存储开销巨大、上下文长度限制迫使模型压缩历史，而历史编辑会使后续缓存失效，且缓存状态依赖操作历史并导致传统前缀复用失效。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者形式化具有历史依赖性的缓存操作序列，设计支持无状态接口的AKV服务系统，并利用扩展基数树、支持丢弃与重定位的复用和淘汰机制及单次前向的分段批注意力实现这些操作，同时评估滚动丢弃、工具控制策略和递归自我改进策略。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在三个大语言模型和四个智能体基准上，相比阈值触发的摘要，该方法平均成绩提高约1.7分、平均成本降低27.7%，AKV最高实现1.2倍输出吞吐和1.5倍更快的P95延迟，RSI还使成本较专家设计进一步降低7.1%。
