Paper 1

Jeveration: System One Models Now Generate

Published: 2026-11-17

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24738

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: Jeveration is a multimodal computer-vision paper that reframes text-to-image generation, image-to-image synthesis, and dense prediction as parallel typed decisions produced by a frozen vision-language model.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on Jev-style System One models, masked-token and diffusion generators, and Di[M]O distribution matching, the paper introduces shared-random-state conditioning so that parallel image-code decisions remain globally coherent and one checkpoint serves both generation and perception.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses the latency of iterative or autoregressive image generation, the incoherence caused by naive independent code selection, and the difficulty of jointly supporting stochastic generation and deterministic dense prediction.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors attach decision tokens, shared rank-256 LoRA adapters, and task-specific heads to frozen Qwen3.5-4B, represent images with 128 TA-TiTok codes, train first through masked prediction and then through distribution matching with confidence-guided correction, and use spatial queries for dense maps.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On one H200, Jeveration generates 512×512 images in 77.8 ms with a 0.74 GenEval score, is 122× faster than Janus-Pro-7B, achieves strong image-to-image structural fidelity, matches dense-only segmentation performance, and retains nearly all text-to-image quality during joint training.
1. 📘 主题与领域: Jeveration是一篇多模态计算机视觉论文，将文本生成图像、图像到图像生成和稠密预测统一重构为冻结视觉语言模型并行输出的类型化决策。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于Jev式系统一模型、掩码令牌与扩散生成器及Di[M]O分布匹配，引入共享随机状态使并行图像码决策保持全局一致，并由单一检查点同时完成生成与感知。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决迭代式或自回归图像生成延迟高、朴素独立选码导致图像不连贯，以及难以统一支持随机图像生成和确定性稠密预测的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者在冻结的Qwen3.5-4B上加入决策令牌、共享的秩256 LoRA适配器和任务专用头，用128个TA-TiTok码表示图像，依次采用掩码预测、分布匹配和置信度引导校正进行训练，并通过空间查询预测稠密图。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Jeveration在单张H200上以77.8毫秒生成512×512图像并取得0.74的GenEval分数，速度是Janus-Pro-7B的122倍，同时具备较强的图像到图像结构一致性、接近稠密专用模型的分割性能，并在联合训练中几乎不损失文本生成图像质量。

Paper 2

Game2Player: Scaling Gameplay Experience for Visual Agents through Environment-Player Co-Development

Published: 2026-11-17

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24154

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents Game2Player, a framework for scaling gameplay experience for vision-language-model visual agents by co-developing verifiable web-game environments together with the agents that play them, situated at the intersection of interactive game AI, computer-use agents, and multimodal (pixel-based) agent learning. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on interactive game benchmarks (GameWorld, PlaySuite, LMGame-Bench), gameplay corpora with inferred actions (VPT, NitroGen, PLAYER-1, GameScaling), automated environment synthesis from code/web, and privileged-state distillation, the paper's new ideas are environment–player co-development (a Builder agent plus a VLM Player and a Coding Player whose defect reports and observation requests drive verified environment repairs), progress-weighted SFT distillation of both players' episodes, and the held-out PlayerBench benchmark. 3. **❓ Problem:** The paper addresses the lack of scalable, goal-labeled and state-verifiable gameplay trajectories for training visual agents across diverse games, since human gameplay is expensive, online videos lack actions and outcomes, existing datasets lack explicit tasks or verifiable success, and per-game engineering plus silent defects in auto-generated environments cap coverage. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** A Scout selects playable offline web games, a Builder agent instruments each game with a state API (a scoring-only state probe and a screen-faithful player observation), control guide, start routine, and state-based progress/success functions, and then a VLM Player (screenshot-only, pausing per step, emitting action plus note/thinking) and a Coding Player (Python policy over the player observation, real-time, no model calls) play every task and file reports that the Builder verifies against recordings before repairing the environment; accepted environments then yield trajectories that are segmented, filtered, weighted by progress events/credits/penalties with stochastic-rounding resampling and game balancing, and distilled into Qwen3.5-4B/9B by progress-weighted SFT. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** The pipeline produces 568 verified games, 3,436 tasks, 15.7K trajectories (307 hours) across 25+ genres and seven engines, with a 20-game/145-task study showing player reports cut incorrect environments from 51 to 40 and raise code-policy-solved tasks from 34 to 46 (62→82 with a GPT-6.1 Sol Builder) at ~20% extra cost, and code policies recording 22× more steps per hour than the VLM Player (7,775 vs 354) while being cheaper after ~12 minutes of play; training on this data roughly doubles Qwen3.5-9B's PlayerBench progress (14.3%→31.2%) and raises GameWorld progress (30.0%→53.1%), making Game2Player-9B the best sub-10B open model on both benchmarks, with ablations showing VLM data is more sample-efficient on PlayerBench, code-policy data transfers better to GameWorld, progress weighting beats uniform sampling, and training makes inference-time thinking reliably helpful.

1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出 Game2Player 框架，通过让游戏环境与其玩家（测试者）协同开发来为视觉语言模型（VLM）智能体规模化获取游戏经验，属于交互式游戏 AI、计算机操作智能体与多模态（纯像素）智能体学习领域。 2. **💡 先前研究与新思路：** 论文建立在交互式游戏基准（GameWorld、PlaySuite、LMGame-Bench）、带动作推断的游戏数据集（VPT、NitroGen、PLAYER-1、GameScaling）、基于代码/网页的自动环境合成以及特权状态蒸馏等工作的之上；其新思路是"环境—玩家协同开发"（Builder 加 VLM Player 与 Coding Player，由玩家提交的缺陷报告和观测请求驱动经验证的修复）、对两类玩家轨迹的进度加权 SFT 蒸馏，以及留出集基准 PlayerBench。 3. **❓ 问题：** 论文要解决的是缺乏可规模化、具备明确目标且可状态验证的游戏轨迹数据的问题，因为人类游玩采集成本高、在线视频缺少动作与结果、已有数据集缺少显式任务或可验证的成功判定，且逐游戏工程化加上自动生成环境中不易察觉的缺陷限制了覆盖面。 4. **🛠️ 方法：** Scout 筛选可离线单人游玩的网页游戏，Builder 代理为每个游戏接入状态 API（仅用于评分的状态探针与忠实于屏幕的玩家观测）、控制指南、起始例程以及基于状态的进度与成功函数；随后 VLM Player（仅看截图、每步暂停、输出动作并附带说明与思考）与 Coding Player（仅读取玩家观测的 Python 策略、实时运行、无需模型调用）试玩每个任务并提交报告，Builder 依据录像核实后修复环境，通过脚本门控后环境被接受并转为数据采集，最终经动作切分、过滤、基于进度事件的加权与随机取整重采样及游戏均衡，用进度加权 SFT 蒸馏到 Qwen3.5-4B/9B。 5. **📊 结果与评估：** 流水线产出 568 个已验证游戏、3,436 个任务、15.7K 条轨迹（307 小时），覆盖 25+ 品类与七种引擎；在 20 个游戏/145 个任务的消融研究中，玩家报告将错误环境从 51 降至 40、把代码策略可解任务从 34 提升到 46（以 GPT-6.1 Sol 为 Builder 时为 62→82），额外成本约 20%，且代码策略每小记录步数是 VLM Player 的 22 倍（7,775 对 354）、约 12 分钟后成本更低；用该数据训练使 Qwen3.5-9B 在 PlayerBench 的进度大致翻倍（14.3%→31.2%），在 GameWorld 上从 30.0% 提升到 53.1%，成为 10B 以下开源模型中两者最优；消融还表明 VLM 数据在 PlayerBench 上样本效率更高、代码策略数据向 GameWorld 迁移更好、进度加权优于均匀采样，且训练使推理时思考变得稳定有效。

CurvDelta: Low-Curvature Delta Modeling For Surgical Learning and Unlearning

Published: 2026-11-17

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.24654

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This preprint presents preservation-constrained, parameter-efficient adaptation of large language models for surgical capability learning and selective unlearning.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on replay, regularization, gradient or subspace projection, orthogonal LoRA, and CrispEdit, the key novelty is embedding a preservation-loss-derived low-curvature geometry directly into dense and low-rank delta parameterizations so the realized update remains constrained even under adaptive optimizers.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses catastrophic forgetting and collateral capability degradation during adaptation by requiring target learning or forgetting while keeping behavior on a designated preservation set within a tolerance, without costly retraining.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The method estimates token-level KL preservation curvature at the pretrained model using block-diagonal K-FAC, retains trailing factor eigendirections through factorwise spectral truncation, applies fixed two-sided projections, and trains either dense CurvDelta or low-rank CurvLoRA updates under a local preservation bound.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across mathematical reasoning, code generation, TOFU, and MUSE, CurvDelta preserves substantially more general capability while maintaining competitive task performance, achieves strong forget quality and near-retraining utility on the main TOFU SimNPO results, and ablations favor curvature-selected directions and projected parameterization over random subspaces and gradient projection.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本预印本提出面向大语言模型外科式能力学习与选择性遗忘的保留约束参数高效适配框架。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在回放、正则化、梯度或子空间投影、正交LoRA和CrispEdit等工作的基础上，论文的核心创新是将由保留损失导出的低曲率几何直接嵌入稠密与低秩增量参数化，使实际更新即使在自适应优化器下也始终受约束。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决适配过程中的灾难性遗忘与能力连带退化问题，使模型既能学习或遗忘目标任务，又能将指定保留集上的行为变化控制在容限内而无需昂贵重训。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 方法在预训练模型处使用块对角K-FAC估计词元级KL保留曲率，通过因子级谱截断保留尾部特征方向，应用固定的双侧投影，并在局部保留界下训练稠密版CurvDelta或低秩版CurvLoRA更新。
