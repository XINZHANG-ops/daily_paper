Paper 1

Route Anything: A Study of Model and Policy Routing for General-Purpose AI Systems

Published: 2026-11-02

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05275

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper studies unified model and policy routing for general-purpose AI systems spanning language, vision, video, speech, coding agents, and robotic manipulation.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It extends language-only cascades, preference-based routers, quality–cost optimization, and algorithm portfolios into a unified omni-modal framework, introducing outcome-based routing targets, an information ladder, and finite-sample quality-floor certification.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses how an AI system can dynamically select and coordinate specialized candidates per input while balancing quality, cost, latency, and the information available to the router.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors develop a state-dependent value–cost theory, construct a 25-benchmark trajectory matrix with 374,401 scored runs and 452 candidate configurations, train Qwen3.5-4B type, outcome, and winner-label routers, and evaluate price routes, cascades, verifiers, and retries using matched comparisons, bootstrap intervals, and independent-sample certification.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: The per-input oracle exceeds the best fixed candidate by 5.4 points, learned routers gain 0.3 points overall with 2.1-point and 1.1-point group-level gains in video and robotics, and cost-aware routing raises efficiency by 8.82× on average, reaching 25.5× for coding and 7.1× for text question answering.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究面向通用AI系统的统一模型与策略路由，覆盖语言、视觉、视频、语音、代码智能体和机器人操作等模态。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文将语言模型级联、偏好路由、质量—成本优化和算法组合拓展为统一的全模态框架，并提出面向逐候选结果的训练目标、信息阶梯和有限样本质量下限认证。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文试图解决通用AI系统如何针对每个输入动态选择并协调最合适的专用候选，同时权衡质量、成本、延迟及路由器可获得的信息。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者建立依赖状态的收益—成本理论，构建包含452种候选配置、374,401次评分运行和25个基准的全模态轨迹矩阵，训练Qwen3.5-4B类型路由、结果路由与胜者标签路由，并通过匹配比较、自举区间和独立样本认证评估价格路径、级联、验证器与重试。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 逐输入预言机比最佳固定候选高5.4分，学习路由整体提升0.3分并在视频和机器人任务上分别取得2.1分与1.1分的组别增益，而成本感知路由平均将效率提高8.82倍，其中代码任务达到25.5倍、文本问答达到7.1倍。

Behind Video Generation: Observations and Insights on Multimodal Understanding from Kling

Published: 2026-11-02

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.04916

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This technical report presents the multimodal-understanding stack behind Kling’s controllable image, video, and audio-video generation systems.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on multimodal LLMs, diffusion transformers, captioning, prompt rewriting, reward modeling, and video benchmarks, it proposes the unified data–conditioning–alignment–evaluation framework and explicit semantic contracts for generation supervision.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses how to convert broad but uneven multimodal understanding into reliable semantic control and how to trace generation failures to their actual upstream source rather than relying on aggregate quality scores.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors train a unified Kling-MLLM with a four-stage multimodal curriculum and specialize it into expert captioners, reference-grounded captioners, prompt-enhancement models, perceptual text encoders, explainable and decision reward models, and task-specific evaluators.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Experiments show strong video and audiovisual understanding, superior specialized captioning and reference binding, cumulative prompt-enhancement gains, a 1.27 overall human-preference ratio for the refined text encoder, and leading internal and public benchmark accuracy for the decision reward model.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本技术报告介绍了Kling可控图像、视频及音视频生成系统背后的多模态理解技术栈。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文综合多模态大模型、扩散Transformer、字幕生成、提示词改写、奖励模型与视频基准等已有工作，提出统一的数据—条件—对齐—评估框架，以及用于生成监督的显式语义契约。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文致力于将广泛但不均衡的多模态理解转化为可靠的语义控制，并定位生成故障的真实上游来源，而非仅依赖笼统的质量分数。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者采用四阶段多模态课程训练统一的Kling-MLLM，并将其专化为专家字幕器、参考锚定字幕器、提示增强模型、感知文本编码器、可解释与决策奖励模型以及任务特定评估器。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 实验显示该系统在视频与音视频理解、专用字幕生成和参考绑定方面表现突出，提示增强各组件可带来累积收益，改进后的文本编码器总体人类偏好比达到1.27，决策奖励模型在内外部基准上取得领先准确率。

Published: 2026-11-02

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.05377

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: In embodied AI and robot learning, this paper introduces DreamX, which repurposes a pretrained video diffusion model to learn universal latent action representations from human egocentric and robot videos. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Unlike prior latent action models (LAPA, Genie, MoTo, AdaWorld, VideoWorld2) that learn encoders or external dynamics modules from scratch on frame pairs, DreamX reads compact latent actions directly from the video diffusion backbone's spatiotemporal features and supervises them through generative future-frame reconstruction. 3. **❓ Problem:** The paper addresses how to learn action representations from video that capture motion shared across embodiments while disentangling it from embodiment and scene appearance, enabling cross-embodiment transfer and policy learning without paired human–robot data or action labels. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** DreamX appends learnable, frame-causal query tokens to a frozen Cosmos-Predict2.5 2B backbone to compress 1,200 frame tokens into 4 latent-action tokens per frame, LoRA-trains an encoder–decoder pair on 2.19k hours of human and robot videos to reconstruct future frames from the initial frame plus latent actions with optional proprioceptive action supervision, and uses a variational bottleneck (2,048→32 dims per token) so a policy can predict compact codes and low-level actions for transfer to target frames or robot states. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** DreamX achieves state-of-the-art LARYBench performance (0.17 normalized regression MSE and 69.67% classification accuracy versus DiLA's 0.38 and 62.25%), outperforms AdaWorld and VideoWorld2 on zero-shot human-to-robot video transfer (FVD 57.44, LPIPS 0.179), enables open-loop cross-embodiment action transfer, and reaches 96.7% real-world drawer-closing success with 19.1× faster inference than Mimic-Video (1,474 ms).

1. 为什么 DreamX 每个潜在帧的四个查询令牌能够表示运动，而不是单纯复制场景外观？
查询令牌以帧因果方式读取时空特征，而初始帧已经提供场景外观，因此未来帧重建会迫使查询令牌编码缺失的运动动态。
查询令牌只接收语言指令，并直接根据文本描述生成未来像素。
查询令牌被训练成固定的动作类别标签，用于选择预定义的机器人技能。
Q2
2. With no paired human–robot videos, how does DreamX turn a human demonstration into executable robot behavior?
It copies the human RGB frames to the robot scene and transfers the human joint values directly.
It retrains a target-specific latent-action encoder using only task captions.
It extracts source latent actions Qsrc and feeds the same Qsrc, together with the target robot's initial proprioceptive state, into the frozen decoder to produce low-level actions.
2. 在没有任何配对人机视频的情况下，DreamX 如何将人类演示转化为机器人可执行行为？
将人类 RGB 帧逐帧复制到机器人场景，并直接迁移人类的关节数值。
仅利用任务字幕重新训练一个目标机器人专用的潜动作编码器。
从源视频提取潜动作 Qsrc，将同一组 Qsrc 与目标机器人的初始本体状态一起输入冻结的解码器，从而生成低层动作。
Q3
3. In downstream DreamX policy learning, how do human and robot demonstrations provide supervision?
Both sources supervise only future-video generation, without latent-code or low-level-action targets.
Human videos supervise only compact latent-action codes, robot demonstrations supervise both codes and low-level actions, and the action head can attend to the latent-action features.
Only human videos are labeled; robot demonstrations are discarded because their action spaces differ.
3. 在下游 DreamX 策略学习中，人类视频和机器人示范分别如何提供监督？
两类数据都只监督未来视频生成，不涉及潜动作代码或低层动作。
人类视频只监督紧凑潜动作代码，机器人示范同时监督潜动作代码和低层动作，而且动作头可以读取潜动作特征。
只有人类视频带有监督信号；由于动作空间不同，机器人示范会被丢弃。
