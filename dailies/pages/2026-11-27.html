Paper 1

Stable Gated DeltaNet-2: Understanding and Mitigating Recurrent State Instability

Published: 2026-11-24

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34051

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies recurrent-state stability in Gated DeltaNet-2-based linear attention for efficient long-context sequence modeling.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on GDN, KDA, and GDN-2’s decoupled channel-wise erase/write gates, the paper traces instability to asymmetric erasure and proposes Stable GDN-2 with symmetric erasure and decay-regulated residual writing.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses catastrophic recurrent-state amplification, numerical overflow, capacity collapse, and long-context training failures in GDN-2, including growth that persists after new writes have ceased.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors derive the erase operator’s exact spectral gain, construct an unbounded-growth example, replace GDN-2 with \(u_t=\sqrt{b_t}\odot k_t\) and \(r_t=\sqrt{1-d_t^2}\odot(k_t-u_t)\), prove non-expansive propagation and an \(O(\sqrt T)\) state bound, and retain chunkwise parallel training for 1.6B-parameter recurrent and hybrid models.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Stable GDN-2 trains stably, improves recurrent 128K RULER by 6.80 points over GDN-2 (11.62 versus 4.82) and hybrid nine-task accuracy by 0.62 points, maintains competitive language-modeling quality, and ablations confirm the contributions of both residual writing and decay regulation.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究基于 Gated DeltaNet-2 的线性注意力在高效长上下文序列建模中的递归状态稳定性。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在 GDN、KDA 及 GDN-2 解耦通道级擦除/写入门控的基础上，论文将不稳定性归因于非对称擦除，并提出结合对称擦除与衰减调节残差写入的 Stable GDN-2。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决 GDN-2 中递归状态爆炸、数值溢出、容量坍缩及长上下文训练失败等问题，包括停止写入后仍持续增长的状态放大现象。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者推导擦除算子的精确谱增益并构造无界增长实例，随后以 \(u_t=\sqrt{b_t}\odot k_t\) 和 \(r_t=\sqrt{1-d_t^2}\odot(k_t-u_t)\) 改造 GDN-2，证明状态传播非扩张且满足 \(O(\sqrt T)\) 界，并在 16 亿参数递归与混合模型中保留分块并行训练能力。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Stable GDN-2 实现了稳定训练，将递归模型在 128K 下的 RULER 分数较 GDN-2 提高 6.80 分（11.62 对 4.82），将混合模型九项任务平均准确率提高 0.62 个百分点，同时保持有竞争力的语言建模质量，且消融实验证实残差写入和衰减调节均不可或缺。

1. Why can GDN-2's recurrent-state energy keep growing even after all subsequent value writes are set to zero?
Its erase step reads along b⊙k but modifies along k, creating a non-normal shear whose expanding directions can align across successive tokens.
A nonzero write gate continues injecting fresh key–value outer products even when the values are zero.
The zero-initialized state has unstable eigenvectors before the first token is processed.
1. 为什么即使将后续所有值写入都设为零，GDN-2 的循环状态能量仍可能持续增长？
其擦除过程沿 b⊙k 读取，却沿 k 修改，从而产生非正规剪切；其扩张方向可能在连续 token 间相互对齐。

零初始化状态在处理第一个 token 之前就已具有不稳定的特征向量。

2. Which statement agrees with Stable GDN-2's theoretical guarantees?
Without new writes, each symmetric-erasure transition is non-expansive, and bounded values with S₀=0 imply ||S_T||_F≤√(3T)V.
The O(√T) state bound requires a uniform decay cap ||d_t||∞≤ρ<1; otherwise exponential growth remains possible.

OS-Omni: Benchmarking Generalist Computer-Using Agents on Long-Horizon Workflows Across Platforms

Published: 2026-11-24

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34232

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: OS-Omni is a live, executable benchmark and execution environment for evaluating generalist computer-using agents (CUAs) on long-horizon GUI workflows spanning Windows, Ubuntu, macOS, iOS, Android, and Web, situated in the GUI-agent/embodied-AI evaluation domain. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** It extends execution-based desktop evaluation from OSWorld, WindowsAgentArena, AndroidWorld, macOSWorld, and web benchmarks such as WebArena/VisualWebArena/WorkArena, and adds three novel elements: a unified cross-platform action/observation contract with final-state evaluators, 18 benchmark-controlled "Open-series" applications (covering 60% of v1 tasks) for reproducible service-backed state, and a live benchmark released in versioned 300-task waves plus single-screen desktop↔Android cross-device workflows. 3. **❓ Problem:** Prior benchmarks isolate one platform, interface, or scoring protocol at a time, so it remains unclear whether a single generalist agent can complete realistic user goals that stretch over long horizons, heterogeneous OS conventions, and persistent application state that must survive strict post-execution inspection. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The authors build a shared orchestration layer with platform-specific adapters and deterministic state resets (VM snapshot/golden-image restore, Tart clones, ADB seeding, simulator clones), a two-level action space (free-form PyAutoGUI plus a finite structured schema with terminal DONE/FAIL), three observation modes with screenshot-only as the main protocol, and a five-stage annotation pipeline (design, independent review, cross-platform difficulty calibration, oracle execution validation, failure-state testing) that yields 300 v1 tasks that were annotated over ~2,400 person-hours; nine models are benchmarked against a human reference using final-state evaluators. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** On v1, Claude Opus 5.5 achieves the best macro-average success of 68.5% (vs. a 77.3% human reference, an 8.8-point gap), followed by GPT-6-Astra at 66.1% and Gemini 4 Argon at 58.6%, while smaller models solve almost nothing; performance is sharply platform-dependent (all frontier models exceed humans on Linux but trail by 33.5 and 25.6 points on iOS and macOS), human tasks require 67.54 steps vs. OSWorld's 15.0, and the dominant failure modes are visual grounding, long-horizon state tracking, persistence of final state, and platform/timing handling.
