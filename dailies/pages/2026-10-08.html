Long-WAM: Scaling the Context of World-Action Models

Published: 2026-10-06

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.10528

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on robotics and real-time robot manipulation, specifically scaling visual context for World-Action Models (WAMs) that integrate video prediction with action generation for closed-loop robot control.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds upon existing world-action models (DreamZero, LingBot-VA, Motus, Fast-WAM) and video foundation models (Wan2.2, LongLive-2.0). Its new idea is a staged approach: first performing autoregressive (AR) robot-domain video pretraining (LongLive2.0-Robot) to learn causal temporal factorization, then adapting to a "causal-to-causal" world-action model that preserves this history-to-future structure, combined with an asynchronous real-time deployment system.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses how to scale visual history context for world-action models under real-time control constraints—specifically, how longer visual history can improve robot control performance without introducing prohibitive inference latency that delays action execution.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper uses (1) robot-domain autoregressive video pretraining on ~10,000 hours of robot and egocentric video; (2) causal-to-causal world-action adaptation with inverse dynamics modeling (IDM) where actions condition on both observed history and predicted future latents; (3) streaming VAE encoding, asynchronous execution, NVFP4 quantization, CUDA Graph compilation, shared input quantization, and device-specific kernel tuning for edge deployment on RTX 5090, DGX Spark, and Jetson AGX Thor.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Achieves state-of-the-art performance including 99.5% on LIBERO-Long (up from 94.5%), 78.7% on RoboCasa GR-1 (up from 63.3%, with 19.2s of context), 94.4% average on RoboTwin 2.0, and 34.9% on DOMINO. Real-world results include 90-100% grasping success on a conveyor (3.0-7.5 cm/s) and 95% success on dynamic cup stacking, where both π0.5 and Fast-WAM scored 0%. Latency reaches 107.4 ms per action chunk on RTX 5090 (3.3× speedup over BF16). The model also serves as an executor that amplifies hierarchical planning (54.4% vs. 25.2% with GPT-6 Astra alone on RoboCasa365).

SGF+: Decoupling Gradient Flows for Autoregressive Video Generation

Published: 2026-10-06

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.10429

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on autoregressive video generation using diffusion models, specifically addressing the parameterization of context writing and denoising roles in long-horizon video synthesis.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on Self Forcing (SF) and Self Gradient Forcing (SGF), which respectively addressed train-test mismatch and missing context gradients. The new idea is to decouple context writing and denoising into role-specific parameters to resolve their conflicting gradient updates.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper solves the optimization conflict between context-writing and denoising gradients that occur when these two roles share parameters, which causes partial cancellation of updates and degrades both visual quality and long-horizon consistency.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper introduces SGF+, which assigns independent parameters to a context writer (C_θc) and a denoiser (D_θd) initialized from the same diffusion model, preserves their interaction through causal attention, and jointly optimizes both using the original generation objective without auxiliary losses.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: SGF+ outperforms SF and SGF on most VBench quality and consistency metrics at 5s, 60s, and 240s horizons, supports continuous 24-hour generation from only 5s training rollouts, with only a modest ~8.2% increase in training time and nearly unchanged inference latency.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于使用扩散模型的自回归视频生成，具体涉及长时视频合成中上下文写入和去噪角色的参数化问题。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于 Self Forcing (SF) 和 Self Gradient Forcing (SGF)，分别解决了训练-测试不匹配和缺失上下文梯度的问题。新思路是将上下文写入和去噪解耦为角色专属参数，以解决它们之间冲突的梯度更新。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了当上下文写入和去噪共享参数时，这两种角色的梯度之间存在的优化冲突，该冲突导致更新部分相互抵消，从而降低视觉质量和长时一致性。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了 SGF+，为上下文写入器 (C_θc) 和去噪器 (D_θd) 分配独立参数（从同一扩散模型初始化），通过因果注意力保留其交互，并使用原始生成目标联合优化两者，无需辅助损失。
5. 📊 结果与评估: SGF+ 在 5s、60s 和 240s 时间跨度的多数 VBench 质量和一致性指标上优于 SF 和 SGF，仅通过 5s 训练即可支持连续 24 小时生成，训练时间仅增加约 8.2%，推理延迟几乎不变。

Start with Paper 1 on World-Action Models for robotics—it ties together the video generation themes across all three papers by building on LongLive-style autoregressive video pretraining, and its real-world robotics results (95% success on dynamic cup stacking where baselines score 0%) make it the most broadly impactful. Follow up with Paper 3 (SGF+) if you want to understand the underlying autoregressive video generation technique, as its elegant decoupling of context-writing and denoising parameters could also benefit Paper 1's long-horizon rollout. Paper 2 (GRACE) addresses a complementary efficiency angle via latent compression and DiT acceleration, useful if deployment speed is your priority.
建议首先阅读第一篇关于世界-动作模型 (World-Action Models) 的机器人论文——它基于 LongLive 风格的自回归视频预训练，将三篇论文中的视频生成主题串联起来，且其实世界机器人成果（动态叠杯任务 95% 成功率，而基线为 0%）使其最具广泛影响力。若想深入了解底层的自回归视频生成技术，可以接着读第三篇 SGF+，它通过优雅地解耦上下文写入与去噪参数来提升长程一致性，第一篇也可借鉴此思路。第二篇 GRACE 则从潜在压缩与 DiT 加速的角度解决互补的效率问题，适合在部署速度优先时参考。
