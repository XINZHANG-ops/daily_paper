Paper 1

SWE-Bench ProMax: Benchmarking Agents on Large-Scale Multilingual Code Refactoring

Published: 2026-08-09

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.09802

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on benchmarking AI coding agents on large-scale, multilingual code refactoring tasks across seven programming languages. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on existing repository-level benchmarks like SWE-bench Verified, SWE-bench Pro, and Multi-SWE-bench, the paper introduces the novel idea of a refactoring-focused, expert-curated, multilingual benchmark to address saturation and evaluation quality issues. 3. **❓ Problem:** The paper addresses the saturation of existing benchmarks, flawed test suites (overly narrow or broad tests) affecting nearly 60% of unsolved instances, lack of multilingual coverage, and the underrepresentation of real-world refactoring tasks requiring large-scale cross-file coordination. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The authors employ a three-stage curation pipeline—data collection from GitHub, Docker environment construction, and expert-driven filtering/rewriting—to produce 170 high-quality refactoring instances, then evaluate six frontier models under two agent scaffolds (mini-swe-agent and OpenHands). 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** The best-performing model (GPT-5.2) achieves only 41.2% resolve rate, with open-weight models like GLM-5 reaching 36.5% at roughly one-twentieth the cost; analysis reveals that incomplete cross-file coordination is the dominant failure mode, as agents consistently modify fewer files than the gold patch requires.

Paper 2

Macaron-V1: Towards Open Continual Learning with Self-Improvement and Mixture-of-LoRA

Published: 2026-08-09

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.09819

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents Macaron-V1, an open agent-model family in the domain of artificial intelligence, specifically focused on experiential intelligence, continual learning, and agent systems through a Mixture-of-LoRA (MoL) architecture.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on LoRA (Hu et al., 2022), centralized post-training practices, mixture-of-experts models, and continual learning literature; its new idea is co-designing a frozen base model with composable specialist LoRA adapters orchestrated via a Proxy-mediated routing loop, combined with Model-Harness Co-design and a recursive self-improvement (RSI) cycle to enable post-deployment adaptation and cross-team composition.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the limitation that current agent models are fixed at release and cannot continue learning from real-world experience, while also suffering from cross-task interference when jointly post-trained on heterogeneous tasks like chat, agentic tool use, coding, and generative UI.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes the Mixture-of-LoRA (MoL) architecture (frozen 744B GLM-5.2 base + four LoRA specialists for Chat, Agent, Coding, and GenUI with Proxy-mediated per-turn routing), Model-Harness Co-design (UI4A component-native generative UI harness, REPL-based agent harness with executable composition and validated reuse, and Harness Context Protocol), a three-stage Recursive Self-Improvement loop (Discovery, Expansion, Update) via MindForge, and supporting infrastructure including MinT for adapter lifecycle and LongStraw for long-context RL.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Macaron-V1-Venti achieves 58.3 on Macaron ChatBench, 64.0 on Macaron LivingBench, 87.8 Final Score on UI4A-Bench (leading all baselines), and 87.6 on TerminalBench 2.1 (leading), while trailing on some agent and coding benchmarks like ClawGym and DeepSWE; routing accuracy reaches 99.12% on a 6,448-sample trace, and configuration search over a base-failure set achieves 122/122 cumulative coverage through 69 jobs without any weight update.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文介绍了 Macaron-V1，一个开放式的智能体模型家族，属于人工智能领域，专注于通过混合 LoRA（Mixture-of-LoRA）架构实现经验智能、持续学习和智能体系统。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本文基于 LoRA（Hu et al., 2022）、集中式后训练实践、混合专家模型和持续学习文献；其新思路是通过代理介导的路由循环将冻结的基座模型与可组合的专家 LoRA 适配器进行协同设计，并结合模型-工具架协同设计与递归自我改进（RSI）循环，以实现部署后自适应和跨团队组合能力。
3. ❓ 问题: 本文解决了当前智能体模型在发布后即固定、无法从真实世界经验中持续学习的问题，以及在聊天、智能体工具使用、代码和生成式 UI 等异构任务上联合后训练时出现的跨任务干扰问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 本文提出了混合 LoRA（MoL）架构（冻结的 744B GLM-5.2 基座 + 四个用于聊天、智能体、代码和生成式 UI 的 LoRA 专家，通过代理介导的每轮路由）、模型-工具架协同设计（UI4A 组件原生生成式 UI 工具架、基于 REPL 的智能体工具架支持可执行组合和验证复用，以及工具架上下文协议 HCP）、通过 MindForge 实现的三阶段递归自我改进循环（发现、扩展、更新），以及包括用于适配器生命周期管理的 MinT 和用于长上下文强化学习的 LongStraw 在内的支持基础设施。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Macaron-V1-Venti 在 Macaron ChatBench 上取得 58.3 分，在 Macaron LivingBench 上取得 64.0 分，在 UI4A-Bench 上以 87.8 分的最终得分领先所有基线，在 TerminalBench 2.1 上以 87.6 分领先，但在 ClawGym 和 DeepSWE 等部分智能体和代码基准上落后；路由准确率在 6,448 样本轨迹上达到 99.12%，且在基座失败任务集上通过 69 个任务的配置搜索实现了 122/122 的累计覆盖率，且全程没有任何权重更新。

Paper 3

SmartMage: Dynamic Modality Orchestration for 3D Scene Understanding

Published: 2026-08-09

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05137

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on 3D scene understanding using multimodal large language models (MLLMs), a topic situated at the intersection of computer vision, multimodal learning, and embodied AI, targeting indoor scene reasoning across heterogeneous modalities (RGB, depth, point cloud, BEV, voxel).
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior 3D MLLMs (e.g., Ross3D, Video-3D LLM, GPT4Scene, LLaVA-3D) and MoE-based multimodal modeling that typically rely on fixed modality combinations. The new idea is to decompose multimodal reasoning into two steps: semantic-guided dynamic modality selection followed by modality-aware expert specialization, replacing rigid fusion with query-adaptive orchestration.
3. ❓ Problem: Existing MLLMs treat all modalities as equally useful regardless of the query, causing semantic noise from irrelevant modalities, underutilization of informative ones, wasted computation, and diluted reasoning—so that simply stacking more modalities under fixed fusion can even hurt performance.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes SmartMage, comprising the SMART module (Semantic Prior Estimator + Semantic Similarity Scorer + Modality Quality Evaluator) for adaptively routing relevant modalities per query, and the MAGE module (Modality-aware Expert Speculation + sparse MoE-based LLM with 8 experts/top-2 routing) for modality-consistent expert specialization. It also introduces the ScanFacet diagnostic benchmark across 8 semantic facets.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: SmartMage achieves SOTA on five 3D benchmarks—e.g., 32.6 EM@1 on ScanQA, 66.8 EM@1 on SQA3D, 65.9/59.5 Acc@0.25/0.5 on ScanRefer (+5.1 over Ross3D), and 65.4/60.7 F1 on Multi3DRefer (+6.4 over Ross3D)—while delivering 2.0×–2.6× training speedup and remaining competitive on RGB-only video benchmarks, with the largest gains on material (+27.1 CIDEr) and color (+15.9 CIDEr) understanding.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文聚焦于基于多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的三维场景理解，研究领域涵盖计算机视觉、多模态学习和具身智能，针对室内场景中 RGB、深度、点云、BEV、体素等多种异构模态的联合推理。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本文基于已有三维 MLLM（如 Ross3D、Video-3D LLM、GPT4Scene、LLaVA-3D）以及基于 MoE 的多模态建模方法，这些方法通常依赖固定的模态组合。新的思路是将多模态推理分解为语义引导的动态模态选择和模态感知的专家特化两个步骤，以查询自适应的编排取代僵化的融合方式。
3. ❓ 问题: 现有 MLLM 在处理查询时将所有模态同等对待，导致无关模态引入语义噪声、信息量大的模态被忽视、计算资源浪费以及推理能力被稀释——在固定融合方案下简单堆叠更多模态甚至可能损害三维场景理解的性能。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了 SmartMage，包括 SMART 模块（语义先验估计器 + 语义相似性评分器 + 模态质量评估器）用于按查询自适应路由相关模态，以及 MAGE 模块（模态感知专家推测 + 基于 8 专家/top-2 路由的稀疏 MoE LLM）实现模态一致的专家特化。此外还引入了覆盖 8 个语义类别的诊断基准 ScanFacet。
5. 📊 结果与评估: SmartMage 在五个三维基准上取得 SOTA——例如 ScanQA 上 32.6 EM@1、SQA3D 上 66.8 EM@1、ScanRefer 上 65.9/59.5 Acc（较 Ross3D 提升 +5.1）、Multi3DRefer 上 65.4/60.7 F1（提升 +6.4），同时训练加速达 2.0×–2.6×，在仅 RGB 视频基准上仍具竞争力，其中材质（+27.1 CIDEr）和颜色（+15.9 CIDEr）理解提升最为显著。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with the Macaron-V1 paper (Paper 2) as it presents the most ambitious and broadly relevant contribution—a modular agent architecture whose frozen-base-plus-LoRA-specialist design conceptually mirrors the dynamic expert routing in SmartMage (Paper 3), and whose coding/agent evaluations are directly stress-tested by the multilingual refactoring benchmark in Paper 1. Together, the three papers sketch a coherent picture: Paper 1 diagnoses where current agents fail (cross-file coordination), Paper 2 offers an architectural remedy, and Paper 3 validates a similar modular-routing principle in a different modality.
建议先阅读 Macaron-V1（论文 2），其"冻结基座 + LoRA 专家"的模块化设计与论文 3 SmartMage 的动态专家路由思想异曲同工，而其编码/智能体能力也可直接由论文 1 的多语言重构基准进行压力测试。三篇论文共同勾勒出一幅清晰图景：论文 1 诊断出现有智能体的短板（跨文件协同），论文 2 给出架构层面的解法，论文 3 则在另一模态上验证了类似的模块化路由原则。
