EnvHarness: Awakening Static Worlds for Agent Learning

Published: 2026-08-19

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19880

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper presents EnvHarness, a framework for dynamically customizing static environments used in LLM agent training, situated in the domain of artificial intelligence, specifically LLM agents, reinforcement learning, and environment engineering.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on agent harness designs (Anthropic, OpenAI), automated environment generation methods (GenEnv, VeriEnv, SWE-smith), and self-evolving agent research; its new idea is to treat the environment as a wrappable object through a domain-agnostic interface, reshaping behavior via plug-in components while leaving the underlying environment and its trusted verifier completely untouched.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the rigidity of static, hand-built training environments that fail to provide targeted learning signals for an agent's specific weaknesses and quickly become obsolete as the agent improves, while existing environment generation pipelines are domain-specific and produce unreliable verifiers.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes EnvHarness, a programmable layer with three plug-in components (Stage for initial states, Contract for action/observation transitions, Chain for composing environments), operating through the standard reset/step interface, and EnvRigger, an automated four-stage loop (Observe, Diagnose, Write, Validate) that diagnoses policy weaknesses from execution trajectories and iteratively synthesizes targeted components.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across five benchmarks in four domains, EnvHarness achieves up to a 9.0-point improvement on held-out tasks with 9.8% fewer execution steps, outperforms domain-specific generation baselines (GenEnv, VeriEnv, SWE-smith), provides superior optimization signals for reinforcement learning (up to 6.5 points), generalizes across four LLM backbones, and continues to scale where original and generated environments plateau.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文提出了EnvHarness，一个用于动态定制LLM智能体训练静态环境的框架，属于人工智能领域，具体涉及LLM智能体、强化学习和环境工程。
2. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了手工构建的静态训练环境的僵化问题——这些环境无法针对智能体的特定弱点提供定向学习信号，且随着智能体能力的提升很快失去作用，而现有的环境生成流水线具有领域特定性且生成的验证器不可靠。
3. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了EnvHarness，一个具有三种插件组件（用于初始状态的Stage、用于动作/观察转换的Contract、用于组合环境的Chain）的可编程层，通过标准reset/step接口运行；同时提出EnvRigger，一个自动化的四阶段循环（观察、诊断、写入、验证），从执行轨迹中诊断策略弱点并迭代合成针对性组件。
4. 📊 结果与评估: 在四个领域的五个基准测试中，EnvHarness在保留任务上实现了高达9.0分的提升，执行步骤减少9.8%，优于领域特定的生成基线（GenEnv、VeriEnv、SWE-smith），为强化学习提供了更优的优化信号（最高6.5分），可在四种LLM骨干模型上泛化，并在原始和生成环境趋于平稳时继续扩展。

SWE-bench Science: Can Coding Agents Resolve Engineering Tasks in Science?

Published: 2026-08-19

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19799

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper is situated at the intersection of AI/code generation benchmarking and scientific software engineering, introducing and evaluating a repository-level benchmark for assessing whether coding agents can repair scientific software while preserving scientific validity. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** It builds on prior code/scientific benchmarks such as SWE-bench, SciCode, ScienceAgentBench, and AInsteinBench, and introduces the new idea of a broad-coverage, repository-level scientific benchmark that not only measures success but also systematically analyzes failure mechanisms and the role of scientific auxiliary information. 3. **❓ Problem:** The paper addresses the lack of cross-domain, repository-level evaluation for coding agents on scientific software and the limited insight into why agents fail when repairing scientific code where errors can compromise scientific conclusions. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The authors employ a four-stage Chain-of-Evidence Protocol to construct 119 tasks, organize them into three paradigms (Issue-driven, Expert-exploratory, Engineering-integration), evaluate eight frontier coding-agent configurations, manually audit failures into four recurring mechanisms, and conduct a paired ablation removing scientific auxiliary information. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Even the best agent (Claude-Opus-5) achieves only 47.90% Pass@1 with a 96.64% public score, four recurring failure mechanisms are identified across models, and paired ablation shows scientific information has model-dependent effects—improving DeepSeek-V4-flash's Pass@1 from 16.48% to 23.08% but decreasing GPT-5.6-sol's from 36.26% to 31.87%.

4DAnyone: Create Anyone in 4D from a Casual Monocular Video

Published: 2026-08-19

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.20335

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on 4D human reconstruction and free-viewpoint video generation from casually captured monocular videos, within the domain of computer vision, computer graphics, and generative AI for multi-view video synthesis and 3D/4D Gaussian Splatting.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds upon camera-controlled video diffusion models (CameraCtrl, ReCamMaster, TrajectoryCrafter), multi-view diffusion frameworks (CAT3D, CAT4D), and 4D Gaussian Splatting methods, introducing novel ideas of Reference Context Packing (RCP) and Target Context Routing (TCR) to resolve the bounded-attention-context problem for achieving reconstruction-grade multiview consistency.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the challenge of reconstructing high-fidelity 4D humans from a single uncalibrated monocular video, specifically overcoming the failure of existing camera-controlled video diffusion models to maintain cross-view consistency when scaled to the tens of target views required for 4DGS reconstruction.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper employs 3D-aware skeleton conditioning with depth-buffered rendering, Reference Context Packing (RCP) to compress growing reference views into fixed-length O(1) context, Target Context Routing (TCR) to cyclically regroup target views during high-noise denoising and fix adjacent groups at low-noise denoising, a three-stage curriculum training protocol, and a newly constructed MVGameHuman dataset.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: 4DAnyone outperforms MV-Performer, TrajectoryCrafter, and ReCamMaster across PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS metrics on DNA-Rendering and DyMVHumans benchmarks for both generated video quality and downstream 4DGS reconstruction, achieving e.g. 24.33 PSNR (vs. 21.47 for ReCamMaster) on DNA-Rendering generated video consistency, with robust generalization to in-the-wild videos.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文聚焦于从随意捕获的单目视频重建4D人体，涵盖计算机视觉、计算机图形学以及面向多视角视频合成与三维/四维高斯溅射的生成式AI领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于相机可控视频扩散模型（CameraCtrl、ReCamMaster、TrajectoryCrafter）、多视角扩散框架（CAT3D、CAT4D）及四维高斯溅射方法，提出了参考上下文打包（RCP）与目标上下文路由（TCR）的新颖思路，以解决有界注意力上下文问题，从而实现可重建级别的多视角一致性。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决从单个未标定的单目视频重建高保真4D人体的难题，特别是克服现有相机可控视频扩散模型在扩展到4DGS重建所需的数十个目标视角时无法维持跨视角一致性的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文采用了基于深度缓冲渲染的3D感知骨架条件注入、将不断增长的参考视图压缩为固定长度O(1)上下文的参考上下文打包（RCP）、在高噪声阶段循环重组目标视图并在低噪声阶段固定相邻分组的目标上下文路由（TCR）、三阶段课程训练协议以及新构建的MVGameHuman数据集。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 4DAnyone在DNA-Rendering和DyMVHumans基准上的生成视频质量和下游4DGS重建的PSNR、SSIM和LPIPS指标均优于MV-Performer、TrajectoryCrafter和ReCamMaster，例如在DNA-Rendering生成视频一致性上达到24.33 PSNR（ReCamMaster为21.47），并对野外视频具有鲁棒的泛化能力。
