Sage S2.1 Flash Technical Report

Published: 2026-11-15

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.21072

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This technical report presents Sage S2.1 Flash, a 320-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts foundation model for multimodal scientific reasoning, computer use, and verifiable agentic workflows.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on visual-feedback agents, scientific foundation encoders, tool-centric data synthesis, and reinforcement learning, the paper contributes a unified scientific-input interface, broad verifiable-environment post-training, and outcome-driven projector adaptation.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses how to combine heterogeneous scientific evidence—molecular graphs, biological sequences, spectra, text, and images—with long-horizon tool, coding, GUI, and research actions whose outcomes can be objectively verified.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The model combines a hybrid-attention MoE backbone with scientific encoders and modality-specific projectors, then applies SFT, rejection sampling, and GRPO with execution-, state-, artifact-, and science-specific rewards across tool-use, search, coding, ML-engineering, cybersecurity, workspace, and visual environments.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across 33 benchmarks in seven domains, Sage S2.1 Flash ranks first against the five primary baselines on OfficeQA Pro, MLE-bench Lite, Harvey LAB, and SpreadsheetBench 2, and second on OSWorld-Verified, WebArena-Verified, and DeepResearch Bench II, while trajectory analysis of 48 paired runs reveals stronger constraint preservation, experimental checking, and artifact validation than the baseline.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本技术报告介绍了 Sage S2.1 Flash，这是一个面向多模态科学推理、计算机操作和可验证智能体工作流的 3200 亿参数混合专家基础模型。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在视觉反馈智能体、科学基础编码器、以工具为中心的数据合成和强化学习的基础上，论文提出了统一的科学输入接口、大规模可验证环境后训练流程以及由任务结果驱动的投影器适配方法。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决如何将分子图、生物序列、频谱、文本和图像等异构科学证据，与可通过客观方式验证完成情况的长程工具、编程、图形界面及科研操作相融合的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 模型采用混合注意力混合专家骨干网络、科学编码器和模态专用投影器，并利用基于执行结果、环境状态、交付物及科学指标的监督微调、拒绝采样和 GRPO，在工具使用、搜索、编程、机器学习工程、网络安全、工作空间和视觉环境中进行训练。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在七个领域的 33 个基准上，Sage S2.1 Flash 相较五个主要基线在 OfficeQA Pro、MLE-bench Lite、Harvey LAB 和 SpreadsheetBench 2 上排名第一，在 OSWorld-Verified、WebArena-Verified 和 DeepResearch Bench II 上排名第二，且对 48 组配对轨迹的分析显示其约束保持、实验核查和交付物验证能力优于基线。

InclusionMed: Benchmarking AI for Inclusive Healthcare

Published: 2026-11-15

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.21218

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This technical report introduces InclusionMed, an open benchmarking initiative for evaluating large language models in inclusive healthcare across healthcare roles and AI capabilities.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on MedQA, HealthBench, MedAgentBench, CHI-Bench, HELM/MedHELM, and existing aggregate indexes, the paper proposes the InclusionMed Atlas—a transparent role-by-capability benchmark map—and an InclusionMed Index with role-, capability-, task-, and cost-specific analyses.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses uneven and opaque benchmark coverage that can conceal whose healthcare needs are represented, make overall model rankings misleading, and obscure differences across roles, capabilities, task types, and inference costs.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors collected 388 candidates, applied LLM-assisted curation and human review under six audit criteria, independently reproduced eight shortlisted benchmarks with eight models, retained four public benchmarks plus the private InclusionMed-DxFiles, evaluated 22 model releases with equal 20% benchmark weights, and analyzed task attributes and token-based costs.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: The five retained benchmarks cover only three of nine Atlas cells and produce Index scores from 17.5 to 49.1, while rankings vary by role and capability, top models perform better on patient communication than clinical decision-making, treatment tasks underperform, and similar-scoring models can differ in estimated cost by roughly 24-fold.

Paper 3

AllocFlow: End-to-End Portfolio Trading Action Generation from Raw Market Data

Published: 2026-11-14

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.19937

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: AllocFlow studies end-to-end algorithmic portfolio trading on hourly cryptocurrency perpetual futures, directly generating joint signed target weights from raw OHLCV histories.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on critiques of predict-then-optimize pipelines, financial foundation models such as Kronos, imitation-assisted portfolio learning, and Flow-GRPO, the paper’s novelty is learning a coherent executable portfolio distribution directly rather than first forecasting independent signals.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the sample-inefficient and potentially misaligned conversion of noisy market observations into coordinated long–short positions under shared capital, exposure, and transaction-cost constraints.
4. 🛠️ Methods: A frozen Kronos encoder produces market tokens, a cross-asset DiT-style flow Transformer generates complete continuous portfolios, imitation of causal strategy allocations establishes structured proposals, and Flow-GRPO with a frozen imitation reference refines them using 24-hour group-relative Sharpe rewards and an after-fee execution ledger.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On strict November 2024–December 2025 out-of-sample tests with 5-bps costs, RL raises annualized net return from 27.79% to 51.38%, reduces maximum drawdown from 29.10% to 12.81%, and improves Sharpe/Calmar from 0.954/0.955 to 2.192/4.043, outperforming the listed baselines and teacher, while six additional months in 2026 without retraining achieve a 4.90 Calmar ratio.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文属于金融机器学习与算法交易领域，研究如何从每小时加密货币永续合约的原始OHLCV数据直接生成联合签名的目标资产权重。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文承接对“预测后优化”流程的批评、金融基础模型Kronos、模仿辅助的组合学习及Flow-GRPO等研究，其新意是绕过独立信号预测，直接学习可执行联合投资组合的分布。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决将噪声市场观测转换为协调多空持仓时学习样本低效、预测与配置目标错位，以及共享资本、风险敞口和交易成本约束难以同时满足的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: AllocFlow以冻结的Kronos编码器生成市场标记，以跨资产DiT式流Transformer生成完整连续组合，先通过因果策略模仿形成结构化候选，再以冻结模仿策略为参考、基于24小时组内相对夏普奖励和扣费执行账本进行Flow-GRPO强化学习。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在采用5个基点成本的2024年11月至2025年12月严格样本外测试中，RL将年化净收益率从27.79%提高至51.38%、最大回撤从29.10%降至12.81%、夏普比率/卡玛比率从0.954/0.955提高至2.192/4.043，优于所列基线和教师策略，并在无需重训的2026年前六个月取得4.90的卡玛比率。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read Sage S2.1 Flash first: it is the most ambitious systems paper here, integrating heterogeneous scientific data with verifiable long-horizon tool use and showing strong results across 33 benchmarks. Its outcome-based reinforcement learning and workflow evaluation provide a useful contrast to InclusionMed’s role- and capability-focused healthcare benchmarking and AllocFlow’s end-to-end, after-fee portfolio learning; together, the papers examine how to evaluate and optimize models whose outputs must operate reliably in real workflows.
建议先读 Sage S2.1 Flash：它最具系统性与技术深度，将异构科学数据、可验证的长程工具调用及强化学习后训练整合起来，并在 33 个基准上展示强结果。它与 InclusionMed 的角色/能力导向医疗评测，以及 AllocFlow 的端到端扣费后组合学习形成有趣对照；三篇论文共同关注如何在真实工作流中可靠地评估并优化模型输出。
