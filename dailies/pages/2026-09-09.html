Paper 1

NeoHorse-1: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement via Agentic Post-Training with Routing Harness

Published: 2026-09-07

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.08183

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper presents NeoHorse-1, an agent-native model family focused on recursive self-improvement (RSI) through agentic post-training with a routing harness, situated in the domain of large language model (LLM) training and agentic AI systems.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds on prior work in trajectory-based SFT (FireAct, AgentTuning), LLM routing (FrugalGPT, RouteLLM, Agentic Routing), curriculum learning, and on-policy distillation, and introduces the new idea of treating the routing harness itself as a concrete RSI mechanism that converts execution trajectories, routing signals, and outcome feedback into an evolving training distribution.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the lack of a concrete mechanism for recursive self-improvement—specifically, how an AI system can observe its own capabilities from agentic interactions and convert that evidence into the next round of learning in an automated feedback loop.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors convert routing-harness interactions into user-turn training examples via structural validation and six-dimensional semantic evaluation, organize them into a three-stage routing-guided curriculum for supervised fine-tuning, extend the same progression to routing-guided on-policy distillation with a reverse-KL objective, and close the loop through capability-guided data allocation based on evaluation feedback.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across ten benchmarks, post-training raises the 4B model's macro-average from 58.94 to 64.87 and the 9B model's from 65.60 to 69.04, with NeoHorse-1-4B substantially narrowing the aggregate gap to the 9B base model and routing-harness data outperforming public agent data (Toucan) by +6.26 average points under matched training conditions.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文提出了 NeoHorse-1，一个通过带路由 Harness 的智能体后训练实现递归自我改进的智能体原生模型家族，属于大语言模型训练与智能体 AI 系统领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该工作建立在基于轨迹的监督微调（FireAct、AgentTuning）、LLM 路由（FrugalGPT、RouteLLM、Agentic Routing）、课程学习和在策略蒸馏等先前研究之上，并引入了将路由 Harness 本身视为具体 RSI 机制的新思路，将执行轨迹、路由信号和结果反馈转化为不断演化的训练数据分布。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了递归自我改进（RSI）缺乏具体机制的问题——即 AI 系统如何从智能体交互中观察自身能力，并将这些证据转化为下一轮学习的自动化反馈闭环。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者通过结构验证和六维语义评估将路由 Harness 交互转化为用户轮次训练示例，将其组织成三阶段路由引导的监督微调课程，并将相同进程扩展到采用反向 KL 目标的路由引导在策略蒸馏，最后通过基于评估反馈的能力引导数据分配来闭合反馈循环。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在十个基准测试上，后训练将 4B 模型的宏平均分从 58.94 提升到 64.87，将 9B 模型从 65.60 提升到 69.04，NeoHorse-1-4B 大幅缩小了与 9B 基座模型的总体差距，且在相同训练条件下，路由 Harness 数据比公共智能体数据（Toucan）平均高出 +6.26 分。

Paper 2

AuK Technical Report: An Open-Source Foundational Model for Speech Generation and Editing

Published: 2026-09-07

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.08936

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents AuK, an open-source foundational model for unified speech generation and editing, spanning the domain of speech synthesis, audio processing, and multimodal AI.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The work builds on prior zero-shot TTS (Seed-TTS, CosyVoice, F5-TTS), instruction-controlled synthesis (InstructTTS, Qwen3-TTS), speech editing systems (Step-Audio-EditX, Ming-UniAudio), audio VAEs (DAC, BigVGAN), flow-matching objectives, and flow-based RL methods (Flow-GRPO, Diffusion-DPO). Its new idea is unifying five task families—generation, acoustic editing, paralinguistic editing, content editing, and enhancement/separation—through a single instruction-conditioned interface with a hybrid MMDiT/DiT backbone and a jointly trained speech-audio-music VAE.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the fragmentation of speech AI capabilities across task-specific models (e.g., separate TTS, editing, enhancement systems), the lack of a unified model interpreting free-form natural-language instructions for diverse speech manipulation tasks, and the challenge of balancing broad capability coverage with scalable training and efficient inference.
4. 🛠️ Methods: AuK combines a frozen multimodal LLM (Qwen2.5-Omni) for semantic conditioning with layer-wise hidden state aggregation, a flow-augmented VAE trained on speech/audio/music for acoustic conditioning, and a hybrid FLUX-style Transformer with dual-stream MMDiT blocks and single-stream DiT blocks using rectified flow matching. Training follows a two-stage curriculum (generation warm-up then joint generation–editing), with post-training using flow-based DPO with human feedback for editing and Flow-GRPO for generation, plus consistency initialization and task-routed Decoupled DMD distillation to produce the CFG-free 4-step AuK-Flash variant.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: AuK achieves leading performance on Seed-TTS-Eval (2.65% avg WER, 0.795 SIM), best IFR/CR on MMAE-Speech (48.23%/88.11%), best SpeechEditBench content/prosody scores (91.83%/71.33%), and strong gains on Ming-Freeform-Audio-Edit (Full-ZH WER 3.09%, Full-EN WER 3.96%), while remaining competitive on signal-level tasks (DNS Challenge dWER 2.66%, Libri2Mix WER 9.12%); AuK-Flash retains most capabilities with a 4.5× wall-clock speedup and 4-step CFG-free inference.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文介绍了 AuK，一个用于统一语音生成与编辑的开源基础模型，涵盖语音合成、音频处理和多模态人工智能领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该工作建立在先前的零样本 TTS（Seed-TTS、CosyVoice、F5-TTS）、指令控制合成（InstructTTS、Qwen3-TTS）、语音编辑系统（Step-Audio-EditX、Ming-UniAudio）、音频 VAE（DAC、BigVGAN）、流匹配目标以及基于流的强化学习方法（Flow-GRPO、Diffusion-DPO）之上。其新思路是通过统一的指令条件化接口，将五个任务家族——生成、声学编辑、副语言编辑、内容编辑以及增强/分离——整合在单一模型中，并采用混合 MMDiT/DiT 主干和联合训练的语音-音频-音乐 VAE。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了语音 AI 能力在任务专用模型之间的碎片化问题（如独立的 TTS、编辑、增强系统），缺乏能够理解自由形式自然语言指令以处理多样化语音操作任务的统一模型，以及在广泛任务覆盖、可扩展训练与高效推理之间取得平衡的挑战。
4. 🛠️ 方法: AuK 结合了冻结的多模态 LLM（Qwen2.5-Omni）进行语义条件化（采用逐层隐藏状态聚合）、在语音/音频/音乐上联合训练的概率流增强 VAE 进行声学条件化，以及采用双流 MMDiT 模块和单流 DiT 模块的 FLUX 风格混合 Transformer，并使用整流流匹配。训练采用两阶段课程学习（先生成预热，再联合生成-编辑），后训练使用基于流的人类反馈 DPO 进行编辑优化，使用 Flow-GRPO 进行生成优化，最后通过一致性初始化和任务路由的解耦 DMD 蒸馏，得到无需 CFG 的 4 步推理 AuK-Flash 变体。
5. 📊 结果与评估: AuK 在 Seed-TTS-Eval 上取得领先性能（平均 WER 2.65%，SIM 0.795），MMAE-Speech 上取得最佳 IFR/CR（48.23%/88.11%），SpeechEditBench 上内容/韵律编辑得分最佳（91.83%/71.33%），Ming-Freeform-Audio-Edit 上提升显著（Full-ZH WER 3.09%，Full-EN WER 3.96%），同时在信号级任务上保持竞争力（DNS Challenge dWER 2.66%，Libri2Mix WER 9.12%）；AuK-Flash 在 4.5× 实际加速和 4 步无 CFG 推理的情况下保留了大部分能力。

Paper 3

Omni Interaction Agent Technical Report

Published: 2026-09-07

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.08977

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper presents an end-to-end Omni Interaction Agent model called Gander, situated in the domain of multimodal AI, speech-language interaction, and LLM-based agentic systems, unifying omni perception, realtime full-duplex interaction, and long-horizon agentic task execution.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work in full-duplex speech models (Moshi, GPT-Live, BayLing-Duplex, Seeduplex), omni-modal systems (Qwen Omni, MiniCPM-o 4.5), and voice agent systems (Qwen Audio Agent). Its new idea is a decoupled Brain–Cerebellum collaborative framework with a chunk-level streaming Thinker-Talker architecture, where interactivity is treated as an intrinsic model capability rather than an external orchestration layer, and a training-free plug-and-play back brain enables scalable agentic reasoning.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the limitations of conventional turn-based, text-centric human-AI interaction by tackling two questions: whether robust interactivity must be an intrinsic capability of the model (rather than composed of VAD/ASR pipelines), and whether a single model can simultaneously deliver low-latency realtime interaction and long-horizon agentic reasoning in dynamic, multimodal environments.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes (1) a Cerebellum-Brain collaborative framework where the Cerebellum handles realtime multimodal interaction via a Thinker-Talker architecture and the Brain (e.g., Codex) handles complex reasoning through tool calls; (2) streaming chunk flattening that serializes audio, video, and text into a unified chunk-level autoregressive sequence with control tokens (listen/speak/interrupt); (3) chunk-based perception encoders with a SigLIP-based visual encoder and a downsampled acoustic encoder; (4) a training-free orchestration runtime with lean and coordinator control modes; and (5) large-scale training corpora spanning speech interaction, audio-visual interaction, agentic interaction, and robustness/negative supervision data.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Gander achieves the best turn-taking performance on Full-Duplex-Bench v3 (100% appropriate turn-taking and only 8.0% premature interruptions, outperforming GPT-Realtime and other commercial systems), leads the full-duplex group on SpokenQA (75.60 on Llama Questions, 59.30 on Web Questions), maintains competitive omni understanding (49.62 on WorldSense, 78.53 on Daily-Omni), and shows genuine multimodal fusion with gains of +5.01 and +19.13 points when combining audio and video over the best single modality on the respective benchmarks; however, it slightly trails on task-accuracy metrics (Pass@1 of 0.400) and on open-ended VoiceBench subsets.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文提出了一个名为Gander的端到端全模态交互智能体模型，属于多模态人工智能、语音语言交互和基于大语言模型的智能体系统领域，统一了全模态感知、实时全双工交互和长时程智能体任务执行能力。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于全双工语音模型（Moshi、GPT-Live、BayLing-Duplex、Seeduplex）、全模态系统（Qwen Omni、MiniCPM-o 4.5）和语音智能体系统（Qwen Audio Agent）的研究基础。其新思路是采用解耦的"大脑-小脑"协作框架和基于分块的流式思考器-说话器架构，将交互能力视为模型的内在能力而非外部编排层，并引入免训练、即插即用的后端大脑以实现可扩展的智能体推理。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文针对传统基于轮次、文本为中心的人机交互范式的局限，致力于回答两个核心问题：稳健的交互能力是否必须是模型的内在能力（而非通过VAD/ASR等流水线组合而成），以及单一模型能否在动态多模态环境中同时实现低延迟实时交互和长时程智能体推理。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了（1）"小脑-大脑"协作框架，其中小脑基于思考器-说话器架构处理实时多模态交互，大脑（如Codex）通过工具调用处理复杂推理；（2）流式分块展平机制，将音频、视频和文本序列化为统一的分块级自回归序列，并使用控制令牌（听/说/打断）来决策交互行为；（3）基于分块的感知编码器，采用基于SigLIP的视觉编码器和降采样的声学编码器；（4）具有精简模式（lean）和协调模式（coordinator）的免训练编排运行时；（5）涵盖语音交互、音视频交互、智能体交互以及鲁棒性/负样本监督数据的大规模训练语料库。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Gander在Full-Duplex-Bench v3上取得了最佳的轮次切换表现（100%的适当接话率和仅8.0%的过早打断率，优于GPT-Realtime及其他商业系统），在SpokenQA的全双工组中领先（Llama Questions上75.60，Web Questions上59.30），保持了有竞争力的全模态理解能力（WorldSense上49.62，Daily-Omni上78.53），并表现出真正的多模态融合能力，在两个基准上联合音视频比最佳单模态分别提升+5.01和+19.13分；然而，其任务准确率指标（Pass@1为0.400）和开放式VoiceBench子集上略逊于基线。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with the NeoHorse-1 paper, as it introduces the most conceptually novel contribution—a concrete recursive self-improvement loop driven by a routing harness—that has broad implications for agentic LLM training and ties directly into the agentic themes of Gander. Read Gander next to see how intrinsic agentic capabilities (full-duplex interactivity, Brain–Cerebellum collaboration) are realized in a deployed multimodal system. Save AuK for last unless your focus is specifically speech synthesis, though it shares multimodal-unification themes with Gander.
建议首先阅读 NeoHorse-1 论文，因为它提出了最具概念新颖性的贡献——通过路由机制实现的递归自我改进循环，对智能体式大语言模型训练有广泛影响，并与 Gander 的智能体主题直接相关。其次阅读 Gander，了解全双工交互、脑-小脑协作等内在智能体能力如何在实际多模态系统中实现。除非你专注于语音合成，否则将 AuK 放在最后阅读，不过它与 Gander 共享多模态统一的主题。
