Making MLLM Latents Matter: Factor-Aware Predictive Learning for Cinematic Video Generation

Published: 2026-10-22

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30926

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies multimodal-large-language-model-conditioned video diffusion for cinematic generation, including sparse-to-video generation, factor-aware editing, and multi-shot consistency. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on MLLM–DiT conditioning, flow matching, and predictive representation learning, it proposes Factor-Aware Predictive Learning (FAPL), which explicitly grounds MLLM latents, supervises factor-level interventions, and learns limited-input prediction through a fixed generator. 3. **❓ Problem:** It addresses MLLM-feature bypass and insufficient factor- and observation-level supervision, which prevent generated videos from reliably inferring temporal content and selectively controlling character, scene, action, and camera. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** FAPL uses three stages: latent-only DiT grounding, paired factor-span replacement with frozen MLLM and connector, and mixed text-only/complete-video fine-tuning of the MLLM pathway through a frozen DiT. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** FAPL outperforms the strongest baseline UniVideo-13B, improving Motion Order from 48% to 61%, factor-edit accuracy from 42% to 58%, and subject, scene, and facial consistency from 0.462, 0.427, and 0.451 to 0.516, 0.471, and 0.523, respectively.

DONIC: Generalist Dexterous Bimanual Tracking Control from Human Demonstrations

Published: 2026-10-20

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.29997

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents DONIC, a generalist dexterous bimanual robot-control and reference-tracking method, together with BiHOI, a multi-view human bimanual hand–object interaction dataset.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on reference-conditioned RL and imitation trackers such as DexTrack and Dexplore, the paper introduces cross-source bimanual generalization, an object-centered per-hand reward, Clip-Centered Advantages, and iterative PPO plus behavioral cloning from verified demonstrations.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses the scarcity of tracked, contact-rich bimanual demonstrations and the difficulty of coordinating changing hand roles and contact while learning one policy that generalizes across trajectories and objects.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors reconstruct and retarget multi-view human interactions, train a shared two-hand reference-conditioned policy with PPO and BC, use object-motion and gated contact rewards, and apply CCA to reference-specific advantage offsets.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Without fine-tuning, DONIC achieves 86.79% success on unseen trajectories and 57.32% on unseen objects, outperforming the strongest baseline by 77.36 and 45.73 percentage points, while ablations validate CCA, contact gating, and BiHOI data.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出 DONIC，一种通用的灵巧双臂机器人参考跟踪控制方法，并发布 BiHOI，一个多视角人类双手—物体交互数据集。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在 DexTrack 和 Dexplore 等参考条件强化学习与模仿学习工作的基础上，论文提出跨数据源的双臂泛化、面向物体且按手分工的奖励、片段中心化优势，以及基于验证演示的迭代 PPO 与行为克隆。
