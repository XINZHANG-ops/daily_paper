Paper 1

Learn What's Left, Not What's Mastered: Saturation Aware Advantage Reweighting for Multi-Reward Policy Optimization

Published: 2026-08-16

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.16072

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: Multi-reward reinforcement learning policy optimization for post-training large language model reasoners, spanning mathematical reasoning, adaptive reasoning, and code generation.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Built on GRPO and GDPO, the paper introduces the new idea of saturation-aware advantage reweighting that dynamically allocates optimization effort based on each objective's remaining reward headroom rather than using fixed weights.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses two limitations of scalarized multi-reward policy optimization: loss of reward resolution when distinct profiles collapse to the same scalar value, and fixed weights that ignore objective saturation, causing training to waste gradient budget on already-solved objectives.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes SA-MRPO, which independently normalizes each reward objective and adaptively discounts its contribution by a factor (1−s(k))^γ based on a batch-level saturation ratio, then re-normalizes the aggregate advantage before applying the standard GRPO clipped surrogate objective.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: SA-MRPO improves harder correctness over GDPO in 12 of 15 mathematical reasoning benchmarks (up to 5% on AIME24), all 5 adaptive reasoning benchmarks (+3.8% average, up to 9.2% on AMC23), and 3 of 4 code generation benchmarks (up to 2.3%), while maintaining already-saturated objectives near their original levels.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 大语言模型后训练中多奖励强化学习的策略优化，涵盖数学推理、自适应推理和代码生成领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于GRPO和GDPO，本文提出了基于饱和度感知的优势重加权新思想，根据每个目标的剩余奖励空间动态分配优化力度，而非使用固定权重。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了标量化多奖励策略优化的两个局限：不同奖励剖面坍缩为相同标量值造成的奖励分辨率损失，以及固定权重忽略目标饱和度导致梯度预算浪费在已解决的问题上。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出SA-MRPO，独立归一化每个奖励目标，并根据基于批次级饱和度比率的因子(1−s(k))^γ自适应地折扣其贡献，然后重新归一化聚合优势，再应用于标准GRPO裁剪替代目标。
5. 📊 结果与评估: SA-MRPO在15个数学推理基准中的12个上超过GDPO（AIME24上最高提升5%），在全部5个自适应推理基准上提升（平均+3.8%，AMC23上最高提升9.2%），在4个代码生成基准中的3个上提升（最高2.3%），同时保持已饱和目标接近其原有水平。

Paper 2

StateM: Reaching 95.3% Raw Accuracy, or a \$15 Frontier Run, on Terminal-Bench 2.1 via Harness Scaling

Published: 2026-08-14

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.15089

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on harness scaling for long-horizon AI agents, specifically introducing StateM, an agent-native runtime control layer, in the domain of autonomous CLI-based agent systems and benchmark evaluation.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The work builds on long-horizon planning (Yao et al., 2024), stateful graph-based orchestration (StateFlow, LangGraph), agent aging diagnostics (AgingBench), and automated harness adaptation (Agentic Harness Engineering, Self-Harness, Better Harnesses Smaller Models). Its new idea is formulating "harness scaling" as a complementary capability axis to model scaling and introducing a jointly editable, agent-native state-machine runbook that preserves agent autonomy while enforcing transition contracts.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the problem that long-horizon agents fail not because their underlying models lack capability, but because their execution harness fails to maintain mutable state, reactivate learned lessons, enforce completion checks, and prevent premature termination—causing otherwise capable models to fail complete tasks.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper uses StateM, a YAML-configured state-machine runtime with phase-level states, entry/exit hooks, checked transition protocols (`goto`), versioned runbooks, auditable evidence receipts, failure-driven harness optimization loops, and a hyper-agent that converts execution failures into versioned procedural practices targeting epistemic gaps, procedural-memory gaps, and procedural-compliance gaps.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On Terminal-Bench 2.1, GPT-5.5 xhigh with StateM reaches 92.1% (vs. 83.1% reference) and GPT-5.6 Sol xhigh with a frozen GPT-5.5 runbook achieves 95.28% raw accuracy (424/445 trials); DeepSeek-V4 Flash with adapted profile reaches 88.09% full-suite and 89.09% on the 88-task common core at only ~$15 final-evaluation cost (vs. $574.68 for GPT). On BusinessBench, frozen one-shot held-out gains were modest (+0.55 macro, +1.34 micro), but mechanism-matched families (Budget Approval, Machine Operating) improved by 10.04 points.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于长时程AI智能体的"工具扩展"(harness scaling)，具体介绍了StateM——一种面向智能体的原生运行时控制层，属于自主CLI智能体系统与基准评估领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该工作基于长时程规划（Yao等，2024）、有状态图编排（StateFlow、LangGraph）、智能体老化诊断（AgingBench）以及自动化工具适配（Agentic Harness Engineering、Self-Harness、Better Harnesses Smaller Models）等研究。其新思路是将"工具扩展"形式化为与模型扩展互补的能力维度，并引入一种由智能体与用户共同编辑、基于状态机的智能体原生运行手册，在保留智能体自主性的同时强制执行转换契约。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决的问题是：长时程智能体失败并非因为底层模型能力不足，而是因为执行工具无法维持可变状态、无法重新激活已学经验、无法强制完成检查并防止过早终止——导致本有能力胜任的模型无法完成整体任务。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文采用StateM——一种基于YAML配置的状态机运行时，包含阶段级状态、进入/退出钩子、受检查的转换协议（`goto`）、版本化运行手册、可审计的证据记录、失败驱动的工具优化循环，以及一个将执行失败转化为版本化程序实践的超智能体，分别针对认知缺口、过程记忆缺口和过程合规缺口。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在Terminal-Bench 2.1上，GPT-5.5 xhigh配合StateM达到92.1%（参考值83.1%）；GPT-5.6 Sol xhigh配合冻结的GPT-5.5运行手册达到95.28%原始准确率（424/445次试验）；DeepSeek-V4 Flash配合适配后的配置文件在全套任务中达到88.09%，在88任务共同核心上达到89.09%，最终评估仅耗费约$15（GPT为$574.68）。在BusinessBench上，冻结一次性留出测试增益有限（宏观+0.55，微观+1.34），但机制匹配的任务族（预算审批、机器操作）提升了10.04个百分点。

Paper 3

ASI-Bench: At the Dawn of Artificial Superintelligence

Published: 2026-08-17

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.17271

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper presents ASI-Bench, a benchmark for evaluating AI systems' capabilities in autonomous scientific research and innovative exploration, situated at the intersection of artificial intelligence benchmarking, AI for science, and research toward artificial superintelligence (ASI).
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The benchmark builds upon prior work such as Humanity's Last Exam, Terminal-Bench, ScienceAgentBench, PaperBench, MLE-Bench, RE-Bench, DiscoveryBench, and SciCode. Its novel contribution is the B1–B4 guidance gradient that progressively withdraws human methodological guidance within the same research project to jointly evaluate cross-domain generality, method autonomy, and end-to-end execution.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the lack of a benchmark that measures how far current AI systems can independently conduct end-to-end, project-level scientific research when human methodological guidance is progressively reduced, rather than merely testing knowledge recall or execution of predefined procedures.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors constructed 60 project-level research tasks across 11 scientific domains through over 31,000 human hours, with multi-stage validation including expert review, AI-assisted auditing, and sandbox execution. Four prompt variants (B1: full method; B2: method only; B3: no method; B4: B3 plus distractors) are applied to 18 Agent×Model configurations for evaluation.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across 18 state-of-the-art Agent×Model configurations, the average score dropped sharply from 50.91 under full guidance (B1) to 29.10 with only method specified (B2) and 26.62 when agents had to determine methods independently (B3), with the best system (Codex with GPT-5.6 ultra) reaching only 51.60 on B3, revealing that current AI systems remain heavily dependent on human methodological guidance and that the primary bottleneck is method operationalization rather than method selection.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文提出了 ASI-Bench，一个用于评估 AI 系统在自主科学研究和创新探索方面能力的基准测试，涵盖人工智能基准测试、AI for Science 以及通往通用人工智能（ASI）路径的交叉领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该基准测试建立在 Humanity's Last Exam、Terminal-Bench、ScienceAgentBench、PaperBench、MLE-Bench、RE-Bench、DiscoveryBench 和 SciCode 等已有研究的基础上。其创新之处在于设计了 B1–B4 的指导梯度，在同一研究项目中逐步撤除人为方法指导，从而联合评估跨领域通用性、方法自主性和端到端执行能力。
3. ❓ 问题: 该论文旨在解决当前缺少一个基准测试来衡量 AI 系统在人为方法指导逐步减少的情况下能够独立进行端到端项目级科学研究的问题，而不是仅仅测试知识回忆或执行预定流程的能力。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者通过超过 31,000 人工小时构建了 60 个跨 11 个科学领域的项目级研究任务，并经过多阶段验证，包括专家评审、AI 辅助审计和沙箱执行。四个提示变体（B1：完整方法；B2：仅方法；B3：无方法；B4：B3 加干扰信息）被应用于 18 个 Agent×Model 配置进行评估。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在 18 个最先进的 Agent×Model 配置中，平均分数从完整指导下的 50.91（B1）急剧下降到仅指定方法时的 29.10（B2）以及必须自主确定方法时的 26.62（B3），最佳系统（Codex 与 GPT-5.6 ultra）在 B3 上仅达到 51.60，表明当前 AI 系统仍然严重依赖人为方法指导，主要瓶颈在于方法的可操作性而非方法的选择。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with ASI-Bench (Paper 3) — its B1–B4 guidance gradient provides the sharpest lens on where current AI systems actually break down, revealing that method operationalization, not method selection, is the real bottleneck. Then read StateM (Paper 2), which offers a concrete infrastructure response to exactly the kind of long-horizon failures ASI-Bench exposes, showing how harness-level state management can lift agent accuracy by 9–12 points. SA-MRPO (Paper 1) is the most self-contained and can be read independently if your focus is on training-time optimization rather than agent evaluation.
建议先读ASI-Bench（论文3）——其B1–B4引导梯度设计提供了最清晰的视角，揭示当前AI系统的真正瓶颈是方法落地执行而非方法选择。接着读StateM（论文2），它针对ASI-Bench所暴露的长程失败提出了具体的基础设施级解决方案，通过状态机运行时将代理准确率提升9–12个百分点。SA-MRPO（论文1）较为独立，若你更关注训练阶段优化而非代理评估，可单独阅读。
