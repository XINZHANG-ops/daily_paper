AREX: Towards a Recursively Self-Improving Agent for Deep Research

Published: 2026-07-22

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.21461

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on building LLM-based deep research agents that can recursively self-improve to solve multi-constraint information-seeking tasks in AI and natural language processing.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on tool-augmented deep research, verification-based reasoning, and long-horizon memory management, while introducing the novel concept of "discovery–verification asymmetry" that drives a recursive framework converting partially verified answers into better-targeted research problems.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the challenge that current deep research agents merely search longer without systematic progress, failing to identify which constraints remain unresolved and how to formulate targeted follow-up research for multi-constraint problems.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes AREX, which combines an inner research loop with an outer self-improvement loop, a learned autonomous context-update tool, progressive multi-stage agentic mid-training, key-step focused supervision, and step-aware reinforcement learning with hierarchical normalization.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: AREX-Base achieves 82.5 on BrowseComp, 85.4 on GAIA, 71.0 on xbench-2510, 89.9 on DeepSearchQA, 82.0 on WideSearch-en, and 52.4 on HLE, substantially outperforming comparable-scale baselines and remaining competitive with much larger models.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文聚焦于构建基于大语言模型的深度研究智能体，通过递归自我改进来解决多约束信息检索任务，属于人工智能与自然语言处理领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该论文基于工具增强的深度研究、基于验证的推理以及长程记忆管理，同时引入了"发现-验证不对称性"这一新颖概念，以此驱动一个将部分验证答案转化为更有针对性研究问题的递归框架。
3. ❓ 问题: 该论文解决了当前深度研究智能体仅延长搜索时间而缺乏系统性进展的问题，无法识别哪些约束仍未解决，也难以制定有针对性的后续研究方向来处理多约束问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该论文提出了 AREX，结合了内部研究循环与外部自我改进循环、一个可学习的自主上下文更新工具、渐进式多阶段智能体中训练、关键步骤聚焦监督以及采用分层归一化的步骤感知强化学习。
5. 📊 结果与评估: AREX-Base 在 BrowseComp 上取得 82.5，GAIA 上取得 85.4，xbench-2510 上取得 71.0，DeepSearchQA 上取得 89.9，WideSearch-en 上取得 82.0，HLE 上取得 52.4，大幅超越同规模基线模型并与参数量大得多的模型保持竞争力。

K12-KGraph: A Curriculum-Aligned Knowledge Graph for Benchmarking and Training Educational LLMs

Published: 2026-07-22

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.09635

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on educational AI for K-12 education, specifically constructing a curriculum-aligned knowledge graph from Chinese textbooks to benchmark and train large language models (LLMs) and vision-language models (VLMs). 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on existing K-12 benchmarks (C-Eval, CMMLU, GaokaoBench, EduEval) that only test factual recall, the paper introduces the novel concept of "curriculum cognition" — the structured understanding of prerequisite chains, concept taxonomies, experiment-concept links, and pedagogical sequencing. 3. **❓ Problem:** Current K-12 educational benchmarks only measure whether a model can answer exam questions (factual recall), but fail to assess the structural and visual understanding of curriculum organization, leaving a critical gap in evaluating and training truly capable educational AI. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The paper extracts a heterogeneous knowledge graph (K12-KGraph) from official People's Education Press textbooks using OCR parsing, LLM-based schema-guided extraction, hierarchical merging, and DAG validation, then derives a 23,640-question benchmark (K12-Bench) and a 7,335-sample fine-tuning corpus (K12-Train) with both textual and multimodal supervision. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Experiments show that even Gemini-3-Flash achieves only 57% exact match on K12-Bench while Gemma-4-31B-IT reaches only 46%, yet K12-Train consistently outperforms eight mainstream SFT corpora on GaokaoBench and EduEval, and K12-Train-Full achieves the best overall performance on three multimodal benchmarks (Gaokao-MM, MDK12-medium, K12Vista), demonstrating both the curriculum-cognition gap and the effectiveness of structurally grounded training data.

Paper 3

Visual Contrastive Self-Distillation

Published: 2026-07-22

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.21556

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) for vision-language models, proposing a method called VCSD that constructs target asymmetry purely from visual input conditioning.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on on-policy distillation (OPD) and existing OPSD methods that rely on privileged answers or visual evidence for teacher-student asymmetry, and introduces the novel idea of using the contrast between original-image and content-erased teacher predictions to create this asymmetry without auxiliary supervision.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the problem that existing OPSD methods require auxiliary information such as privileged answers or visual evidence signals to ensure the self-teacher provides a stronger learning signal than the student, asking whether this asymmetry can be constructed purely from input conditioning.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes VCSD, which uses an EMA teacher to produce two next-token distributions under the same prompt and prefix (one conditioned on the original image and one on a content-erased black control), computes their token-wise log-probability difference to identify visually-supported candidates, sharpens the original-image distribution within a plausibility support, and distills the resulting full-distribution target into the student via forward KL along the on-policy trajectory.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: VCSD consistently outperforms both base models and matched OPSD baselines across Qwen3-VL and Qwen3.5 models from 2B to 9B on seven vision-language benchmarks, improving Qwen3-VL aggregate scores by 1.86% to 4.77% and Qwen3.5 by 2.9% to 4.3%, while requiring no external teacher, privileged answers, visual evidence signals, reasoning traces, or additional inference-time cost.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于视觉语言模型的在线自蒸馏（OPSD），提出了一种名为VCSD的方法，通过纯视觉输入条件构建目标不对称性。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于在线蒸馏（OPD）和现有的依赖特权答案或视觉证据来构建师生不对称的OPSD方法，引入了利用原始图像与内容擦除教师预测之间的对比来构建不对称性而无需辅助监督的新思想。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了现有OPSD方法需要特权答案或视觉证据信号等辅助信息以确保自教师提供比学生更强学习信号的问题，探讨是否能纯粹通过输入条件构建这种不对称性。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了VCSD方法，使用EMA教师在相同提示和前缀下生成两个下一词元分布（一个以原始图像为条件，另一个以内容擦除的黑色控制图像为条件），计算其逐词元对数概率差异以识别受视觉支持的候选词，在合理性支持范围内锐化原始图像分布，并通过在线轨迹上的前向KL将得到的全分布目标蒸馏给学生。
5. 📊 结果与评估: VCSD在Qwen3-VL和Qwen3.5模型（2B至9B）的七个视觉语言基准测试中持续优于基础模型和匹配的OPSD基线，将Qwen3-VL的聚合分数提高1.86%至4.77%，将Qwen3.5提高2.9%至4.3%，且不需要外部教师、特权答案、视觉证据信号、推理痕迹或额外的推理时间成本。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with the AREX paper (Paper 1), as its novel 'discovery–verification asymmetry' framework and recursive self-improvement design offer a conceptually rigorous blueprint for building more capable LLM agents.
