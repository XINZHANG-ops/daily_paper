Paper 1

SenseNova-U1.5: Towards Native Unified Visual Intelligence

Published: 2026-09-09

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11929

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper introduces SenseNova-U1.5, an 8B native unified multimodal model for visual understanding, reasoning, and generation, situated in the domain of computer vision and multimodal artificial intelligence.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Built upon the SenseNova-U1 and NEO-unify encoder-free/VAE-free architecture, the paper proposes two new advances: spatially coherent patch reconstruction via Pixel Shuffle and convolutions, and a "specialize-then-unify" post-training strategy combining capability-specific RL experts with multi-expert on-policy distillation.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the fundamental architectural divide in multimodal AI where perception (vision encoders) and generation (VAEs) operate in distinct representational spaces, limiting seamless coordination of understanding, reasoning, and creation within a unified framework.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors employ an 8B-MoT native architecture with compact 32×32 patch tokenization, a spatially coupled decoder with Pixel Shuffle upsampling, asymmetric attention routing between understanding/generation branches, a unified objective combining autoregressive, flow-matching, and LPIPS perceptual losses, plus four specialized RL experts (aesthetics, OCR, infographic, editing) consolidated through on-policy velocity-field distillation.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: SenseNova-U1.5 achieves leading open-source performance on GenEval (0.92), CVTG-2K (0.948), LongText-Bench (0.988 EN/0.989 ZH), BizGenEval, and VBVR-Pro-Bench (68.2 overall), with substantial gains in bilingual text rendering, complex composition, multi-reference editing, and interleaved generation while preserving visual understanding capabilities.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文介绍了 SenseNova-U1.5，一个用于视觉理解、推理和生成的 8B 原生统一多模态模型，属于计算机视觉和多模态人工智能领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于 SenseNova-U1 和 NEO-unify 无编码器/无 VAE 架构，论文提出两项新进展：通过 Pixel Shuffle 和卷积实现空间一致的块重建，以及结合特定能力 RL 专家与多专家在线策略蒸馏的"先专精后统一"后训练策略。
3. ❓ 问题: 该论文解决了多模态 AI 中感知（视觉编码器）和生成（VAE）运行在不同表征空间的基本架构分歧，限制了理解、推理和创作在统一框架内的无缝协调。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者采用了 8B-MoT 原生架构，具有紧凑的 32×32 块标记化、带有 Pixel Shuffle 上采样的空间耦合解码器、理解/生成分支之间的非对称注意力路由，以及结合自回归、流匹配和 LPIPS 感知损失的综合目标，通过在线策略速度场蒸馏整合四个专门的 RL 专家（美学、OCR、信息图、编辑）。
5. 📊 结果与评估: SenseNova-U1.5 在 GenEval（0.92）、CVTG-2K（0.948）、LongText-Bench（0.988 EN/0.989 ZH）、BizGenEval 和 VBVR-Pro-Bench（总体 68.2）等基准上取得领先的开源性能，在双语文本渲染、复杂组合、多参考编辑和交错生成方面有显著提升，同时保持了视觉理解能力。

NCP-ArchPreview Technical Report: Moving towards Latent Space Language Models through Next Concept Prediction

Published: 2026-09-08

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.10715

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents NCP-ArchPreview, a latent-space language model that integrates Next Concept Prediction (NCP) with standard Next Token Prediction (NTP), situated in the AI/NLP domain of large language model architecture and pretraining.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Built upon OLMo-3-7B backbone and ConceptLM's discrete concept prediction framework, the paper proposes scaling latent-space language modeling to 8.9B parameters with 5.73T training tokens via jointly-trained NTP and NCP objectives over a product-quantized concept vocabulary.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the lack of explicit concept-level supervision in standard autoregressive pretraining, aiming to make latent semantic abstractions first-class prediction targets rather than indirect byproducts of token-level modeling.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The method employs a three-module architecture (16-layer Token Encoder, 8-layer Concept Module, 16-layer Token Decoder), product quantization with 32 codebooks of 128 entries for concept vocabulary construction, hierarchical residual connections (IRC and CRC), joint NTP+NCP+VQ training objectives, and Moonlight Muon optimizer.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: NCP-ArchPreview achieves OLMo-3-7B's final loss using only 51.3% of training tokens (1.95× faster convergence), outperforms it by 2.45 points on downstream macro-average with +5.99 on GSM8K, demonstrates 1.74× compute efficiency in scaling laws, supports lightweight domain adaptation with only 17M VQ parameters, and improves DFlash2 drafter mean accepted length by 4.17%.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出了 NCP-ArchPreview，一个将"下一概念预测"（NCP）与标准"下一词元预测"（NTP）相结合的潜在空间语言模型，属于人工智能/自然语言处理领域中大语言模型架构与预训练方向。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于 OLMo-3-7B 骨干网络和 ConceptLM 的离散概念预测框架，本文提出通过在乘积量化的概念词汇表上联合训练 NTP 和 NCP 目标，将潜在空间语言建模扩展到 8.9B 参数和 5.73T 训练词元规模。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了标准自回归预训练中缺乏显式概念级监督的问题，旨在将潜在的语义抽象作为一等预测目标，而非仅作为词元级建模的间接副产品。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该方法采用三模块架构（16 层词元编码器、8 层概念模块、16 层词元解码器），使用 32 个码本各 128 个条目的乘积量化构建概念词汇表，引入分层残差连接（IRC 和 CRC），联合优化 NTP+NCP+VQ 训练目标，并采用 Moonlight Muon 优化器。
5. 📊 结果与评估: NCP-ArchPreview 仅用 51.3% 的训练词元（1.95× 更快收敛）即达到 OLMo-3-7B 的最终损失，在下游宏平均上超越其 2.45 分（GSM8K 提升 5.99 分），缩放定律显示 1.74× 计算效率提升，支持仅 17M VQ 参数的轻量领域自适应，并将 DFlash2 推测解码草稿器的平均接受长度提升 4.17%。

Paper 3

T1: Terminal Agent Reinforcement Learning for Long-Horizon Tasks

Published: 2026-09-09

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11042

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on reinforcement learning for terminal/command-line agents, specifically training large Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) language models to execute long-horizon tasks in real shell environments for software engineering and system administration.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The work builds on Terminal-Bench benchmarks, recursive task synthesis (RST), prior work on training-inference mismatch in RL (IcePop, KPop, GSPO, SAT), and rollout routing replay. The new ideas include combining TITO (token-in-token-out) with R3 to stabilize MoE RL, a dense verification reward design using absolute passing assertion counts, and a complete stabilization stack for sparse agentic RL at frontier scale.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses two core problems: (1) training-inference consistency for sparse MoE models where token re-rendering and router discontinuities corrupt importance ratios during long-horizon multi-turn rollouts, and (2) sparse binary rewards yielding insufficient learning signal when frontier models rarely complete tasks fully.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper uses PPO with a warm-started full-size critic trained at 30× the actor learning rate, TITO for token fidelity (preserving sampled token identifiers across turn boundaries), R3 for routing fidelity (replaying expert selections from inference in the training pass), a dense test-count reward (passing assertions / 20), recursive task synthesis with quality filtering, fully out-of-distribution training corpus, and asynchronous infrastructure with oversampling and context parallelism for hour-scale steps.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: T1 raises Terminal-Bench 2.1 from 43.8% (base) to 64.0% resolved, a 14.6 percentage point gain from RL alone; achieves 27.9% on Long-Horizon Terminal Bench (matching Gemini-3.1-Pro and surpassing GPT-5.4) and 38.0% on Terminal-Bench Hard, outperforming GPT-5.4, DeepSeek-V4-Flash, and Claude Opus 4.6 with only 10B active parameters, while TITO+R3 reduce training-inference log-probability gap from 0.021 to 0.013 with zero drift in the loss region.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文聚焦于面向终端/命令行智能体的强化学习，具体是训练大规模混合专家（MoE）语言模型在真实 shell 环境中执行长时序软件工程和系统管理任务。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本工作基于 Terminal-Bench 基准、递归任务合成（RST）、以及先前关于强化学习中训练-推理不一致的研究（IcePop、KPop、GSPO、SAT）和路由回放技术。新思路包括将 TITO（token-in-token-out）与 R3 相结合以稳定 MoE 强化学习、基于绝对通过断言数量的密集验证奖励设计，以及面向前沿规模稀疏智能体强化学习的完整稳定化技术栈。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决两个核心问题：（1）稀疏 MoE 模型在长时序多轮 rollout 中的训练-推理一致性问题，其中令牌重新渲染和路由器的不连续性会破坏重要性比率；（2）当前沿模型很少能完全完成任务时，稀疏的二值奖励无法提供足够的学习信号。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文使用 PPO 算法配合预热启动的全尺寸评论家（其学习率为 actor 的 30 倍）、用于令牌一致性的 TITO（在回合边界保留采样令牌标识符）、用于路由一致性的 R3（在训练过程中回放推理阶段的专家选择）、密集测试计数奖励（通过断言数 / 20）、带质量过滤的递归任务合成、完全分布外训练语料，以及支持小时级训练步的异步基础设施（含过采样和上下文并行）。
5. 📊 结果与评估: T1 将 Terminal-Bench 2.1 的解决率从 43.8%（基础模型）提升至 64.0%，仅强化学习阶段就带来 14.6 个百分点的提升；在长时序终端基准上达到 27.9%（与 Gemini-3.1-Pro 持平并超越 GPT-5.4），在 Terminal-Bench Hard 上达到 38.0%，以仅 100 亿激活参数超越 GPT-5.4、DeepSeek-V4-Flash 和 Claude Opus 4.6；同时 TITO+R3 将训练-推理对数概率差距从 0.021 降至 0.013，并在损失区域内实现零漂移。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with the T1 paper on terminal-agent RL—it delivers the most striking practical result (matching Gemini-3.1-Pro with only 10B active parameters) and the TITO+R3 recipe for training-inference consistency is broadly reusable. NCP-ArchPreview is the natural second read, as both it and T1 tackle scaling efficiency from complementary angles: NCP halves training tokens via concept-level prediction, while T1 stabilizes long-horizon sparse-MoE RL. SenseNova-U1.5 is a useful third read, sharing the unified-architecture theme with NCP (multimodal vs. concept-token unification) but with more incremental novelty.
建议先读 T1（终端智能体 RL）这篇——它在仅 10B 激活参数下就追平 Gemini-3.1-Pro，实战结果最亮眼，且 TITO+R3 的训练-推理一致性方案可复用性极强。其次推荐 NCP-ArchPreview，它与 T1 从不同角度切入训练效率：NCP 通过概念级预测把训练 token 砍掉近一半，T1 则稳定长时程稀疏 MoE 的 RL 训练。SenseNova-U1.5 适合放在第三篇读，它与 NCP 都聚焦“统一架构”主题（多模态统一 vs. 概念-词元统一），但创新性相对渐进。
