StreamVibe: Proactive Video Agents for Evolving User Intent

Published: 2026-10-25

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34662

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies proactive, intent-aware video-language agents for continuous streaming interactions in physical and digital environments. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on proactive video QA, streaming VLMs, reinforcement learning, and agent-memory research, it introduces a unified explicit–implicit–dynamic intent benchmark, an evidence-grounded response policy, and persistent intent and task-state tracking. 3. **❓ Problem:** It addresses how agents can infer unspoken needs, track changing task states, update evolving intents, and select appropriate response timing and content without premature, missed, outdated, or redundant interventions. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The authors construct StreamVibe-Bench with 4.1K evaluation and 85K training samples, train StreamVibe-VL using adaptive streaming SFT and localized trajectory reinforcement learning, and implement StreamVibe-Harness with independent intent lifecycles and hierarchical memory. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** StreamVibe-VL improves reported response timing and answer quality by 10.7% over baselines while preserving general video understanding, and StreamVibe-Harness yields a further reported 12.3% proactive-interaction gain, particularly for dynamic intents and sustained trajectories.

Published: 2026-10-24

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34467
