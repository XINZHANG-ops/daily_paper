Paper 1

Repo-To-Skill: Distilling GitHub Repositories Into AI4AI Skills

Published: 2026-09-01

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02749

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper addresses autonomous machine learning research agents, focusing on the domain of AI4AI (AI for AI research), specifically the missing "operational knowledge" layer that distinguishes knowing a method from making it work.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work in autonomous ML research agents (e.g., AI Scientist, Agent Laboratory), agent skills specifications (Anthropic), and experience-based skill acquisition methods (Voyager, ExpeL, Agent Workflow Memory). Its new idea is to identify operational knowledge as a distinct missing layer beyond the model and harness, and to distill it into verified, reusable "skills" from declarative sources like GitHub repositories and papers through a four-stage scope-ground-construct-verify process.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper solves the problem that autonomous research agents lack domain-specific operational know-how—they know methods exist but not which packages implement them, how APIs expect data layouts, what configurations work, or how to recover from common pitfalls—forcing them into costly trial-and-error exploration that wastes budget and is not reused across tasks.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes DisCo, a skill-powered research agent operating in two modes: a creator mode that distills skills through task-agnostic distillation (from 1,000 ML repositories into the AREX-Skill Library with 5,000+ skills, 20 areas, 178 capability families) and task-oriented distillation (from papers or task interfaces), and a researcher mode that loads only relevant skills via progressive disclosure. Each skill uses a three-layer structure (SKILL.md knowledge interface, references/ knowledge substrate, scripts/ execution interface) and is organized into skill graphs with routing, dependency, and composition links.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Under a fixed GPT-5.5 Codex harness with matched budgets, the skill-equipped agent achieved 134.3% higher scores on MLE-bench (31.11%→72.89%), 34.4% higher on PaperBench (29.45%→39.59%), 9.2% higher on FrontierCS (70.63→77.14), and 14.0% higher on PassNet (AS Score 1.343→1.531), with gains being largest on harder tasks and on tasks where the no-skill baseline scored lowest.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于自主机器学习研究智能体，涉及 AI4AI（AI 助力 AI 研究）领域，具体研究模型与编排框架之外缺失的"操作知识"层——即区分"了解方法"与"使方法真正奏效"的实操知识。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于自主 ML 研究智能体（如 AI Scientist、Agent Laboratory）、智能体技能规范（Anthropic）以及基于经验的技能获取方法（Voyager、ExpeL、Agent Workflow Memory）等已有研究。其新思路在于首次将"操作知识"明确定义为模型与编排框架之外的独立缺失层，并提出通过"范围界定—证据收集—技能构建—验证"四阶段流程，将 GitHub 仓库和论文等陈述性来源提炼为经过验证的可复用技能。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了自主研究智能体缺乏领域特定操作知识的问题——它们知道方法存在，但不了解哪个软件包实现了该方法、API 期望怎样的数据布局、哪些配置有效、常见陷阱如何恢复——导致智能体只能通过代价高昂的试错探索来弥补，且这些经验无法跨任务复用。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了 DisCo 框架，包含两种模式：创建者模式通过任务无关蒸馏（从 1,000 个 ML 仓库构建包含 5,000+ 技能、20 个领域、178 个能力族的 AREX-Skill 库）和任务导向蒸馏（从论文或任务接口生成技能）来构建技能；研究者模式通过渐进式披露仅加载相关技能。每个技能采用三层结构（SKILL.md 知识接口、references/ 知识基底、scripts/ 执行接口），并被组织为包含路由、依赖和组合链接的技能图。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在固定的 GPT-5.5 Codex 框架和匹配预算下，配备技能的智能体在 MLE-bench 上得分提升 134.3%（31.11%→72.89%），PaperBench 提升 34.4%（29.45%→39.59%），FrontierCS 提升 9.2%（70.63→77.14），PassNet 提升 14.0%（AS Score 1.343→1.531），且增益在困难任务和原本无技能表现较差的任务上最为显著。

SolarWM: Open Data and Scalable Training for Long-Horizon Video World Models

Published: 2026-09-01

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02886

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on interactive video world models, specifically addressing the challenges of long-horizon, camera-controllable video generation across heterogeneous data sources and video generation backbones, in the domain of computer vision and generative AI.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds upon prior work on interactive video world models (e.g., Genie, DIAMOND, GameNGen, Yume, Matrix-Game series) and causal diffusion distillation methods (e.g., Diffusion Forcing, Causal Forcing, DMD). Its new idea is introducing a fully open and unified foundation that couples a reconfigurable multi-source data engine with a backbone-native adaptation framework, enabling a single three-stage recipe (bidirectional adaptation, teacher-forced AnyFlow AR initialization, and DMD) to be applied across multiple heterogeneous video backbones.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the coupling problem between heterogeneous data sources (differing in temporal scale, camera geometry, visual quality, motion, and captioning styles) and diverse video generation backbones (differing in latent representations, architectures, and conditioning mechanisms), which causes inconsistent supervision and limits reproducibility and systematic comparison across models for interactive long-horizon video world modeling.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper uses (1) a reconfigurable multi-source data engine that converts 1.43M clips from 10 datasets into a unified, frame-aligned contract with metric camera geometry, dense captions, quality metadata, and provenance; (2) a backbone-native adaptation framework using fused-PRoPE for camera conditioning that preserves native representations of backbones (Wan2.2, LTX-2.5, MiniMax-H3); and (3) a unified three-stage training recipe combining bidirectional adaptation, teacher-forced AnyFlow autoregressive initialization, and distribution matching distillation (DMD) for causal few-step generation.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: The experiments demonstrate that four 5B–33B models (SolarWM-wan2.2-5B/14B, SolarWM-ltx-2.5-22B, SolarWM-minimax-h3-33B) achieve state-of-the-art performance and, despite being trained on only 5-second sequences, support real-time interactive rollouts spanning from minutes to hours with sustained visual coherence and camera controllability across in-domain, OOD, and long-horizon settings, while the simple training recipe achieves this without specialized ODE or consistency-distillation initialization.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于交互式视频世界模型，具体针对跨异构数据源和视频生成骨干网络的长时间跨度、相机可控视频生成的挑战，属于计算机视觉和生成式人工智能领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文建立在先前关于交互式视频世界模型的研究（如 Genie、DIAMOND、GameNGen、Yume、Matrix-Game 系列）以及因果扩散蒸馏方法（如 Diffusion Forcing、Causal Forcing、DMD）之上。其新思路在于引入一个完全开放且统一的框架，将可重构的多源数据引擎与骨干网络原生适配框架相结合，使得单一的三阶段训练方案（双向适配、Teacher-Forced AnyFlow 自回归初始化和 DMD）能够应用于多个异构视频骨干网络。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了异构数据源（时间尺度、相机几何、视觉质量、运动和字幕风格各异）与多样化视频生成骨干网络（潜在表示、架构和条件机制各异）之间的耦合问题，该问题会导致监督信号不一致，并限制了交互式长时间跨度视频世界建模中模型的复现性和系统性比较。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文采用（1）可重构多源数据引擎，将来自 10 个数据集的 143 万个片段转换为具有度量相机几何、密集字幕、质量元数据和来源信息的统一帧对齐契约；（2）骨干网络原生适配框架，使用融合 PRoPE 进行相机条件控制，保留 Wan2.2、LTX-2.5、MiniMax-H3 等骨干网络的原生表示；（3）统一的三阶段训练方案，结合双向适配、Teacher-Forced AnyFlow 自回归初始化和分布匹配蒸馏（DMD），实现因果少步生成。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 实验表明，四个 5B–33B 模型（SolarWM-wan2.2-5B/14B、SolarWM-ltx-2.5-22B、SolarWM-minimax-h3-33B）达到了最先进的性能，并且尽管仅在 5 秒序列上训练，仍能支持从分钟级到小时级的实时交互式滚动生成，在域内、域外和长时间跨度设置下保持持续的视觉一致性和相机可控性，同时该简单训练方案无需专门的 ODE 或一致性蒸馏初始化即可实现这一目标。

EarlyEval: Cheaper Agent Evaluation via Early Outcome Prediction

Published: 2026-09-01

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.02783

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on LLM agent evaluation efficiency in the field of artificial intelligence and natural language processing, specifically proposing a method to reduce the computational and financial costs of evaluating LLM agents on agentic benchmarks.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work in benchmark distillation (e.g., Anchor Points, tinyBenchmarks) which reduces the number of evaluation tasks; its new idea introduces "early outcome prediction," a complementary efficiency axis that cuts costs within each task by halting agent runs once the final outcome becomes predictable from intermediate behavior.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the prohibitively high cost of evaluating LLM agents (hundreds to thousands of dollars per benchmark pass) and the limitation that existing benchmark distillation methods leave per-task execution costs untouched, slowing down iterative agent development cycles.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes EarlyEval, a lightweight framework that trains a pair of LightGBM success and failure classifiers over behavioral, textual, and reference-solution features extracted from partial trajectories, halting agent runs when either classifier's calibrated confidence crosses a predefined threshold.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across SWE-bench Verified, TerminalBench, and Toolathlon benchmarks, EarlyEval eliminates 13%–26% of execution steps and up to 44.1% of input tokens at 89%–97% prediction accuracy, while perturbing per-agent resolve rates by only 1–2 percentage points and preserving leaderboard rankings with Spearman correlation ρ≥0.959.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文聚焦于人工智能与自然语言处理领域的LLM智能体评估效率问题，具体提出了一种降低LLM智能体在智能体基准测试上计算与经济成本的方法。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该论文基于先前在基准蒸馏方面的工作（如Anchor Points、tinyBenchmarks），这些工作旨在减少评估任务数量；其新思路是引入"早期结果预测"，这是一种互补的效率维度，通过在最终结果可从中间行为中预测出来时停止智能体运行，从而削减每个任务内部的成本。
3. ❓ 问题: 该论文解决了LLM智能体评估成本过高的问题（每次基准测试运行需要数百至数千美元），以及现有基准蒸馏方法未触及每任务执行成本的局限性，这一问题减缓了智能体开发的迭代循环。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该论文提出了EarlyEval，一个轻量级框架，它针对从部分轨迹中提取的行为、文本和参考解特征，训练一对LightGBM成功与失败分类器，并在任一分类器的校准置信度超过预设阈值时停止智能体运行。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在SWE-bench Verified、TerminalBench和Toolathlon三个基准测试中，EarlyEval在89%–97%的预测准确率下消除了13%–26%的执行步骤和高达44.1%的输入token，同时每个智能体的解决率仅变化1–2个百分点，并以Spearman相关系数ρ≥0.959保持了排行榜排名。
