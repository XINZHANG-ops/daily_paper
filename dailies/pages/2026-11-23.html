cmHC: Balancing Stream Mixing in Hyper-Connections via Geometric Contraction

Published: 2026-11-19

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27193

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies geometrically constrained residual-stream mixing for stable and effective hyper-connections in large language-model pretraining. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on Hyper-Connections, Sinkhorn-constrained mHC, and permutation-based mHC-lite, it introduces cmHC, which geometrically contracts the Birkhoff polytope and applies moderate contraction only to shallow layers to balance under-mixing and over-mixing. 3. **❓ Problem:** Existing manifold-constrained methods can remain near permutation vertices and under-mix streams, whereas excessive contraction homogenizes streams and weakens their distinguishability and gradient diversity. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** cmHC replaces each permutation basis with \(P'=\lambda P+(1-\lambda)(11^\top-P)/(n-1)\), preserves exact double stochasticity and spectral bounds, proves cumulative mixing, stream-difference, and coefficient-gradient bounds, applies contraction to the first \(K\) layers, and provides fused Ascend NPU operators. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Across nanoGPT models and 6B/300B MoEs, cmHC generally lowers training loss while maintaining smoother gradients, with \(\lambda=0.97\) and \(K=2\) performing best on average in GPT-2 experiments, while fused Ascend implementations reduce total hyper-connection latency by up to 44.8%.

Non-penetrating World Models

Published: 2026-11-19

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.27963

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies collision-aware interactive video world models and the enforcement of non-penetration in generative visual simulation. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on action-conditioned diffusion world models, learned 3D representations, and geometry-based collision solvers, it introduces NoPeBench and the insight that frozen DiT hidden states can anticipate obstacle contact before pixels are generated. 3. **❓ Problem:** Existing world models lack geometric feedback and often pass through, tunnel past, or erase solid obstacles when commanded into them, while prompt-based and stop-only depth-guard approaches either fail or undermine user intent. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** NoPeWorld freezes each generator and trains a sub-2M-parameter cross-attention head on Unreal Engine collision, clearance, and intent labels to rank stop, shortened-motion, turning, and sliding candidates in collision or prediction mode before chunk generation. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Across six generators and NoPeBench’s teacher-forced, geometry-validated closed-loop, and human-validated VLM evaluations, NoPeWorld reduces average violations from 74% to 16%, improves Safe-Intent performance, and transfers zero-shot to open-domain imagery and moving obstacles.

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read MC-Lens first: it is the most conceptually expansive, showing how multimodal concepts become internally readable yet selectively accessed in VLMs, and its counterfactual intervention and Jacobian-alignment toolkit is reusable on the other two papers' models. Next take cmHC, whose geometric contraction of the Birkhoff polytope is a crisp, tightly proved refinement of Hyper-Connections with concrete Ascend latency gains. Finish with NoPeWorld, the most applied piece — the claim that frozen DiT hidden states anticipate obstacle contact before pixels appear, plus a 74%→16% violation drop, makes it a satisfying payoff; note that all three share a theme of controlling or revealing how information flows between components of a system.
建议先读 MC-Lens：它概念性最强，揭示多模态概念在 VLM 内部何时变得可读、何时被选择性访问，其反事实干预与 Jacobian 对齐方法也可复用于另外两篇的模型。随后读 cmHC：它对 Birkhoff 多胞形做几何收缩，是对 Hyper-Connections 边界清晰、证明严密且有真实昇腾加速收益的改进。最后读 NoPeWorld：应用性最强，冻结的 DiT 隐状态在像素生成前即可预判障碍接触，加上违规率从 74% 降到 16%，结论很有说服力；值得注意的是三篇都围绕同一主线——如何控制或揭示模型内部组件之间的信息流动。
