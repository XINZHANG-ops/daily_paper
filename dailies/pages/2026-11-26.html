Paper 1

Real2SimLab: A Unified Platform for Real-to-Simulation Transfer

Published: 2026-11-24

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34849

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents Real2SimLab, an open-source evaluation platform for real-to-simulation transfer—converting real image/video/3D-scan observations into runnable simulations—at the intersection of computer vision, graphics, and robotics, with its main focus on coding agents that write simulation programs from video recordings. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on existing benchmarks (Physics-IQ, Video2World, 4DCodeBench, PhysCodeGym, PhySimCode, IRIS) and shared evaluation platforms (Harbor, SWE-bench, XPolicyLab, lm-evaluation-harness), the paper's new idea is to make the *delivered simulation program* the universal interface between methods and benchmarks—reducing N×M integrations to N+M—supplemented by a frame-level decontamination pipeline that yields reward-annotated training data and by the Awesome-Real2Sim map of ~300 papers. 3. **❓ Problem:** The paper solves the fragmentation problem that each real-to-simulation benchmark defines its own inputs, deliverables, and scoring code, making cross-benchmark comparison of methods prohibitively expensive and results incomparable, while also addressing the real-to-simulation-specific contamination risk whereby one recording appears in several datasets after re-encoding, resizing, or clipping. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The platform fixes one task format (visible/ vs. hidden/ directories plus metadata), uses method-wise and benchmark-wise adapters and four deliverable conventions (run.py program, 4D-world script, Blender scene, V2W package), and enforces a Build → Freeze (fingerprint) → offline sandboxed Re-run → Score (native official scorers for 5 benchmarks, or a reproduction skill of 1−e/e_static) → Report pipeline, supplemented by thumbnail-based overlap filtering, a synthetic suite with exact ground truth, a Harbor bridge for 47 agents, direct adapters for 5 agents, and 5 non-agent baselines. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Real2SimLab currently supports 47 coding agents, 5 non-agent methods, 6 existing benchmarks, and a synthetic suite; each adapter was validated against the original release (Physics-IQ scores match the official pipeline, Video2World reference packages reproduce their Push-T scores, and the synthetic generator attains a reproduction skill of 1), while the overlap check caught 36 of 40 synthetic copies and all 27 PhysCodeGym clips reused from Physics-IQ—though no systematic method comparison or model training has yet been performed.

Paper 2

Mixture-of-Loops Transformer

Published: 2026-11-24

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.34923

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper introduces Mixture-of-Loops Transformer (MoLT), a family of looped / recurrent-depth Transformer architectures in the deep-learning NLP and foundation-model domain that reuses computation across depths and loops by passing keys and values as memory.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on looped Transformers (Universal Transformer, ALBERT, recent looped-LM scaling laws), depth-recurrent attention (MoDA, DenseFormer, Attention Residuals), prefix-feedback models (Transformer-XL, LCKV, Recurrent Transformer) and encoder-sourced-KV decoders (YOCO, Universal YOCO, DeepSeek CED), the paper's new idea is to pass computed KV as selectively reusable memory via Mixture-of-Loops Attention (MoLA), yielding three variants — MoLT-R (loop residual), MoLT-KV (embedding restart + strict-prefix memory KV) and U-MoLT (U-shaped encoder–decoder split) — together with loop-forcing training and single-traversal Mixture-of-Loops (MoL) decoding that operates beyond the training loop horizon.
3. ❓ Problem: It tackles the expressivity–efficiency bottleneck of looped Transformers, where passing only a compressed residual stream between adjacent loops causes information dilution and forces strictly sequential per-token loop execution at inference, framed as the question of "what should be passed as a Transformer's loops?"
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors define MoLA as one jointly normalized softmax over concatenated sequence KV and same-position depth KV (with per-head RMS QK normalization and RoPE), implement MoLT-R (residual feedback plus current-/preceding-loop local KV reads), MoLT-KV (embedding restart with strict-prefix memory KV projected from the preceding loop's normalized output), and U-MoLT (loop-KV encoder plus a causal cross-only decoder with independent per-layer decoder KV projections), train all variants with a weighted multi-loop loop-forcing objective, and analyze/compare fixed-loop, MoL and MoL-Hybrid decoding schedules via triangular-recurrence stabilization proofs and ablations from S-size up to 700M/1.5B dense and 3B MoE models.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: In 400B-token pretraining U-MoLT consistently beats OLMo3 (700M: 59.1% vs 56.6% mean 10-task accuracy and 14.86 vs 15.64 mean 10-domain PPL; 3B MoE with 660M active parameters: −0.75 PPL and +2.1 pp accuracy; 1.5B: +1.4 pp), achieving 1.7×/1.5× token efficiency on validation/training CE, improving all 11 domains over a matched 100B-token Qwen3.5-0.8B continued-pretraining baseline with 1.4× mean token efficiency and only +0.42% parameters, while ablations favour sparse cross-layer depth access with independent per-layer decoder KV projections (best CE 3.273) and MoL-Hybrid decoding cuts total latency 28–50% with ΔCE within ±0.0014, matching non-looped OLMo3 latency (996.6 ms vs 1000.6 ms) at 4,096-token prefill.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出 Mixture-of-Loops Transformer（MoLT）系列循环深度 Transformer 架构，属于深度学习/NLP 模型架构设计领域，核心是通过将键值（KV）作为"记忆"在层深与循环之间选择性复用来同时提升表达力与效率。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在循环 Transformer（Universal Transformer、ALBERT、循环语言模型 scaling laws）、深度循环注意力（MoDA、DenseFormer、Attention Residuals）、前缀反馈模型（Transformer-XL、LCKV、Recurrent Transformer）以及编码器源 KV 解码器（YOCO、Universal YOCO、DeepSeek CED）的基础上，提出以计算所得 KV 作为可跨层深与跨循环选择性复用的记忆的 Mixture-of-Loops Attention（MoLA），由此形成三种变体——MoLT-R（loop residual）、MoLT-KV（embedding restart + 严格前缀记忆 KV）与 U-MoLT（U 形编码器–解码器分离）——并提出 loop forcing 训练以及可超越训练循环深度的单次遍历 MoL 解码。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决循环 Transformer 的表达力–效率瓶颈问题：仅以单一残差流在相邻循环间传递会稀释信息并迫使推理时逐循环串行执行，其核心提问是"循环之间究竟应传递什么"。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者将 MoLA 定义为对序列 KV 与同位置深度 KV 拼接后的单一联合归一化 softmax（配合逐头 RMS QK 归一化与 RoPE），实现 MoLT-R（残差反馈 + 当前/前一循环 local KV 读取）、MoLT-KV（embedding restart，并从前一循环归一化输出投影严格前缀记忆 KV）与 U-MoLT（loop KV 编码器 + 具备逐层独立解码器 KV 投影的因果 cross-only 解码器），以加权多循环 loop forcing 目标训练，并借助三角递推稳定性证明与消融实验比较固定循环、MoL 与 MoL-Hybrid 等解码调度，规模覆盖 S-size 至 700M/1.5B dense 与 3B MoE。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在 400B token 预训练中 U-MoLT 全面优于 OLMo3（700M：十任务平均准确率 59.1% vs 56.6%、十域平均 PPL 14.86 vs 15.64；3B MoE（660M 激活参数）：PPL 降 0.75、准确率提 2.1 个百分点；1.5B：+1.4 pp），并在验证/训练 CE 上取得 1.7×/1.5× token 效率，在 Qwen3.5-0.8B 的 100B token 继续预训练中于全部 11 个域降低 CE、平均 token 效率 1.4× 且仅增加 0.42% 参数；消融表明稀疏跨层深度访问配合逐层独立解码器 KV 投影最优（CE 3.273），MoL-Hybrid 解码在 ΔCE 不超过 ±0.0014 的前提下将总延迟降低 28–50%，并在 4,096 token prefill 下与非线性 OLMo3 延迟持平（996.6 ms vs 1000.6 ms）。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read MoLT first: it presents the most foundational contribution and the strongest scaling evidence, showing how recurrent-depth Transformers can reuse KV memory while improving accuracy and token efficiency. Read I2VT next if you are interested in generative video editing, as it transfers image-editing expertise into video models without paired video data. Real2SimLab complements both by standardizing evaluation and preventing dataset contamination, although it currently offers infrastructure rather than systematic model comparisons.
建议先读 MoLT：它提出了最基础且证据最充分的架构贡献，展示了循环深度 Transformer 如何复用 KV 记忆，同时提升准确率和 token 效率。若关注生成式视频编辑，可接着读 I2VT，它在无需成对视频数据的情况下将图像编辑能力迁移到视频模型。Real2SimLab 则从评测层面补充这两项工作，强调统一接口与数据去污染，但目前尚缺少系统性的模型对比。
