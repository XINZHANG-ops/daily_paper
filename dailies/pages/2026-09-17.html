Paper 1

ScienceIDE: Turning World's Scientific Codebase into Agent Learnable Environments

Published: 2026-09-15

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.19134

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper introduces ScienceIDE, infrastructure for converting scientific code repositories into agent-learnable environments, situated at the intersection of AI for Science and Science for AI.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds on coding agent benchmarks (SWE-bench, SWE-Gym, R2E-Gym), scientific benchmarks (SciCode, ScienceAgentBench, AInsteinBench), and RL with verifiable rewards (DeepSeek-R1, DAPO). Its new idea is separating expert scientific judgment from automated task generation, turning scientific modules with calibrated checks into reusable contracts that support evaluation, SFT, and RL through a shared episode interface.
3. ❓ Problem: The "scientific experience bottleneck"—heterogeneous toolchains, implicit numerical conventions, and specialized correctness criteria make scientific repositories difficult to convert into reliable training experience for AI agents.
4. 🛠️ Methods: Expert-grounded construction of executable environments with pinned scientific codebases, scientific checks (pointwise and invariants policies with calibrated tolerances), environment-specific task factories using reversible mutations, validation via executable evidence, and training via LoRA-based SFT and online RL with budget-truncation masking and Dr. GRPO advantages.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On ScienceIDE-Hard (85 tasks), Fable 5.1 led at 67.1% success. SFT on PhAI-IDE-4B/9B/72B showed repair reward gains (e.g., +33.33 points on PLUTO-Particles-Dust for 4B) and positive transfer to public benchmarks (e.g., BBH Word Sorting +36 points for 9B). RL improved held-out reward 2.4× on LAPS (0.357→0.857) and 2.0× on MITgcm-biogeo (0.286→0.571), with budget truncation dropping substantially.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文介绍了 ScienceIDE，一种将科学代码仓库转化为智能体可学习环境的基础设施，定位在"AI for Science"与"Science for AI"的交叉领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于编码智能体基准（SWE-bench、SWE-Gym、R2E-Gym）、科学基准（SciCode、ScienceAgentBench、AInsteinBench）以及可验证奖励的强化学习（DeepSeek-R1、DAPO）。新思路是将专家科学判断与自动化任务生成解耦，把带有校准检查的科学模块转化为可复用合约，通过统一的回合接口支持评估、SFT 和 RL。
3. ❓ 问题: "科学经验瓶颈"——异构工具链、隐含的数值约定和专门的正确性标准，使科学代码仓库难以转化为可靠的智能体训练经验。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 基于专家的构造方法，将钉扎版本科学代码库打包为可执行环境；科学检查（逐点策略与不变量策略，附校准容差）；环境特定的任务工厂使用可逆突变生成候选；通过可执行证据进行任务验证；以及基于 LoRA 的监督微调和带预算截断掩码的在线强化学习（采用 Dr. GRPO 优势函数）。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在 ScienceIDE-Hard（85 个任务）上，Fable 5.1 以 67.1% 成功率领先。PhAI-IDE-4B/9B/72B 的 SFT 显示出科学修复奖励提升（例如 4B 在 PLUTO-Particles-Dust 上提升 +33.33 分），并在公开基准上实现正向迁移（9B 在 BBH Word Sorting 上提升 +36 分）。RL 使 LAPS 保留奖励提升 2.4 倍（0.357→0.857），MITgcm-biogeo 提升 2.0 倍（0.286→0.571），预算截断率显著下降。

Paper 2

ProgramDistill: From Interactive Web Apps to Verifiable Reference-Guided SWE Tasks

Published: 2026-09-15

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.18805

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on software engineering benchmarking for AI coding agents, specifically in reference-guided software engineering where agents must infer and implement behavior by interacting with working interactive web applications.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Based on prior work like SWE-bench, R2E-Gym, SWE-smith, ProgramBench, and WebArena, the paper introduces the new concept of "reference-to-current distillation" combined with "application-to-task factorization," shifting from specification consumption to specification discovery where agents recover behavior from executable references rather than written issues.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the gap that existing code agent benchmarks require behavior to be pre-specified through issues, instructions, or tests, whereas real-world web development often requires agents to infer intended behavior directly from a working reference application and implement it in an incomplete codebase.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors propose ProgramDistill with an automated mine–craft–patch pipeline that mines replayable browser interaction traces from 26 web applications, crafts repair tasks by masking source implementations (with logic-only or logic-and-UI scopes, and atomic or cumulative compositions), and evaluates coding agents through partial-application and full-application reconstruction with restoration depth controlled by prerequisite lineages.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: The pipeline generates 1,975 verified behaviors and 4,063 tasks; on the 300-task evaluation suite, GPT-6 Astra achieves the highest mean binary score of 84.3%, with performance dropping sharply as restoration depth increases from 100% at depth 1 to 64.0% at depth 8 for Astra; in full-application reconstruction, GPT-6 Astra reaches 58.98% atomic and 49.15% cumulative recovery, while the largest failure category (59.2%) is behaviors never observed in the reference.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于AI编码代理的软件工程基准测试，特别是参考引导的软件工程，要求代理通过与可运行的交互式Web应用交互来推断并实现行为。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于SWE-bench、R2E-Gym、SWE-smith、ProgramBench和WebArena等先前工作，论文提出了"参考到当前的知识蒸馏"结合"应用到任务的分解"这一新概念，将范式从规范消费转变为规范发现，要求代理从可执行参考中恢复行为，而不是从书面问题描述中。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了现有编码代理基准要求行为通过问题描述、指令或测试预先指定，而实际Web开发中代理通常需要直接从可运行的参考应用中推断预期行为，并在不完整的代码库中实现它的差距。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者提出了ProgramDistill及其自动化的mine–craft–patch流水线：从26个Web应用中挖掘可回放的浏览器交互轨迹，通过遮蔽源码实现（采用仅逻辑或逻辑加界面两种范围，以及原子或累积两种组合方式）来构建修复任务，并通过修复深度由前提依赖关系控制的局部应用重建和完整应用重建来评估编码代理。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 流水线生成了1,975个已验证行为和4,063个任务；在300个任务的评估套件上，GPT-6 Astra取得最高的平均二进制得分84.3%，但随着修复深度从1的100%增加到8的64.0%，性能显著下降；在完整应用重建中，GPT-6 Astra达到58.98%的原子恢复率和49.15%的累积恢复率，最大失败类别（59.2%）是代理从未在参考中观察到的行为。

Paper 3

Rethinking Critic Learning in PPO: Understanding and Mitigating Value Flattening

Published: 2026-09-15

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.18708

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on reinforcement learning for large language models (LLMs), specifically addressing critic learning failures within the Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) framework applied to long-horizon reasoning tasks.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds upon PPO (Schulman et al., 2017) and its variants for LLM post-training, as well as Monte Carlo-based value estimation methods like VinePPO (Kazemnejad et al., 2025). Its new idea is identifying a previously overlooked systematic failure mode called "Value Flattening" and proposing a sparse supervision strategy (SP3O) that applies the critic loss at only a few well-separated states per response.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses Value Flattening—a phenomenon where PPO critic predictions remain comparatively flat across intermediate states, while true state values (estimated from Monte Carlo continuations) can change sharply, leading to poor within-response credit assignment and degraded policy learning, especially as state spaces grow larger.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors propose SP3O (SParse Proximal Policy Optimization), which applies the value loss only to a small set of well-separated states (e.g., at relative positions 0.3, 0.6, 0.9, plus 0.95 for long responses) to mitigate two identified causes: (1) an implicit variance penalty in the critic's MSE loss that penalizes value variation across tokens sharing the same terminal return target, and (2) redundant updates from temporally correlated neighboring states with similar gradients.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Experiments on Qwen3-4B-Base and Qwen3-8B-Base trained on DAPO-Math-17k show SP3O consistently outperforms standard PPO and GRPO, achieving up to 7.97 percentage points improvement on in-domain mathematical reasoning and 7.33 points on out-of-distribution reasoning benchmarks. Ablations confirm that K=3 supervised anchors perform best, fixed-position placement beats random placement, and adding a late-tail anchor reduces repetitive behavior and improves accuracy from 44.10% to 45.57%.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文聚焦于大语言模型（LLM）的强化学习，特别是针对长程推理任务中近端策略优化（PPO）框架内评论器（critic）学习的失败问题。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本文基于PPO（Schulman等人，2017）及其用于LLM后训练的变体，以及基于蒙特卡洛的价值估计方法（如VinePPO，Kazemnejad等人，2025）。其新思路在于识别出一种先前被忽视的系统性失败模式——"价值平坦化"（Value Flattening），并提出了一种稀疏监督策略SP3O，即在每个回复中仅对少数分散良好的状态施加评论器损失。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了PPO评论器中的"价值平坦化"问题——在中间状态之间，评论器的预测保持相对平坦，而真实的状态价值（通过蒙特卡洛延续估计）却可能发生剧烈变化，导致回复内的信用分配较差，策略学习性能下降，且随着状态空间增大问题更加严重。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者提出了SP3O（SParse Proximal Policy Optimization），该方法仅在少数分散良好的位置上施加价值损失（例如在相对位置0.3、0.6、0.9处，长回复额外加入0.95处），以缓解两个已识别的原因：（1）评论器均方误差损失中的隐式方差惩罚——该惩罚会抑制共享同一终止回报目标的各token间的价值变化；（2）来自时序相关且梯度相似的相邻状态的冗余更新。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在基于DAPO-Math-17k训练的Qwen3-4B-Base和Qwen3-8B-Base上的实验表明，SP3O始终优于标准PPO和GRPO，在域内数学推理任务上最高提升7.97个百分点，在域外推理基准上最高提升7.33个百分点。消融实验证实K=3个监督锚点效果最佳，固定位置放置优于随机放置，添加尾部锚点可将准确率从44.10%提升至45.57%，并显著降低重复生成行为（从18.33降至1.12）。

Together, the three form a coherent picture of the current frontier in environment design and RL methodology for code-reasoning agents.
