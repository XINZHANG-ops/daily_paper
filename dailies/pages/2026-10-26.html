Published: 2026-10-18

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.30181

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper introduces OpenDS-World, a benchmark for evaluating multimodal deep-search agents that perform autonomous research in real-world Web information-retrieval environments.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on prior deep-search, browser-agent, multimodal-retrieval, and interactive-access research, it proposes video-anchored, multi-source evidence chains spanning diverse modalities, file formats, and access operations.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses the lack of systematic benchmarks for testing whether agents can autonomously discover, access, process, and integrate evidence across heterogeneous live-Web sources to reach unique factual answers.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors construct 200 independently verified tasks by expanding audiovisual anchors into Web evidence chains, invert the chains into constrained questions, and evaluate 12 models with a unified search-GUI-video harness, one-hour and 100-call budgets, human baselines, and LLM-based judging.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: GPT-6 Astra achieves the best accuracy at 39.0% versus 21.1% for humans, 42.5% of tasks are unsolved by all five human solvers, and further analyses reveal difficulty-related degradation, inefficient resource use, costly tool failures, and frequent budget exhaustion.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出 OpenDS-World，用于评估在真实网络信息检索环境中执行自主研究的多模态深度搜索智能体。
