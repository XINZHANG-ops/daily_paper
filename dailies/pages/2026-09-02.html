Paper 1

SMELT: Scaling Laws for Compute-Matched MoE Looped Transformers

Published: 2026-08-31

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.01343

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on scaling laws and architecture design for Looped Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) Transformers in the domain of language model pretraining, specifically investigating compute-matched comparisons between looped and standard architectures.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The work builds on prior looped Transformer studies (Universal Transformer, Huginn, Ouro, Saunshi et al., Prairie et al.) and MoE scaling laws (Clark et al., Abnar et al.), but critically observes that prior comparisons conflated architectural gains with uncontrolled extra FLOPs. The new idea is SMELT—a recipe that loops the middle 50% of layers twice while strictly matching three budgets (per-token FLOPs, total non-embedding parameters, KV cache) simultaneously, and fitting separate Chinchilla-style scaling surfaces for each architecture.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper resolves whether looping provides an architectural advantage beyond merely spending extra computation, since prior evaluations either fixed parameter count while letting FLOPs grow or vice versa—leaving the core question of compute-matched benefits unresolved.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors (1) use MoE's decoupling of parameters from FLOPs to budget-match by narrowing hidden dimension, raising expert count, and adjusting head/GQA ratio; (2) conduct three ablations at 200M scale identifying the loop span (50%), depth-to-width ratio, and loop count (2×) recipe; (3) scale across 4×4 grid (4 sizes up to 54B params × 4 sparsity levels); (4) fit separate Chinchilla-style scaling laws; (5) perform mechanistic probing of expert routing, residual updates, and attention patterns across visits.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: SMELT saves 6.8–18.0% of training compute on the compute-optimal frontier across sparsity levels, wins 96/96 DCLM Completion pairs, 83/96 DCLM Core pairs, and downstream gains exceed validation-loss predictions. Code benefits most (20.4% CE Gain); advantages concentrate on long samples (1.52× gain ratio) and grow with in-context examples. Mechanistically, the second visit reuses retrieval coordinates (Q/K cosine 0.89–0.93), amplifies residual writes (1.2–3.5× larger), and redirects attention mass from the sink token to content-relevant positions.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文聚焦于循环（Looped）混合专家（MoE）Transformer的缩放定律与架构设计，属于语言模型预训练领域，具体研究在计算预算匹配条件下循环架构与标准架构的比较。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本研究基于此前循环Transformer工作（Universal Transformer、Huginn、Ouro、Saunshi等、Prairie et al.）以及MoE缩放定律（Clark等、Abnar等），但关键性地观察到之前对比混淆了架构优势与未受控的额外FLOPs。新颖之处在于SMELT——一种在严格匹配三项预算（每token FLOPs、总非嵌入参数量、KV缓存）的同时将中间50%层循环两次的方案，并为每种架构拟合独立的Chinchilla风格缩放曲面。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决的核心问题是：循环是否提供超越额外计算的架构优势？此前评估要么固定参数量而让FLOPs增长，要么反之，导致计算匹配条件下循环架构的实际收益这一核心问题悬而未决。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者采用的方法包括：(1) 利用MoE解耦参数与FLOPs的特性，通过缩减隐藏维度、增加专家数量及调整head/GQA比率实现预算匹配；(2) 在200M规模进行三项消融实验，确定循环跨度、深度宽度比和循环计数（2×）的配方；(4) 跨4×4网格（4个规模至54B参数×4个稀疏度级别）进行缩放；(5) 拟合独立的Chinchilla风格缩放定律；(6) 对两次访问中的专家路由、残差更新和注意力模式进行机制性探查。
5. 📊 结果与评估: SMELT在计算最优前沿上节省了6.8–18.0%的训练计算量，在96/96的DCLM Completion配对中获胜，在83/96的DCLM Core配对中获胜，且下游收益超出验证损失所预测水平。代码领域受益最大（20.4%的CE Gain）；优势集中在长样本上（增益比1.52×）并随上下文示例数量增长。机制上，第二次访问复用检索坐标（Q/K余弦相似度0.89–0.93），放大残差写入（1.2–3.5×更大），并将注意力质量从sink token重定向到与内容相关的位置。

Paper 2

Qwen-Drive-1.0: An Initial Step towards a Vision-Language Foundation Model for Autonomous Driving

Published: 2026-08-30

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.00111

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper presents a vision-language foundation model for autonomous driving, integrating 3D perception, visual question answering, and motion planning within a unified framework.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Built upon pretrained VLMs (Qwen3.5-4B) and existing driving VLA adaptation approaches, the new idea is to unify 3D perception, VQA, and motion planning without modifying the pretrained VLM architecture by attaching external modules.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses two limitations of existing driving VLA methods: textual VQA supervision does not constrain 3D spatial structure, and extensive domain adaptation causes catastrophic forgetting of general capabilities needed for OOD reasoning and cockpit-driving integration.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The method uses a shared pretrained VLM with an external BEV perception head (joint 3D detection, semantic occupancy, and map segmentation) and a Planning Expert (flow matching with endpoint parameterization for trajectory generation), trained via a four-stage staged recipe that combines driving-specific supervision with general vision-language data.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Qwen-Drive-1.0 achieves 43.95 mAP and 60.99 map mIoU on nuScenes, 90.7 PDMS on NAVSIM, 7.91 RFS on WOD-E2E test split, while preserving general vision-language capability (66.41 vs 67.40 for Qwen3.5-4B) and significantly improving driving VQA (69.43 average, +5.91 over baseline).
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文提出了一个用于自动驾驶的视觉-语言基础模型，将3D感知、视觉问答和运动规划统一在一个框架内。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于预训练VLM（Qwen3.5-4B）和现有驾驶VLA自适应方法，新思路是通过附加外部模块，在不修改预训练VLM架构的前提下，统一3D感知、视觉问答和运动规划。
3. ❓ 问题: 该论文解决了现有驾驶VLA方法的两个局限性：文本VQA监督无法约束3D空间结构，且大量的领域自适应会导致对通用能力的灾难性遗忘，而这对于分布外推理和座舱-驾驶集成部署是必需的。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该方法使用共享的预训练VLM，配备外部BEV感知头（联合3D检测、语义占据和地图分割）和规划专家（采用带端点参数化的流匹配进行轨迹生成），通过一个结合驾驶特定监督和通用视觉语言数据的四阶段渐进式训练流程进行训练。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Qwen-Drive-1.0在nuScenes上达到43.95 mAP和60.99地图mIoU，在NAVSIM上达到90.7 PDMS，在WOD-E2E测试集上达到7.91 RFS，同时保留了通用视觉语言能力（66.41 vs Qwen3.5-4B的67.40），并显著提升了驾驶VQA性能（平均69.43，比基线提升5.91）。

Paper 3

H3-World: Turning Language Understanding into World Control

Published: 2026-08-31

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.01560

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on interactive world modeling and controllable video generation, specifically turning large pretrained video generators into interactive world models using language as a control interface.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The work builds on large-scale video generation models (like MiniMax-H3, Wan, Stable Video Diffusion) and prior action-conditioned world models (such as Genie, GameGAN, ReactiveGWM, and Incantation). The new idea is that instead of designing dedicated action modules, control can be grounded in the model's native language pathway using lightweight LoRA adaptation, with temporal attention routing ensuring precise temporal alignment.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the gap between video generation and interactive world modeling—namely, how to provide precise, temporally grounded action control in pretrained video generators without introducing heavy, dedicated action-specific modules that may disturb pretrained capabilities.
4. 🛠️ Methods: H3-World represents character and camera actions as compositional textual instructions aligned with video latent intervals via mirrored temporal positions, enforces single-egress temporal attention routing through masking to prevent control leakage, and adapts the model using only LoRA on QKV and output projections (0.199% trainable parameters) with 8,000 gameplay samples and 10,000 optimization steps.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: The framework achieves effective character and camera control while preserving generation quality, generalizes compositionally to unseen action combinations and visually distinct initial observations, and outperforms direct additive-bias and FiLM action-conditioning variants that produce weak or inconsistent responses on held-out clips.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于交互式世界建模与可控视频生成领域，具体研究如何将大型预训练视频生成器转化为交互式世界模型，并将语言作为控制接口。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 研究基于大规模视频生成模型（如 MiniMax-H3、Wan、Stable Video Diffusion）以及先前的动作条件世界模型（如 Genie、GameGAN、ReactiveGWM、Incantation）。其新思路在于不设计专用动作模块，而是通过轻量级 LoRA 适配将控制锚定在模型原生语言路径中，并借助时序注意力路由实现精确的时序对齐。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决的问题是如何在预训练视频生成器中提供精确、时序对齐的动作控制，同时避免引入重型、专用动作模块（这些模块可能损害预训练已获得的能力），弥合视频生成与交互式世界建模之间的鸿沟。
4. 🛠️ 方法: H3-World 将角色和相机动作表示为组合式文本指令，通过镜像时序位置与视频潜在区间对齐；通过掩码实现单出口（single-egress）时序注意力路由以防止控制泄漏；仅对 QKV 与输出投影应用 LoRA 适配（0.199% 可训练参数），使用 8,000 个游戏样本和 10,000 步优化完成训练。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 该框架在保持生成质量的同时实现了有效的角色与相机控制，并能组合泛化到未见过的动作组合及视觉差异显著的初始观察；在保留测试片段上优于产生弱或不一致响应的直接加性偏置与 FiLM 动作条件变体。
