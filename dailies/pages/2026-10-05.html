Paper 1

OneStreamer: Unifying Perception, Memory, and Proactive Response in Streaming Video Interaction

Published: 2026-09-30

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.01762

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on streaming video understanding and interaction, specifically addressing how large language models can process continuous video streams, retain past evidence, and respond proactively in real-time applications like live-stream assistants and wearable agents.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work in online video LLMs (e.g., VideoLLM-Online, Streamo, Dispider), memory compression methods, streaming captioning, and state-token-based response control. Its new idea is to unify query-independent evidence recording and task response through a shared proactive generation process, treating memory formation and response timing as a single causal generation problem rather than separate components.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the challenge of forming reusable factual memory in streaming video LLMs without compromising real-time perception, while also learning when the available evidence is sufficient to warrant a user-visible response—avoiding both the dominance of repeated waiting states and information loss from discarded historical frames.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes OneStreamer with two key components: (1) Proactive Hierarchical Caption Memory (PHCM), which generates time-grounded local-detail captions () and event-level summaries () to complement a recent visual window; (2) Proactive State Transition Learning (PSTL), which selects representative state-change and state-persistence tokens to address state-label imbalance. It also constructs OneStreamer-1M, a dataset with over 1 million streaming records synthesized through an evidence-aligned pipeline.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: The 4B OneStreamer model achieves state-of-the-art results on all eight evaluated streaming video understanding benchmarks (OVOBench, StreamingBench, OVBench, ODVBench, ProactiveVideoQA, OmniMMI, OVO-Timing, ViSpeak), with an average relative improvement of 25.0% over the Qwen3-VL baseline and 6.1% over the strongest competing method. Ablations show that PHCM improves historical QA without degrading real-time perception, and PSTL outperforms dense state supervision while using only 27.5% of annotated state tokens.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文聚焦于流式视频理解与交互领域，具体研究大型语言模型如何处理连续视频流、保留历史证据，并在实时应用（如直播助手、可穿戴设备、安防监控）中实现主动响应。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该论文基于在线视频大模型（如VideoLLM-Online、Streamo、Dispider）、记忆压缩方法、流式字幕生成以及基于状态令牌的响应控制等先前研究。其新思路是通过一个共享的主动生成过程，将与查询无关的证据记录与任务响应统一起来，把记忆形成和响应时机的学习视为单一的因果生成问题，而非相互独立的模块。

Paper 2

On-Policy or Off-Policy Learning? A Systematic Study of Distillation Dynamics

Published: 2026-09-27

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.35259

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies distillation dynamics in large language models (LLMs), specifically comparing on-policy distillation (OnPD) and off-policy distillation (OffPD) in the context of strong-to-weak knowledge distillation for reasoning tasks. The domain lies at the intersection of machine learning, natural language processing, and post-training methods for LLMs.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work on supervised fine-tuning (SFT), reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR), and knowledge distillation (Hinton et al., 2015; Agarwal et al., 2023; DeepSeek-AI, 2025). It also engages with claims that on-policy learning reduces catastrophic forgetting (Shenfeld et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2025), produces sparser parameter updates (Mukherjee et al., 2025), and improves generalization (Chu et al., 2025; Yuan et al., 2026). The new idea is to disentangle the rollout policy from other confounded factors (objective, supervision density, learning rate) by using a controlled strong-to-weak distillation testbed that independently varies rollout policy, token-level KL direction, and learning rate.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the ambiguity in attributing observed differences between SFT and RLVR to rollout policy alone, since these methods also differ in objective, reward signal, supervision density, and optimization procedure. It seeks to isolate the causal role of rollout policy and determine when (if ever) on-policy learning truly provides advantages over off-policy learning in distillation settings.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors use strong-to-weak distillation as a controlled testbed, distilling Llama-3.1-8B teachers into Llama-3.2-1B students (with Qwen2.5-7B/1.5B as additional validation) across three reasoning tasks: MedReason, Science, and Countdown. They independently vary rollout policy (OnPD vs OffPD), token-level KL direction (forward vs reverse KL), and learning rate (1×10⁻⁵ or 5×10⁻⁵), computing KL over the full vocabulary. They introduce a rollout-policy spectrum parameterized by λ to interpolate between student and teacher rollouts, analyze token-level KL gradients theoretically, and perform ablations removing gradient clipping, using sampled KL, and training on longer rollouts (Numina-MATH).
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: The study finds no consistent advantage from on-policy rollouts in final in-distribution accuracy (~72-73% for both OnPD and OffPD), catastrophic forgetting, or parameter-update sparsity. KL direction more clearly shapes task performance (forward KL achieves 71-73% across all rollout policies; reverse KL ranges from 35-72%), while learning rate primarily governs forgetting (drops of 1.3pp at low LR vs 11.2-14.0pp at high LR) and update sparsity (85-90% at low LR vs 52-60% at high LR). Gradient analysis and rollout-policy spectrum experiments reveal forward KL is remarkably robust to rollout policy, while reverse KL is substantially more sensitive and favors student-favoured rollouts. On-policy data improves generalization to harder Countdown variants (10-15% pass@k gains) under both KL directions, but this advantage does not reliably persist after subsequent RLVR. OnPD with reverse KL reduces incidental teacher-style transfer. These findings remain robust across model families, sampled KL estimators, without gradient clipping, and on longer reasoning tasks.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究大语言模型（LLM）的蒸馏动态，特别比较了在强弱知识蒸馏背景下，用于推理任务的在策略蒸馏（OnPD）和离策略蒸馏（OffPD）。该领域处于机器学习、自然语言处理和LLM后训练方法的交叉点。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本文建立在先前关于监督微调（SFT）、可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）以及知识蒸馏（Hinton等人，2015；Agarwal等人，2023；DeepSeek-AI，2025）的研究之上。它还涉及关于在策略学习可以减少灾难性遗忘（Shenfeld等人，2025；Chen等人，2025）、产生更稀疏的参数更新（Mukherjee等人，2025）以及改善泛化能力（Chu等人，2025；Yuan等人，2026）的论断。新的思路是通过使用一个可控的强弱蒸馏测试平台，独立地变化rollout策略、token级KL方向和学习率，从而将rollout策略与其他混淆因素（目标函数、监督密度、学习率）解耦。
3. ❓ 问题: 本文解决了将SFT和RLVR之间观察到的差异归因于rollout策略的模糊性，因为这些方法在目标函数、奖励信号、监督密度和优化过程方面也存在差异。它旨在隔离rollout策略的因果作用，并确定在策略学习在蒸馏设置中何时（如果有的话）真正优于离策略学习。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者将强弱蒸馏用作可控测试平台，将Llama-3.1-8B教师模型蒸馏到Llama-3.2-1B学生模型（使用Qwen2.5-7B/1.5B作为额外验证），跨三个推理任务：MedReason、Science和Countdown。他们独立变化rollout策略（OnPD与OffPD）、token级KL方向（前向KL与反向KL）和学习率（1×10⁻⁵或5×10⁻⁵），在整个词汇表上计算KL。他们引入了一个由λ参数化的rollout策略谱来在学生和教师rollout之间进行插值，理论上分析token级KL梯度，并执行消融实验：移除梯度裁剪、使用采样KL以及在更长rollout（Numina-MATH）上训练。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 研究发现，在最终的分布内准确率（OnPD和OffPD均约为72-73%）、灾难性遗忘或参数更新稀疏度方面，在策略rollout没有一致优势。KL方向更清晰地塑造任务性能（前向KL在所有rollout策略下达到71-73%；反向KL范围为35-72%），而学习率主要控制遗忘（低学习率下下降1.3个百分点，高学习率下下降11.2-14.0个百分点）和更新稀疏度（低学习率下85-90%，高学习率下52-60%）。梯度分析和rollout策略谱实验揭示了前向KL对rollout策略具有显著的鲁棒性，而反向KL则明显更敏感并偏向于学生偏好的rollout。在两种KL方向下，在策略数据都改善了对更难Countdown变体的泛化能力（pass@k提高10-15%），但这一优势在随后的RLVR之后并未持续存在。OnPD结合反向KL减少了偶然的教师风格迁移。这些发现在不同模型系列、采样KL估计器、无梯度裁剪以及在更长推理任务上都保持稳健。

Paper 3

Does Learning Protein Folding Generalize to Broader Reasoning?

Published: 2026-09-29

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.38879

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper investigates whether post-training language models on protein structure prediction (a solved scientific problem) can transfer to broader reasoning capabilities, spanning the intersection of natural language processing, structural biology, and AI reasoning.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Built on protein structure prediction advances (AlphaFold, OpenFold) and protein language models (ESM, SaProt), as well as prior work on instruction tuning and synthetic data for general capability; the new idea is treating known protein 3D coordinates as a verifiable, non-linguistic supervision source to test whether structure-dense scientific data can systematically improve broad reasoning in language models.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses whether specialized scientific post-training (protein folding) generalizes to non-protein downstream reasoning tasks, and whether non-linguistic, structure-dense data can serve as practical post-training supervision beyond human-written text.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors build FoldingCorpus (12 deterministic structural operators applied to 1,200 proteins yielding 14,400 QA records) and Fold2Reason, which post-trains Qwen3.5-9B via LoRA with two complementary signals: discrete structural answers through the native LM head and continuous 3D geometry decoded from a shared workspace, with matched controls (Hidden Geometry, Format Copy, Fixed Shuffle) for comparison.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Fold2Reason improves General-10 macro-average accuracy from 45.09% to 48.33% (+3.23 pp) with positive gains on all 10 benchmarks spanning spatial, graph, scientific, and mixed reasoning, while matched controls yield substantially smaller or negative gains; structural readout scores are 2.7–3.5× those of the Qwen3.5-9B baseline, with transfer persisting across Qwen3.5 scales and InternVL3.5-8B but remaining neutral for Gemma-4-12B-IT.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文研究在蛋白质结构预测（已解决的科学问题）上对语言模型进行后训练，是否能迁移到更广泛的推理能力，涵盖自然语言处理、结构生物学与AI推理的交叉领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于蛋白质结构预测进展（AlphaFold、OpenFold）以及蛋白质语言模型（ESM、SaProt），并参考了指令微调和合成数据提升通用能力的研究；新思路是将已知的蛋白质三维坐标作为可验证、非语言的监督来源，测试结构密集的科学数据能否系统性提升语言模型的通用推理能力。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文探讨专门的科学后训练（蛋白质折叠）能否泛化到非蛋白质的下游推理任务，以及非语言、结构密集的数据能否成为人类文本之外实用的后训练监督来源。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者构建了FoldingCorpus（对1,200个蛋白质应用12种确定性结构操作符，生成14,400个问答记录）和Fold2Reason，通过LoRA对Qwen3.5-9B进行后训练，采用两种互补信号：通过原生语言模型头的离散结构答案和通过共享工作空间解码的连续三维几何信息，并设计了匹配对照组（Hidden Geometry、Format Copy、Fixed Shuffle）进行比较。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Fold2Reason将General-10宏平均准确率从45.09%提升至48.33%（+3.23 pp），在涵盖空间、图、科学和混合推理的10个基准上均获得正向增益，而匹配对照组增益显著较小或为负；结构读出分数是Qwen3.5-9B基线的2.7–3.5倍，迁移效果在Qwen3.5各规模和InternVL3.5-8B上持续存在，但在Gemma-4-12B-IT上保持中性。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with the distillation dynamics paper (Paper 2) for its rigorous controlled study challenging the assumption that on-policy learning is inherently superior—its surprising negative result will reframe how you think about the other two papers. Papers 2 and 3 are closely linked through the question of what actually drives capability transfer in post-training: Paper 2 isolates methodological factors (rollout policy, KL direction, learning rate) while Paper 3 probes a different axis—whether non-linguistic, structure-dense supervision (protein folding) can transfer to general reasoning. Paper 1 (OneStreamer) is more application-focused but complements the other two as a case study in designing multimodal LLM training for a challenging real-world inference regime.
建议先阅读第二篇（蒸馏动力学论文），因为它通过严谨的对照实验挑战了"在策略学习必然优于离策略学习"这一常见假设，其出人意料的负向结论会帮助你重新审视另外两篇论文。第二篇和第三篇紧密相关，都聚焦于后训练阶段能力迁移的核心问题：第二篇隔离了方法学因素（rollout策略、KL方向、学习率）的影响，而第三篇则探索了不同维度——非语言的、结构密集的监督信号（蛋白质折叠）能否迁移到通用推理。第一篇（OneStreamer）更偏应用，但可作为另外两篇的补充案例，展示面向复杂真实推理场景的多模态大语言模型训练设计。
