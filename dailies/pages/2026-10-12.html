Paper 1

DiffusionART: Efficient, Stable and Scalable Reinforcement of Diffusion Models

Published: 2026-10-07

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.12572

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on online reinforcement learning (RL) for fine-tuning visual diffusion and flow-matching models in the domain of image and video generation.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work in diffusion reinforcement learning, specifically reverse-process methods like Flow-GRPO and forward-process methods like DiffusionNFT. The new idea introduces DiffusionART (Diffusion Reinforcement with Aggregation-First Reward Tilting), which diagnoses a hidden forward–reverse consistency force in NFT-style training and proposes an aggregation-first guidance approach with drift-adaptive restoration.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the instability of NFT-style forward-process diffusion RL that depends on joint positive-negative objectives and carefully tuned exponential moving average (EMA) schedules, while maintaining efficiency and eliminating per-task tuning requirements.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes DiffusionART, which (1) aggregates each prompt's endpoints and rewards into a shared reward-improving direction G(c) applied to reverse-rollout states, removing the consistency force by construction; (2) uses a second MSE update with drift-adaptive restoration proportional to each state's displacement; and (3) adds a trust-region mask mechanism based on a KL proxy to constrain excessive policy updates.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On HunyuanVideo-13B with equally weighted VQ:MQ rewards, DiffusionART reaches late-stage mean reward levels of DiffusionNFT and Flow-GRPO-Fast with 12.02× and 11.72× fewer GPU hours, while achieving 28.8% and 53.0% higher mean training rewards. For image-to-video generation, it reaches MQ 6 with 4.8× fewer GPU hours than Flow-GRPO-Fast, and demonstrates stable training without per-task tuning across text-to-image, text-to-video, and image-to-video tasks.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文聚焦于视觉扩散和流匹配模型的在线强化学习微调，属于图像和视频生成领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本文基于扩散强化学习的先前工作，特别是反向过程方法如Flow-GRPO和前向过程方法如DiffusionNFT。新思路是提出了DiffusionART（聚合优先奖励倾斜的扩散强化），诊断了NFT风格训练中隐藏的正反向一致性力，并提出了聚合优先的引导方法和漂移自适应恢复机制。
3. ❓ 问题: 本文解决了NFT风格前向过程扩散强化学习的不稳定性问题，该方法依赖于联合正负目标和精心调节的指数移动平均（EMA）计划，同时需要保持效率并消除每任务调节的需求。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 本文提出了DiffusionART，包括：（1）将每个提示的端点和奖励聚合为共享的奖励改进方向G(c)，并将其应用于反向展开状态，通过构造消除一致性力；（2）使用第二次MSE更新实现与每个状态位移成比例的漂移自适应恢复；（3）添加基于KL代理的信任域掩码机制以约束过度的策略更新。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在HunyuanVideo-13B上使用等权重VQ:MQ奖励时，DiffusionART以12.02倍和11.72倍更少的GPU小时达到DiffusionNFT和Flow-GRPO-Fast的后期平均奖励水平，同时平均训练奖励分别高出28.8%和53.0%。对于图生视频任务，它以比Flow-GRPO-Fast少4.8倍的GPU小时达到MQ 6，并在文本到图像、文本到视频和图生视频任务中展示了无需每任务调节的稳定训练。

Reflex: Visually Grounded Reactive Humanoid Control

Published: 2026-10-07

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.12772

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on visually grounded reactive whole-body control for humanoid robots, specifically within robotics, reinforcement learning, and humanoid manipulation.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds upon prior work in catching moving objects, humanoid whole-body control with motion priors, and privileged learning for visual sim-to-real transfer, proposing a novel three-stage decomposition that decouples whole-body control, object dynamics inference, and visual perception.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the challenge of enabling humanoid robots to reactively catch fast-moving, uninstrumented objects using egocentric vision, requiring whole-body coordination, real-time reaction to object dynamics, and visual grounding simultaneously.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors introduce Reflex, a three-stage framework: Stage 1 trains a state-based whole-body catching policy with PPO and human motion priors; Stage 2 learns a compact object-dynamics latent via action matching and DAgger; Stage 3 grounds this latent in egocentric RGB-D using a GRU-based visual encoder while keeping the controller fixed.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Reflex achieves 85.7% catch success in simulation from RGB-D (vs. 87.9% with privileged input) and 65%/40% real-robot success on a Unitree G1 at 2.5/5 m, reaching 76%/59% of human catchers' performance.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于类人机器人的视觉接地反应式全身控制，属于机器人学、强化学习和类人机器人操作领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 研究基于物体接取、结合动作先验的类人机器人全身控制以及特权学习用于视觉仿真到现实迁移的现有工作，提出了一种将全身控制、物体动力学推断和视觉感知解耦的三阶段分解新框架。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了使类人机器人能够使用自我中心视觉反应性地接取快速移动的未标记物体的难题，需要同时实现全身协调、对物体动力学的实时反应和视觉感知。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者提出了Reflex，一个三阶段框架：第一阶段使用PPO和人体动作先验训练基于状态的全身接取策略；第二阶段通过动作匹配和DAgger学习紧凑的物体动力学潜变量；第三阶段在控制器固定的情况下，使用基于GRU的视觉编码器将潜变量接地到自我中心RGB-D。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Reflex在仿真中从RGB-D实现85.7%的接取成功率（特权输入下为87.9%），在Unitree G1真实机器人上2.5/5米处分别达到65%/40%的成功率，达到人类接取者表现的76%/59%。

Paper 3

EmbodiedCLBench: Evaluating Continual Learning for Self-Evolving Embodied Agents

Published: 2026-10-08

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.13021

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on continual learning evaluation for self-evolving embodied agents, situated at the intersection of embodied AI, continual learning, and agent benchmarking.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior continual learning research (which has focused on knowledge tasks, digital environments, and simulated worlds) and embodied AI benchmarks (covering perception, navigation, manipulation, and planning), but notes that none specifically evaluate continual learning in embodied settings. Its new idea is to use compositional generalization as a principled evaluation framework—agents should first learn basic tasks and then recombine acquired skills to solve unseen, complex advanced tasks—constructing the first embodied simulation benchmark (EmbodiedCLBench) for self-evolving agents.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the lack of a dedicated benchmark to evaluate whether self-evolving embodied agents can accumulate experience from prior interactions and transfer learned skills to solve novel, more complex tasks in physical environments.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors use the TongSim simulation platform to construct three task levels (atomic actions, basic tasks, advanced tasks), build offline reproducible state-transition graphs for deterministic evaluation, and test four agent harnesses (HERMES, OPENCLAW, CODEX, CLAUDECODE) paired with two backbone models under three settings: zero-shot, In-Context Learning (ICL), and Skill Learning, analyzing effects of compositional complexity, number of learning samples, error behavior, and step budget sensitivity.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Agents achieve ~50% zero-shot success on basic tasks but only ~10% on advanced tasks; ICL and Skill Learning raise advanced task success to above 30%, showing experience consistently helps. The improvement gain over zero-shot remains stable as task complexity increases (from 3 to 6 atomic actions), but does not scale with more learning samples—most gain comes from a single basic task with no consistent further improvement. Error analysis reveals that experience reduces invalid actions by 13–20% and increases optimal action selection, while success rates improve monotonically with additional step budget tolerance.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文聚焦于自我进化具身智能体的持续学习评估，处于具身AI、持续学习和智能体基准测试的交叉领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于先前的持续学习研究（侧重知识任务、数字环境和模拟世界）和具身AI基准（涵盖感知、导航、操作和规划），但指出没有专门评估具身环境中持续学习能力的基准。其新思路是以组合泛化为原则性评估框架——智能体应先学习基础任务，并将所学技能重新组合以解决未见过的复杂高级任务——构建首个用于自我进化智能体的具身模拟基准（EmbodiedCLBench）。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了缺乏专用基准的问题，即无法评估自我进化具身智能体是否能够从先前交互中积累经验，并将所学技能迁移到物理环境中新的、更复杂的任务上。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者使用TongSim模拟平台构建三个任务层级（原子动作、基础任务、高级任务），构建离线可复现的状态转移图以进行确定性评估，并在三种设置（零样本、上下文学习ICL、技能学习）下测试四个智能体框架（HERMES、OPENCLAW、CODEX、CLAUDECODE）配合两个骨干模型，分析组合复杂度、学习样本数量、错误行为和步骤预算敏感性的影响。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 智能体在基础任务上获得约50%的零样本成功率，但在高级任务上仅约10%；ICL和技能学习将高级任务成功率提升至30%以上，表明经验持续有效。随着任务复杂度增加（从3到6个原子动作），相对于零样本的提升保持稳定，但不会随着学习样本数量增加而扩展——大部分增益来自单个基础任务，没有持续进一步的提升。错误分析显示，经验使无效动作减少了13–20%，并增加了最优动作的选择，而成功率随额外步骤预算容差的增加而单调改善。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with DiffusionART (Paper 1) — it offers the most impactful practical result (12× fewer GPU hours vs. DiffusionNFT and Flow-GRPO-Fast) and a clear theoretical insight about the hidden consistency force in NFT-style training. All three papers share an RL thread: DiffusionART uses online policy optimization for generative models, Reflex applies PPO with motion priors to whole-body humanoid control, and EmbodiedCLBench evaluates continual/skill-learning in embodied agents — so reading them in this order naturally progresses from efficient RL training to RL-driven control to RL-based agent evaluation.
建议先读 DiffusionART（第1篇）——它在效率上成果最显著（相比 DiffusionNFT 和 Flow-GRPO-Fast 节省 12 倍 GPU 小时），并提出了关于 NFT 式训练中隐藏一致性力的清晰理论洞见。三篇政策均围绕强化学习展开：DiffusionART 将在线策略优化用于生成模型，Reflex 用 PPO 结合动作先验实现人形机器人全身控制，EmbodiedCLBench 则评估具身智能体中的持续/技能学习，按此顺序阅读可自然地从高效 RL 训练过渡到 RL 驱动的控制，再到基于 RL 的智能体评估。
