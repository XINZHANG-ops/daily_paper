Paper 1

Motion Mamba 2.0: Autoregressive Diffusion Mamba for Long-Horizon Humanoid Control

Published: 2026-11-22

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.29897

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper addresses long-horizon, real-time, text- and spatially controllable humanoid motion generation for interactive animation and Unitree G1 control.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on ARDY-style autoregressive root–body diffusion, Mamba state-space models, and the authors’ earlier Motion Mamba, it introduces persistent content memory that converts accepted recurrent-state summaries, changes, and text features into attention-readable slots, together with accepted-only history commits and state-preserving CUDA optimizations.
3. ❓ Problem: It tackles the loss of distant context in bounded attention caches, the difficulty of exposing compressed recurrent history directly to attention, and the inference overhead of repeatedly denoising short motion windows under changing text and spatial goals.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The framework generates 52-frame motion windows with a two-stage root–body diffusion model combining local attention, resumable Mamba-3 recurrence, and persistent content memory, commits only accepted clean windows, and accelerates execution through flow simplification, fused kernels, and CUDA Graph replay.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On retargeted HiPHI, it achieves 7.55 cm root error and 13.64 cm terminal constraint error at 39.52 s, ablations validate recurrent memory and PCM, optimized latency drops from 1685.35 to 276.14 ms for a 6.138× speedup, and the physical G1 completes 94/100 tasks without falls.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究面向交互动画与 Unitree G1 控制的长时间、实时文本与空间可控人形运动生成。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在 ARDY 式自回归根—身体扩散、Mamba 状态空间模型及作者早期 Motion Mamba 的基础上，本文提出持久内容记忆（PCM），将已接受的循环状态摘要、状态变化和文本特征转换为注意力可读槽位，并采用仅提交已接受历史及保状态 CUDA 加速。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决有界注意力缓存丢失远期上下文、压缩循环历史难以直接供注意力检索，以及变化文本与空间目标下重复去噪短运动窗口所导致的高推理开销。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该框架以两阶段根—身体扩散模型逐个生成52帧运动窗口，组合局部注意力、可恢复的 Mamba-3 循环和持久内容记忆，仅提交已接受的干净窗口，并通过执行流简化、融合内核和 CUDA Graph 重放加速。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在重定向 HiPHI 上，模型在39.52秒时取得7.55厘米根位置误差和13.64厘米末端约束误差，消融实验验证了循环记忆与 PCM 的作用，优化后延迟由1685.35毫秒降至276.14毫秒、加速6.138倍，实体 G1 在无跌倒情况下完成94/100次任务。

Paper 2

Embo-Harness: Towards Understanding Frontier-Model-as-Policy Harness

Published: 2026-11-22

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.31201

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies how to design robotic harnesses that use frontier multimodal models directly as closed-loop manipulation policies and investigates the capabilities underlying their performance.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Whereas prior tool-, code-, and model-as-policy systems primarily demonstrated end-to-end performance, Embo-Harness systematically ablates prompting, planning, action execution, and tool use while probing whether spatial perception or low-level action generation drives frontier-model competence.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the lack of principled guidance for building effective frontier-model-as-policy harnesses and the lack of understanding of why general-purpose frontier models can outperform specialized VLAs and WAMs.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors conduct progressive controlled ablations on 20 RoboLab tasks with three runs per task, derive a recipe using absolute EEF actions, fixed chunks, high-resolution multi-view observations, short multi-action sequences, and VLA refinement, and evaluate it on RoboLab-120, four real-world tasks, and spatial-estimation probes.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Embo-Harness achieves 83.3% success and an 89.2 score on RoboLab-120—26.2 percentage points above SenseNova-RoboRSI—completes 15 of 20 real-world trials, and shows that accurate spatial target estimation contributes more than explicit joint-level or trajectory-level action generation.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究如何设计将前沿多模态模型直接用作闭环操作策略的机器人控制框架，并探究支撑其性能的核心能力。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 既有工具式、代码式和模型式策略系统主要展示整体性能，而Embo-Harness通过系统消融提示、规划、动作执行和工具使用，并探查空间感知与低层动作生成对前沿模型能力的贡献。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决前沿模型直接充当机器人策略时缺乏系统设计原则，以及通用前沿模型为何能优于专用VLA和WAM这一机制尚不清楚的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者在20个RoboLab任务上以每任务三次运行进行渐进式受控消融，形成包含绝对末端执行器动作、固定动作块、高分辨率多视角观测、短多动作序列和VLA精化的配方，并在RoboLab-120、四个真实世界任务及空间估计实验中验证。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Embo-Harness在RoboLab-120上取得83.3%的成功率和89.2的得分，比SenseNova-RoboRSI高26.2个百分点，完成20次真实世界试验中的15次，并表明精确空间目标估计比显式关节级或轨迹级动作生成更为关键。

WeMM-Ecom: Advancing Multimodal Content Understanding for WeChat E-Commerce

Published: 2026-11-22

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.30662

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents WeMM-Ecom, a family of open multimodal large language models for WeChat e-commerce spanning 0.8B–35B-A3B and supporting both thinking and non-thinking modes.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on Qwen3.5, general MLLMs, and prior e-commerce adaptation, it introduces variant-level, evidence-grounded, rule-conditioned multimodal supervision, progressive domain continued pre-training, hybrid-thinking on-policy distillation with model merging and GRPO, and a comprehensive e-commerce evaluation framework.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses general MLLMs’ inadequate understanding of product variants, dense visual evidence, cross-listing matching, and platform compliance rules, while preventing domain specialization from degrading general perception and reasoning.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The models undergo two-stage full-parameter continued pre-training on 370.3B and 76.7B tokens with e-commerce data shares of 45.8% and 61.1%, followed by hybrid-thinking OPD, an equal-weight merge with post-trained Qwen3.5, and GRPO using task-specific verifiable rewards.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across 28 e-commerce and 64 general benchmarks, every model outperforms its size-matched Qwen3.5 baseline on e-commerce and matches or exceeds it generally, while WeMM-Ecom-35B-A3B leads evaluated flagships with 74.4 despite 5×–22× fewer total parameters and is deployed at billion-request daily scale.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出 WeMM-Ecom 系列开放多模态大语言模型，面向微信电商，覆盖 0.8B 至 35B-A3B 规模并同时支持思考与非思考模式。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该研究以 Qwen3.5、通用多模态模型及既有电商适配研究为基础，创新性地引入变体级、可溯证、规则条件化的多模态监督、渐进式领域继续预训练、结合模型融合与 GRPO 的混合思考蒸馏，以及系统化电商评测框架。
3. ❓ 问题: 研究旨在解决通用多模态模型对商品变体、密集视觉证据、跨商品匹配和平台合规规则理解不足的问题，同时避免领域专化损害通用感知与推理能力。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 各模型以对应的 Qwen3.5 Base 初始化，在电商数据占比分别为 45.8% 和 61.1% 的两阶段全参数继续预训练中分别学习 370.3B 和 76.7B token，随后进行混合思考 OPD、与后训练 Qwen3.5 的等权参数融合以及采用任务可验证奖励的 GRPO。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在 28 项电商和 64 项通用基准上，所有模型均超过同规模 Qwen3.5 的电商表现并达到或优于其通用能力，其中 WeMM-Ecom-35B-A3B 以 74.4 分领先受评旗舰模型且总参数量少 5–22 倍，并已支撑每日十亿级请求的生产部署。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read the humanoid motion-generation paper first: its combination of persistent recurrent memory, long-horizon controllability, and a 6.1× inference speedup is especially notable, with the G1 deployment providing compelling real-world validation. Embo-Harness is the closest conceptual companion because it likewise turns a generative model into a closed-loop controller, while WeMM-Ecom broadens the theme to domain specialization under real-world deployment constraints.
建议先读人形运动生成论文：持久循环记忆、长时程可控生成与 6.1 倍推理加速构成其突出贡献，G1 实机部署也提供了有力验证。Embo-Harness 与之最接近，同样探索如何将生成模型构造成闭环控制器；WeMM-Ecom 则将主题拓展到实际部署约束下的领域专门化。
