PhysLoop: Harnessing LLMs' Physics Awareness for Self-Improving Video Generation Models

Published: 2026-11-11

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16748

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents PhysLoop, an LLM- and simulator-grounded framework for physically consistent video generation, localized repair, and training-data construction toward recursive self-improvement of video models.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on self-generated data, physical supervision, LLM-driven simulation, and video assessment/editing, PhysLoop uniquely unifies LLM planning, executable physics, iterative verification, and correction within one harness.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses the lack of scalable, controllable training videos and dependable quality signals for detecting physical errors—such as broken causality, object-count changes, and implausible interactions—in otherwise realistic generated videos.
4. 🛠️ Methods: A code agent converts requests into specifications and simulator scenes, executes them in UE, Taichi, or Genesis with synchronized visual and state observations, verifies task assertions before and after appearance refinement, and iteratively revises failures, producing 100K videos and the PhysLoop-Gen and PhysLoop-Repair datasets.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: PhysLoop achieves 85% generation success versus 73% for standalone Seedance 2.5 and 86% repair success versus 19.33% for the strongest standalone repair baseline, while improving human-rated plausibility and causality but reducing visual realism and scoring 52.06 versus 48.07 on Physics-IQ.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出 PhysLoop，一种基于大语言模型与物理模拟器的统一框架，用于生成物理一致的视频、修复局部错误及构建训练数据，并推动视频生成模型的递归式自我改进。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在自生成数据、物理监督、LLM 驱动仿真及视频评估与编辑等研究基础上，PhysLoop 的新思路是将 LLM 规划、可执行物理模拟、迭代验证与纠错统一到一个框架中。
3. ❓ 问题: 该工作旨在解决逼真视频仍可能存在因果关系断裂、物体数量变化和交互不符合物理规律等问题，同时缺乏可扩展、可控的训练视频及可靠纠错信号。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 代码智能体将请求转换为任务规范和模拟场景，在 UE、Taichi 或 Genesis 中执行并同步采集视觉与状态信息，在外观优化前后验证任务断言并根据失败证据迭代修改，最终发布 10 万条视频及 PhysLoop-Gen 和 PhysLoop-Repair 数据集。
5. 📊 结果与评估: PhysLoop 的生成成功率达到 85%，高于独立 Seedance 2.5 的 73%，修复成功率达到 86%，远高于最强独立修复基线的 19.33%，同时提升了人工评分的物理合理性与因果一致性，但降低了视觉真实性，并在 Physics-IQ 上以 52.06 对 48.07 领先。

SO-Arena: Benchmarking the Capability Spectrum and Reliability of System One Models

Published: 2026-11-10

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.15777

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper introduces SO-Arena, a benchmark for evaluating the capability breadth, inference latency, and decision reliability of single-pass System One models across semantic, reasoning, safety, evidence, and agentic domains. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on benchmarks such as JevBench, JEVal, and OpenDecisions, SO-Arena adds manually verified evidence-grounded labels, domain-relative difficulty tiers, interactive sandbox evaluation, and a separate reliability metric combining option-order stability with missing-answer recognition. 3. **❓ Problem:** Existing benchmarks rely heavily on aggregate accuracy and therefore offer limited insight into capability breadth, label trustworthiness, interaction competence, and whether decisions remain reliable when the available options change. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The authors curate 2,919 tasks from 40 sources into 11 domains and five capability families, combine static and environment-based tasks, compute an equal-domain capability score emphasizing difficult tiers, derive reliability from 12 option reorderings and null-option discrimination, and evaluate 21 models for capability and latency. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** System Two models lead overall while open-weight System One models answer in under 0.3 seconds per task, but capability and reliability rankings diverge, as JevAny-27B is most reliable despite mid-tier capability, order stability largely follows performance, null recognition varies widely, and gains from scaling or reasoning are strongly domain-dependent.

Actor, Judge, or Teacher? Understanding Decision Models in Long-Horizon Agents

Published: 2026-11-11

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17167

行动与判断获得的信息完全相同，因此两者的表现必然一致。
行动需要预判尚未出现的后果，而事后判断则能观察一个动作如何促成后续进展。
