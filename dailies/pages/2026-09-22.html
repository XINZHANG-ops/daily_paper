WorldCrafter: Consistent Video World Model with Implicit 3D-aware Memory

Published: 2026-09-20

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.24984

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on video world models for interactive exploration of dynamic environments, specifically in the domain of generative AI and computer vision, targeting long-horizon consistency and camera-controlled video generation.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on video diffusion transformers (DiT), memory mechanisms in video world models (context, spatial, and implicit memory), and 3D representation learning (e.g., LagerNVS). Its new idea is to use a camera-queryable implicit 3D-aware memory that compresses multi-view evidence into a fixed set of target view-specific tokens, allowing the requested viewpoint to shape how historical observations are integrated.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the problem of long-horizon consistency in video world models—specifically, maintaining coherent scene appearance and structure when users explore environments and revisit previously observed locations from different viewpoints over extended time horizons.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes WorldCrafter, which uses a memory encoder initialized from LagerNVS to map history latent frames into a compact 3D-aware representation, a pose-guided memory readout module that extracts target-view-specific tokens, max-coverage history retrieval, and joint training of the memory encoder with the video DiT. It also employs few-step distillation to achieve real-time streaming inference.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: WorldCrafter achieves a 47.6% improvement in revisit consistency over the strongest baseline (reducing LPIPS from 0.487 to 0.255 and increasing PSNR from 14.050 to 18.016 dB), achieves the lowest camera-control errors on all metrics, best visual quality scores on VBench (overall 81.910), and is 21.7× faster in memory processing compared to depth-based spatial memory methods.

2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于视频扩散变换器（DiT）、视频世界模型中的记忆机制（上下文记忆、空间记忆和隐式记忆）以及三维表示学习（如LagerNVS）。其新思路是使用相机可查询的隐式三维感知记忆，将多视图证据压缩为一组固定数量的目标视图特定token，使请求的视点决定历史观测的整合方式。

GameHorizon Suite: Multi-Horizon Data and Evaluation in Gameplay

Published: 2026-09-20

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25001

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper introduces the GameHorizon Suite, a unified data and evaluation framework for measuring AI models' gameplay capabilities across multiple temporal horizons (short, medium, long) in AAA video games, situated at the intersection of multimodal AI, vision-language models, and game agents.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior game datasets (e.g., MineDojo, VPT, STEVE-1, NitroGen, Open-P2P, GameVerse, WildWorld) and benchmarks (e.g., BALROG, VideoGameBench, GameWorld) but identifies three key gaps: narrow game scope, lack of comprehensive text instructions, and reliance on high-variance online rollouts. Its new idea is to create the first unified, reproducible yardstick that aligns videos, player actions, and multi-horizon natural-language instructions across diverse AAA games, applicable to both dedicated game agents and general-purpose VLMs.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the lack of a standardized, low-cost, and reproducible benchmark that can evaluate and compare both action-specialized game agents and planning-capable general-purpose VLMs/agents on multi-horizon gameplay tasks, while providing dense instruction annotations and fine-grained failure localization beyond simple success rates.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes three components: (1) GameHorizon-Annotator—an automated, bottom-up VLM-based pipeline using action-aware video segmentation, dynamic-programming-based temporal merging, and hierarchical instruction generation (L1/L2/L3); (2) GameHorizon-Data—5,000 hours of synchronized 2K video and keyboard-mouse recordings from 100 expert players across 21 AAA titles, annotated with 6.18 million multi-horizon instructions; and (3) GameHorizon-Bench—reproducible offline MCQ evaluation (5 primary/variant tasks) plus stepwise online causal/thematic task evaluation with environment resets for failure localization.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Through over one million model invocations across 47 models, the benchmark reveals a meaningful task-difficulty hierarchy (T1: 57.3%, T2: 65.1%, T3: 71.6%) and discriminates models from 44.6% to 80.2% accuracy, with GPT-6-Astra leading at 80.2%; dedicated game agents perform near random on unseen AAA games (22–28%) but achieve 54.2% on in-domain Minecraft; incorporating multi-horizon instructions improves future-action planning by 7.2 percentage points; and online success rates positively correlate with offline rankings, validating offline scores as a proxy for real gameplay ability.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文提出了 GameHorizon 套件，一个用于评估 AI 模型在多个时间尺度（短期、中期、长期）游戏能力的统一数据与评估框架，领域涉及多模态 AI、视觉语言模型与游戏智能体。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于先前游戏数据集（如 MineDojo、VPT、STEVE-1、NitroGen、Open-P2P、GameVerse、WildWorld）与基准（如 BALROG、VideoGameBench、GameWorld），但指出其三大局限：游戏覆盖范围窄、缺乏全面的文本指令、依赖高方差的在线评测。其新思路在于构建首个统一的、可复现的"标尺"，将视频、玩家动作与多层次自然语言指令在多样化 AAA 游戏上对齐，同时适用于专用游戏智能体和通用 VLM。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了现有游戏 AI 评估缺乏标准化、低成本、可复现基准的问题——既无法同时评估擅长动作执行的专用游戏智能体和擅长规划的通用 VLM/智能体，也缺乏密集的指令标注和细粒度的失败定位，单纯的成功率指标难以区分不同失败模式。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出三个核心组件：(1) GameHorizon-Annotator——基于 VLM 的自底向上自动标注流水线，结合动作感知视频分割、动态规划时序合并和分层指令生成（L1/L2/L3）；(2) GameHorizon-Data——100 位专家玩家在 21 款 AAA 游戏上录制的 5,000 小时同步 2K 视频与键鼠数据，标注 618 万条多层次指令；(3) GameHorizon-Bench——可复现的离线 MCQ 评测（5 个主要/变体任务），加上带环境重置和失败定位的逐步在线因果/主题任务评测。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 通过对 47 个模型超过 100 万次调用，基准揭示了合理的任务难度梯度（T1：57.3%、T2：65.1%、T3：71.6%），模型准确率从 44.6% 到 80.2% 显著区分，GPT-6-Astra 以 80.2% 居首；专用游戏智能体在未见过的 AAA 游戏上表现接近随机（22–28%），但在领域内 Minecraft 上达 54.2%；引入多层次指令将未来动作规划提升 7.2 个百分点；在线成功率与离线排名呈正相关，证明离线分数可有效反映真实游戏能力。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with the GameHorizon Suite paper, as it provides the broadest contribution—a unified benchmark that reveals how current AI models perform across temporal horizons in complex interactive environments, making it valuable regardless of your focus. RRSI is a natural second read if you're interested in agent self-improvement, since its regularization insights could apply to the game agents evaluated by GameHorizon. All three papers share a thread around long-horizon reasoning: WorldCrafter tackles temporal consistency in generated video worlds, GameHorizon measures multi-horizon gameplay planning, and RRSI addresses how agents can iteratively improve without overfitting—making them complementary reading for anyone studying temporal AI capabilities.
建议先阅读GameHorizon Suite这篇论文，因为它贡献最为广泛——提供了一个统一的基准测试，揭示了当前AI模型在复杂交互环境中跨时间维度的表现，无论你关注哪个方向都很有价值。如果你对智能体自我改进感兴趣，RRSI是顺理成章的第二选择，其正则化洞见可应用于GameHorizon所评估的游戏智能体。三篇论文都围绕长时程推理展开：WorldCrafter解决生成视频世界中的时序一致性，GameHorizon衡量多时程游戏规划能力，RRSI则关注智能体如何在不发生过拟合的情况下迭代改进——它们对研究时序AI能力具有互补价值。
