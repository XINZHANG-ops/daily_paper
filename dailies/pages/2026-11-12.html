Paper 1

World In-Context Learning for Agentic Robot Manipulation

Published: 2026-11-08

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.12885

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper introduces World In-Context Learning (WICL), a foundation-model-based paradigm for robust, generalizable, and cross-embodiment bimanual robot manipulation.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on in-context learning, trained VLA/WAM policies, and self-reflective coding agents, it proposes freezing model weights while converting one demonstration and simulator feedback into a readable semantic Task Memory that supports recursive improvement and cross-world transfer.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses the distribution coupling of weight-trained policies and the absence of environmental feedback in demonstration-only in-context learning, which cause brittleness under distribution shifts and accumulating execution errors.
4. 🛠️ Methods: RoboScript builds reusable skills, rehearses from one demonstration in simulation, revises and lints semantic memory under strict data isolation, freezes it for unseen-scene deployment with anticipation and recovery, and supports reflection across disjoint evaluation blocks without gradient updates.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: With one demonstration and no gradient updates, RoboScript achieves 83.6% clean and 83.2% randomized success, averaging 83.4% across all 50 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks versus 61.7% for the strongest trained policy, while also demonstrating positive reflection gains and 30/40 cross-embodiment real-robot successes.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文提出世界上下文学习（WICL）范式，研究基于基础模型的通用、鲁棒且可跨机器人形态迁移的双臂操作。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在上下文学习、训练式VLA/WAM策略和自我反思式编程智能体基础上，论文提出冻结模型权重，将一次示范与仿真反馈转化为可读语义任务记忆，从而支持递归改进和跨世界迁移。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文针对基于权重的策略与训练分布耦合，以及仅示范上下文学习缺乏环境反馈的问题，以解决分布偏移下的脆弱性和执行错误累积。
4. 🛠️ 方法: RoboScript构建可复用技能，从单次示范开始在仿真中排练，在严格数据隔离下修订并检查语义记忆，冻结记忆后以预期规划和失败恢复执行未见场景，并可跨互不重叠的评估块进行反思且无需梯度更新。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 仅用一次示范且不进行梯度更新时，RoboScript在RoboTwin 2.0全部50项任务上取得83.6%的干净场景成功率、83.2%的随机化成功率和83.4%的平均成功率，高于最强训练策略的61.7%，同时还展现出正向反思收益及30/40的跨形态真机成功。

MMDM: Measuring Massive Multitask Decision Making

Published: 2026-11-09

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.14309

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper introduces MMDM, a benchmark for evaluating text-based System-1 decision models across knowledge, reasoning, agentic, preference, and rubric-scoring tasks.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on Jev and prior decision benchmarks such as JEVal and DecisionBench, MMDM adds fresh or recomposed instances, long contexts, large option spaces, and unified single-select, multi-select, and soft-label evaluation.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses benchmark contamination and the inadequate joint testing of long-context understanding, large-scale option selection, logical dependencies, constraint awareness, complete multi-select recovery, and probability calibration in existing evaluations.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors construct and validate 5,448 questions across 16 categories using source-grounded rewrites, semantic recompositions, executable constructions, model screening, and human review, then evaluate 28 models with accuracy, SB-F, Brier error, normalized score MAE, ECE, and scaling analyses.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: WeMM-JEV 27B achieves the best single-select accuracy at 76.00%, but the best multi-select accuracy is only 36.81% and every model’s SB-F remains below 0.20, while category, calibration, and scaling results reveal persistent weaknesses in interdependent options, constraints, workflows, and large-option decisions.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文提出MMDM基准，用于评估文本型系统1决策模型在知识、推理、智能体、偏好及评分任务中的表现。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在Jev及JEVal、DecisionBench等既有决策基准的基础上，MMDM引入全新或重组样本、长上下文、大规模选项空间，以及统一的单选、多选和软标签评估。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文针对现有基准可能存在的测试污染，以及未能综合检验长上下文理解、大规模选项选择、逻辑依赖、约束意识、完整多选恢复和概率校准的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者通过基于来源的改写、语义重组、可执行构造、模型筛查和人工复核，建立并验证了16个类别的5,448道题，并使用准确率、SB-F、Brier误差、归一化评分MAE、ECE及扩展性分析评估了28个模型。
5. 📊 结果与评估: WeMM-JEV 27B以76.00%取得最高单选准确率，但最佳多选准确率仅为36.81%且所有模型的SB-F均低于0.20，而分类、校准和规模实验还揭示了模型在相互依赖选项、约束、工作流及大规模选项决策上的持续弱点。
