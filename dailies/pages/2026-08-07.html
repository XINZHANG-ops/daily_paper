AgentOPSD: Recursive Self-Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

Published: 2026-08-05

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05987

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on credit assignment in long-horizon agentic reinforcement learning (RL) for large language models, addressing sparse-reward multi-turn interactive environments such as embodied tasks, web shopping, and retrieval-augmented QA.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The work builds on group-relative policy optimization (GRPO) and on-policy self-distillation (OPSD, SDAR, RLSD, StepOPSD) for agentic RL. Its new idea is that turn-level credit should reflect how much a turn revises the accumulated belief of eventual success, rather than being measured by a local teacher–student gap in isolation, formalized as a recursive Bayesian belief update in log-odds space.
3. ❓ Problem: It tackles the misaligned credit assignment caused by broadcasting a trajectory-level advantage uniformly across all turns, which cannot distinguish pivotal decisions from routine or redundant actions in long-horizon agentic interactions, especially when token-level self-distillation signals are not aligned with environment turn boundaries.
4. 🛠️ Methods: AgentOPSD aggregates token-level teacher–student log-probability gaps into turn-level evidence, recursively maintains a Bayesian belief of success in log-odds space with geometric decay, computes marginal belief revision �Bₖ per turn, aligns it with the outcome sign, and applies bounded advantage reshaping on top of the standard GRPO clipped objective—requiring no extra rollouts or learned critic.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On ALFWorld, WebShop, and Search-QA with Qwen2.5-3B/7B, AgentOPSD reaches 89.1% success on ALFWorld (7B), consistently outperforms GRPO and strong self-distillation baselines across all benchmarks, exhibits the best horizon robustness (–0.54 success points per extra turn vs. –2.91 to –3.59 for competitors), and ablations confirm that turn-level aggregation, recursive belief revision, signed direction, and prior anchoring each contribute measurably.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于面向大语言模型的长程智能体强化学习中的信用分配问题，涉及具身任务、网购和检索增强问答等多轮交互环境中的稀疏奖励场景。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 研究基于组相对策略优化（GRPO）以及面向智能体RL的在线自蒸馏方法（OPSD、SDAR、RLSD、StepOPSD）。其新思路在于：轮级信用应反映该轮对累积成功信念的修正幅度，而非仅由局部的教师–学生差异决定，并将这一直觉形式化为对数赔率空间中的递归贝叶斯信念更新。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决长程智能体交互中，将轨迹级优势在所有轮次上均匀广播所导致的信用分配错位问题——该做法无法区分关键决策与常规或冗余动作，尤其是当基于token的自蒸馏信号与环境的轮边界并不对齐时。
4. 🛠️ 方法: AgentOPSD将token级的教师–学生对数概率差异聚合为轮级证据，在对数赔率空间中以几何衰减递归维护成功信念，计算每轮的边际信念修正∆Bₖ并与结果符号对齐，再通过有界优势重塑叠加到标准GRPO截断目标上，无需额外rollout或学习的评论家。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在ALFWorld、WebShop和Search-QA上使用Qwen2.5-3B/7B进行实验，AgentOPSD在ALFWorld（7B）上达到89.1%成功率，三个基准上均稳定优于GRPO及强自蒸馏基线，且在任务长度鲁棒性上最优（每增加一turn仅损失–0.54成功率，对比基线的–2.91至–3.59），消融实验验证轮级聚合、递归信念修正、符号方向和先验锚定各自均有可观测贡献。

Paper 2

Recursive Synthesis for Long-Horizon Terminal Tasks

Published: 2026-08-04

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05466

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on long-horizon terminal-agent training, specifically on synthesizing verified, executable terminal tasks at scale for LLM agent training in the domain of AI agents and reinforcement learning.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work in terminal-agent benchmarks (Terminal-Bench, SWE-Bench, Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench), synthetic environment generation (Endless Terminals, SETA, TermiGen), and self-training/recursive synthesis methods. Its new idea is a recursive verified synthesis framework (RST) that extends reference solutions first, then realigns the verifier and instruction, validates in a sandbox, and reuses accepted tasks as seeds across rounds to progressively grow task difficulty.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the prohibitive cost and scalability bottleneck of producing high-quality long-horizon terminal-agent training data, where each task requires keeping the instruction, environment, reference solution, and verifier mutually consistent, costing hundreds to thousands of dollars per task through human authoring.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes RST, a four-stage pipeline per round: (1) select a rewrite operator from 40 operators across 5 families; (2) extend the reference solution and update verifier, instruction, and environment to maintain consistency; (3) validate via static checks, anti-shortcut audits, and sandbox oracle execution; (4) apply diversity-capped selection (parent lineage, category, rewrite family caps) to seed the next round. The method also includes SFT on rejection-sampled Qwen3.5 trajectories and PPO-based agentic reinforcement learning on the synthesized tasks.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: RST produced 37,484 verified terminal tasks at ~$0.05 per task across 15 recursive rounds from 639 seeds, with stable validation yield (~78% pass rate) and no ceiling observed. Task difficulty grew substantially (median solution lines 67→374, commands 40→244, DeepSeek-V4-Pro pass@4 dropped 90%→2.5%). SFT improved Qwen3.5-27B and Qwen3.5-122B-A10B by up to 10 points on Terminal-Bench 2, Terminal-Bench Hard, and LHTB; PPO further boosted Qwen3.5-27B-RL to 49.44%, 32.00%, and 22.07%, corresponding to relative gains of 20.0%, 41.2%, and 21.9%.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于长视野终端智能体训练，具体研究在AI智能体与强化学习领域中如何大规模合成可验证的、可执行的终端任务。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于终端智能体基准（Terminal-Bench、SWE-Bench、Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench）、合成环境生成（Endless Terminals、SETA、TermiGen）以及自训练/递归合成方法的研究。其新思路是提出递归验证合成框架（RST），先扩展参考解答，再重新对齐验证器和指令，在沙盒中验证，并将已接受的任务作为后续轮次的种子，以递进式地提升任务难度。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了高质量长视野终端智能体训练数据生产成本高昂且难以扩展的问题——每个任务都需要保证指令、环境、参考解答和验证器之间的一致性，人工编写的成本高达每任务数百至数千美元。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了RST，每轮包含四个阶段的流水线：（1）从5个族中的40个算子中选择重写算子；（2）扩展参考解答并更新验证器、指令和环境以保持一致性；（3）通过静态检查、防捷径审计和沙盒预言机执行进行验证；（4）采用多样性约束选择（父代谱系、类别、重写族上限）以种子下一轮。方法还包括在拒绝采样的Qwen3.5轨迹上进行SFT，以及在合成任务上进行基于PPO的智能体强化学习。
5. 📊 结果与评估: RST在15轮递归合成中从639个种子生成了37,484个验证任务，每任务约0.05美元，验证产出稳定（约78%通过率）且未观察到上限。任务难度显著提升（中位解答行数67→374、命令数40→244、DeepSeek-V4-Pro pass@4从90%降至2.5%）。SFT使Qwen3.5-27B和Qwen3.5-122B-A10B在Terminal-Bench 2、Terminal-Bench Hard和LHTB上提升最多10分；PPO进一步将Qwen3.5-27B-RL提升至49.44%、32.00%和22.07%，相对提升分别为20.0%、41.2%和21.9%。

EnvACE: Internalizing Environment Dynamics via World Rehearsal for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

Published: 2026-08-05

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.06197

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on agentic reinforcement learning for large language model (LLM) agents, specifically addressing long-horizon tool use through a novel internal environment modeling paradigm.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on agentic RL with verifiable rewards (e.g., GRPO), learned world models, and LLM-based environment simulation; its new idea is "world rehearsal," in which a single shared policy simultaneously acts as both the agent and the environment to internalize action-response dynamics within its own parameters.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the high cost and difficulty of constructing/verifying real or synthesized executable environments and the inaccuracy of external LLM simulators, which make existing agentic RL training fundamentally dependent on external environment access.
4. 🛠️ Methods: EnvACE assigns two roles (acting and rehearsal) to a shared policy, which alternates between generating tool calls and self-rehearsing environment responses; both roles are jointly optimized end-to-end via role-wise GRPO with separate reward baselines, and the internalized world model also enables test-time scaling through private rehearsal.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于面向大语言模型（LLM）智能体的智能体强化学习，重点研究通过一种新颖的内部环境建模机制实现长程工具使用。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于带可验证奖励的智能体强化学习（如GRPO）、学习型世界模型以及基于LLM的环境模拟；其新思路是"世界预演"（world rehearsal），即让一个共享策略同时扮演智能体和环境两个角色，将动作-响应的动态关系内化到自身参数中。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了构建/验证真实或合成可执行环境的高成本与高难度问题，以及外部LLM模拟器响应不准确的问题，使现有智能体强化学习训练从根本上摆脱对外部环境的依赖。
4. 🛠️ 方法: EnvACE为单一共享策略分配两个角色（行动与预演），交替生成工具调用并自预演环境响应；两个角色通过带有独立奖励基线的角色级GRPO进行端到端联合优化，且内化的世界模型还支持测试时的私有预演以扩展推理能力。
5. 📊 结果与评估: EnvACE在BFCL-v4、τ²-Bench、VitaBench和FinMCP-Bench上取得32.91%的总体得分，超越环境扩展基线（如EnvScaler-8B、AWM-14B），在不同模型规模（1.7B和8B）上均表现稳定，并在适度预演预算下实现额外的测试时性能提升（总体最高达40.9%）。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with EnvACE (Paper 3), as its 'world rehearsal' paradigm—internalizing the environment within a single shared policy—is the most conceptually novel and paradigm-shifting of the three, and it elegantly sidesteps both the data-generation bottleneck and the credit-assignment challenges addressed by the other papers. Read RST (Paper 2) next for a practical complement: it tackles the complementary problem of cheaply producing long-horizon training tasks, which is exactly the kind of scalable data EnvACE-style methods need. AgentOPSD (Paper 1) is a focused algorithmic contribution on turn-level credit assignment that nicely rounds out the trilogy—together the three papers cover the 'where to train' (EnvACE), 'what to train on' (RST), and 'how to assign credit' (AgentOPSD) dimensions of long-horizon agentic RL.
