Paper 1

Vidu S2: Real-Time Interactive, Editable, and Spatial Video Generation

Published: 2026-09-09

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.11638

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on real-time interactive video generation and editing, specifically streaming digital-character animation and on-the-fly video stream editing, with extensions to spatial (stereoscopic) video for VR applications, in the domain of computer vision and generative AI.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The work builds on Vidu S1, Self-Forcing, Diffusion Forcing, DMD, and TurboDiffusion/TurboServe. The new ideas include Self-Replay Forcing (SRF) that enables cross-block gradient flow via re-noised replay, frame-aligned attention for streaming video editing, one-step latent super-resolution Refiner with asymmetric cache scheduling, and a VLM agentic system for dynamic reference interaction at any moment during streaming.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the limitation of existing offline, one-shot video generation paradigms that prevent real-time user interaction, and aims to achieve high-resolution (720p) real-time streaming video generation, editing, and spatial video synthesis with long-horizon stability, dynamic reference control, and strong instruction following.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The methods include bidirectional Image-to-Video and Reference-to-Video joint training, hybrid Teacher Forcing and Diffusion Forcing, Self-Replay Forcing for on-policy DMD training, a one-step super-resolution Refiner with asymmetric noise caching, frame-aligned attention for editing, a VLM agentic system with visual feedback, and an efficient inference stack combining SageAttention, SpargeAttention, Sparse-Linear Attention, W8A8 GEMM quantization, kernel fusion with CUDA Graphs, and Ulysses-style multi-GPU context parallelism.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Vidu S2-Avatar achieves the best score on all metrics of StreamAV-Bench (VA 0.687, VQ 3.370, SC 0.998, BC 0.993), and GSB human evaluations show 85.7–100% preference over Runway, PixVerse, and HeyGen; Vidu S2-Editing achieves the highest Overall on Sparkle-Bench (3.74), Joint Overall of 4.26 on OpenVE+RefVIE, and VFIDI of 9.95 on ViViD, significantly outperforming both offline and streaming baselines including Bernini-R 14B and Decart-Lucy2.5.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于实时交互式视频生成与编辑，具体涵盖流式数字人动画生成、视频流实时编辑以及面向VR的空间（立体）视频生成扩展，属于计算机视觉与生成式人工智能领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该工作基于Vidu S1、Self-Forcing、Diffusion Forcing、DMD以及TurboDiffusion/TurboServe；新思路包括通过重新加噪回放实现跨块梯度流动的Self-Replay Forcing (SRF)、用于流式视频编辑的帧对齐注意力机制、采用非对称缓存调度的单步潜在超分辨率Refiner，以及支持流式过程中任意时刻动态参考图更新的VLM智能体系统。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了现有离线、一次性视频生成范式无法支持实时用户交互的局限，旨在实现高分辨率（720p）实时流式视频生成、编辑与空间视频合成，并具备长时稳定性、动态参考控制以及强指令遵循能力。

ZGCM-1: A Fully Open and Extremely Efficient Foundation Model for Math and Agentic Search

Published: 2026-09-10

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.13356

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents ZGCM-1, a fully open 7B dense foundation model for mathematical reasoning and agentic search, situated in the AI/NLP domain of efficient large language model training.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds on prior work such as Muon optimizer, FP8 mixed-precision training (TWEO), Gemma 3's hybrid sliding-window/global attention, and Qwen2.5-1M's multi-stage context expansion, while introducing the novel thesis that compact models can overcome capacity limits by coupling deliberate internal reasoning with active external tool use.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses two key barriers: the scale barrier (frontier reasoning/agentic capabilities being locked behind hundred-billion-parameter systems) and the opacity barrier (most competitive models being released as open-weight rather than fully open-source).
4. 🛠️ Methods: The method employs an interleaved gated sliding-window/global attention architecture (5:1 ratio), FP8 + Muon + TWEO optimization stack, progressive curriculum mid-training with MDP reformulation (16K→64K→256K context), tiered SFT data quality pruning, mixed think/no-think co-training, GRPO-based reinforcement learning, and an AI-native R&D workflow where agents autonomously manage the development lifecycle.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: ZGCM-1-7B achieves 97.1% on MATH-500, 75.0% on AIME 2026, 70.4% on HMMT 2025, 63.1% on WebWalkerQA, and 62.0% on Binary Function Search—competitive with frontier models orders of magnitude larger—while delivering approximately 4.2× pre-training time-to-loss efficiency improvement over BF16/AdamW baselines.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文提出了ZGCM-1，一个完全开源的7B密集型基础模型，专注于数学推理和智能体搜索，属于AI/NLP领域中高效大语言模型训练的研究范畴。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该研究基于Muon优化器、FP8混合精度训练（TWEO）、Gemma 3的混合滑动窗口/全局注意力机制以及Qwen2.5-1M的多阶段上下文扩展等先前工作，并提出了新观点——紧凑模型可以通过结合深思熟虑的内部推理与主动的外部工具使用来克服参数容量限制。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了两个关键障碍：规模障碍（前沿推理和智能体能力被锁定在千亿参数系统中）和不透明障碍（大多数有竞争力的模型仅以开放权重而非完全开源形式发布）。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 采用的方法包括门控滑动窗口/全局注意力交错架构（5:1比例）、FP8 + Muon + TWEO优化栈、带有MDP重构的渐进式课程中期训练（16K→64K→256K上下文）、分层SFT数据质量剪枝、混合思考/非思考联合训练、基于GRPO的强化学习，以及由智能体自主管理整个开发生命周期的AI原生研发工作流。
5. 📊 结果与评估: ZGCM-1-7B在MATH-500上达到97.1%，AIME 2026上达到75.0%，HMMT 2025上达到70.4%，WebWalkerQA上达到63.1%，二进制函数搜索上达到62.0%——可与规模大数个数量级的前沿模型相媲美——同时相比BF16/AdamW基线实现了约4.2倍的预训练时间到损失效率提升。

Paper 3

RSIAgent: Autonomous Exploration for Recursive Self-improvement in New Environments

Published: 2026-09-13

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15364

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on digital agents and large language models, specifically proposing a training-free multi-agent framework for recursive self-improvement when adapting to new digital environments.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds on prior work in digital agents (e.g., OpenCUA, Cradle, OS-Copilot) and agent self-improvement (e.g., Darwin Gödel Machine, Voyager, EchoTrail-GUI); the new idea is a broad-then-deep exploration strategy that coordinates curriculum, actor, and verifier agents to autonomously construct reusable causal memory without updating model parameters.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses how a digital agent can autonomously adapt to new environments whose interfaces and tools are not captured by pretrained knowledge, without costly retraining or human supervision.
4. 🛠️ Methods: RSIAgent uses a three-agent (curriculum, actor, verifier) harness with a two-stage exploration strategy: Broad Recursive Self-exploration (BRS) for parallel diverse discovery and Deep Recursive Self-exploration (DRS) for sequential refinement of hard cases, producing frozen reusable memory for test-time reuse.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On OSWorld-v2 and Agents' Last Exam, RSIAgent enables open-source models (Kimi-K3, GLM-5.3) to reach 78.98% and 84.82% partial scores, outperforming frontier closed-source models like GPT-6 Astra (72.60%, 82.26%) and Claude Opus 5 without updating model parameters.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于数字智能体与大语言模型领域，提出了一种无需训练的、多智能体协同的递归自我改进框架，用于在新数字环境中实现自主探索与适应。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该工作建立在现有数字智能体研究（如OpenCUA、Cradle、OS-Copilot）以及智能体自我改进方法（如Darwin Gödel Machine、Voyager、EchoTrail-GUI）的基础上；其创新点在于提出"先广后深"的探索策略，通过课程、智能体执行者与验证者三者的协同，在不更新模型参数的情况下自主构建可复用的因果记忆。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了数字智能体如何在接口、工具及失败模式未被预训练知识完全覆盖的新环境中实现自主适应的问题，避免代价高昂的再训练或人工监督。
4. 🛠️ 方法: RSIAgent采用由课程智能体、执行智能体和验证智能体组成的三智能体框架，并实施两阶段探索策略：广度递归自探索（BRS）用于并行多样化发现，深度递归自探索（DRS）用于对困难案例进行序贯精炼，最终生成冻结的可复用记忆用于测试时复用。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在OSWorld-v2和Agents' Last Exam基准上，RSIAgent使开源模型（Kimi-K3、GLM-5.3）分别达到78.98%和84.82%的部分得分，在不更新模型参数的前提下超越了GPT-6 Astra（72.60%、82.26%）及Claude Opus 5等前沿闭源模型。
