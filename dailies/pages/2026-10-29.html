Multiscale Policy Optimization for Long-Horizon LLM Agents

Published: 2026-10-25

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35013

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies reinforcement-learning post-training of long-horizon, multi-turn LLM agents for search, tool use, and automation, emphasizing optimization across assistant-turn and token scales. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on sequence-level methods such as GRPO/GSPO and token-level methods such as PPO/VAPO/SAO, it proposes Multiscale Policy Optimization (MPO), motivated by the observation that critic-value variation is dominated by turn boundaries. 3. **❓ Problem:** Existing methods either assign one sequence-level advantage that cannot distinguish turns or tokens, or propagate credit and importance ratios token by token, thereby misrepresenting complete environment interactions as macro actions and introducing additional within-turn distribution-shift error. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** MPO treats each assistant turn as a macro action, combines turn-level GAE with a mean-preserving local token correction, and uses complete-turn importance sampling with stop-gradient ratios to retain token-specific credit in its clipped policy update. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Across 4B and 30B-A3B models on search, tool-use, and automation benchmarks, MPO achieved the best score on all nine metrics, while token-versus-turn importance-weighting and parameter ablations supported both components, although each ablation configuration used only one training run.

ChronoReplay: One Answer Interface Is Not Enough for Streaming Spatial Intelligence

Published: 2026-10-24

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.34283

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies cross-interface spatial reasoning in streaming egocentric video with multimodal large language models, covering categorical, metric, and timestamp-causal queries over one causal history.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Whereas prior work primarily adds streaming-memory architectures or post-trains one answer interface, ChronoReplay identifies “interface lock-in” and combines native-format, family-balanced learning, chronological causal-prefix replay, and query-adaptive temporal frame selection.
3. ❓ Problem: Optimizing one spatial readout can improve its benchmark while weakening or failing to transfer to other readouts, while detaching metric or temporal labels from their valid chronological prefixes makes the supervision ambiguous.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The method post-trains Qwen3-VL-8B on 33,180 single-turn preservation examples and 6,381 ordered trajectory episodes containing 45,006 supervised turns, preserves native targets, binds each target to its legal causal prefix, balances preservation and trajectory risks with α=0.5, and applies adaptive temporal sampling at inference.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Under matched fixed observation access, one checkpoint improves OVO-S/OST/UCS from 48.98/50.52/45.33 to 53.89/63.93/52.77, respectively, chronological replay exceeds cyclic shifting by 2.41 OST points, and adaptive access raises OVO-S to 57.98 using 80.5 frames instead of 128.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究多模态大语言模型在第一人称视频流中的跨接口空间推理，涵盖同一因果历史上的分类、度量与时间戳因果查询。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 既有工作主要引入流式记忆架构或针对单一答案接口进行后训练，而ChronoReplay识别出“接口锁定”现象，并结合原生格式的任务族平衡学习、按时间顺序的因果前缀回放及查询自适应的时序帧选择。
3. ❓ 问题: 针对一种空间读出进行优化可能提升本任务却削弱或无法迁移至其他读出，同时将度量或时间标签脱离其有效时间前缀会造成监督歧义。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该方法在33,180个单轮保持样本和6,381个有序轨迹片段（共45,006个监督轮次）上微调Qwen3-VL-8B，保留原生目标、将每个目标绑定至合法因果前缀、以α=0.5平衡保持与轨迹风险，并在推理阶段采用自适应时序采样。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在匹配且固定的观测访问条件下，同一检查点将OVO-S/OST/UCS分别从48.98/50.52/45.33提升至53.89/63.93/52.77，按时间顺序回放比循环移位高2.41个OST百分点，而自适应访问以80.5帧而非128帧将OVO-S提升至57.98。

DTCA: Denoising Token Credit Assignment for Reinforcement Learning in Diffusion Language Models

Published: 2026-10-25

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.35201

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies reinforcement learning for masked diffusion language models, focusing on token-level credit assignment for mathematical reasoning.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on RLDF’s denoising-state optimization and OPEFO’s entropy-flow calibration, DTCA introduces POS-aggregated confidence gains from token commitments to guide both timestep selection and token credit allocation.
3. ❓ Problem: Iterative parallel denoising prevents sequence-level rewards from revealing which committed tokens drive subsequent refinement, while simultaneous commitments and rollout variability make direct token attribution noisy and expensive.
4. 🛠️ Methods: DTCA estimates token contributions through offline individual-token forward passes, aggregates confidence gains by POS into fixed weights in [0.8, 1.2], selects timesteps using POS-weighted uncertainty and 70% score-mass coverage, normalizes token credits, and combines entropy-change balancing, response-level credit restoration, and k3-KL-regularized policy optimization.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On LLaDA-8B-Instruct, DTCA achieves 39.8/43.2/44.0 on MATH500 and 80.6/85.3/85.4 on GSM8K at budgets 128/256/512, outperforming the compared baselines with ablations supporting both credit modules, although validation is limited to mathematics on one architecture.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究掩码扩散语言模型中的强化学习，重点关注面向数学推理的词元级信用分配。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在RLDF的去噪状态优化和OPEFO的熵流校准基础上，DTCA提出利用按词性聚合的词元提交置信度增益，同时指导去噪时间步选择与词元信用分配。
3. ❓ 问题: 迭代式并行去噪使序列级奖励难以揭示哪些已提交词元促成了后续精炼，而同一步中的多词元交互和采样间波动又使直接归因昂贵且噪声较大。
4. 🛠️ 方法: DTCA通过离线逐词元前向传播估计提交贡献，将置信度增益按词性聚合并映射为[0.8, 1.2]的固定权重，以词性加权不确定性选择累计覆盖70%分数质量且数量为2–32的时间步并归一化词元信用，再结合熵变化平衡、响应级信用恢复和k3-KL正则化策略优化。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在LLaDA-8B-Instruct上，DTCA在128/256/512预算下于MATH500达到39.8/43.2/44.0、于GSM8K达到80.6/85.3/85.4并优于所比较基线，消融实验支持两个信用模块，但其验证仍局限于单一架构上的数学任务。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read the Multiscale Policy Optimization paper first: its turn-level macro-action framing and local token correction directly address long-horizon agent credit assignment, with strong gains across nine search, tool-use, and automation metrics, though the single-run ablations warrant caution.
