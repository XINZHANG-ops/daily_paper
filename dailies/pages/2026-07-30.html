TurboVLA: Real-Time Vision-Language-Action Model at 32 Hz on an RTX 4090 with <1 GB VRAM

Published: 2026-07-28

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.27205

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for language-conditioned robotic manipulation, focusing on efficient real-time inference for resource-constrained deployment.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on LLM-centric VLA models (RT-2, OpenVLA, π0/π0.5, CogVLA) that follow a V→L→A pathway, and efficient VLA designs like SmolVLA, Evo-1, and VLA-Adapter, the new idea replaces the large language model core with a direct V+L→A paradigm using lightweight bidirectional vision-language interaction inspired by Grounding DINO.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the substantial computational and memory overhead imposed by LLM-centric VLA models, which limits real-time robotic execution due to high inference latency (limiting control frequency) and large VRAM requirements, making deployment on consumer-grade hardware impractical.
4. 🛠️ Methods: TurboVLA uses DINOv3 as the vision encoder, BERT as a lightweight text encoder, a bidirectional vision-language interaction module with stacked cross-attention layers, and an ACT-style transformer decoder that predicts continuous action chunks in parallel via L1 behavior cloning loss, removing the LLM from the execution pathway.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Achieves 97.7% average success on LIBERO (matching π0.5's 96.9% with only 6% of parameters), 60.2% on RoboTwin 2.0 (outperforming π0.5's 57.0%), 31.2ms inference latency (32 Hz), 0.9 GB VRAM, only 0.2B parameters, and outperforms π0.5 on real-world AgileX Piper tasks across four language-conditioned manipulation scenarios.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 面向语言条件机器人操作的视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型，聚焦于高效实时推理及资源受限设备部署。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于以LLM为核心的VLA模型（RT-2、OpenVLA、π0/π0.5、CogVLA等）的V→L→A范式，以及SmolVLA、Evo-1、VLA-Adapter等高效VLA设计，新思路是借鉴Grounding DINO的双向跨模态交互方式，提出直接V+L→A范式，用轻量级的视觉-语言直接交互取代大语言模型的核心地位。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了以LLM为核心的VLA模型所带来的巨大计算和内存开销问题，这些开销导致推理延迟高（限制控制频率）、显存需求大，使得在消费级硬件上部署用于实时机器人控制变得不切实际。
4. 🛠️ 方法: TurboVLA使用DINOv3作为视觉编码器、BERT作为轻量文本编码器，采用带堆叠跨注意力层的双向视觉-语言交互模块，以及ACT风格的Transformer解码器通过L1行为克隆损失并行预测连续动作块，完全将LLM从执行通路中移除。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在LIBERO上达到97.7%的平均成功率（匹敌π0.5的96.9%，但参数量仅为其6%），在RoboTwin 2.0上达到60.2%（优于π0.5的57.0%），推理延迟31.2ms（32 Hz），显存仅0.9 GB，参数量仅0.2B，并在AgileX Piper真实机器人四项语言条件操作任务中均超越π0.5。

HumanCLAW: Can Vision-Language Models Act Through a Body?

Published: 2026-07-28

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.27180

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper investigates the "action intelligence" of vision-language models (VLMs), specifically whether they can act through a physical body, situated at the intersection of embodied AI, multimodal reasoning, and humanoid control.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on prior embodied environments (VirtualHome, ALFRED, Habitat), end-to-end VLA models (RT-2, OpenVLA), and controllable motion generation (MoMask); the new idea is HumanCLAW, a framework that decouples high-level decision-making from low-level motor execution while keeping the body acting freely in a physics-grounded world.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the difficulty of distinguishing whether embodied task failures stem from a VLM's bad action choice or from motor execution errors (e.g., losing balance), since most existing benchmarks either abstract away physical motion or entangle decisions with embodiment-specific control.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes a closed-loop framework combining a frozen VLM harness with hierarchical reasoning (high-to-mid-to-low), a plug-and-play skill-conditioned motion generator (base DiT + per-skill ControlNet adapters), a half-physics simulator preserving contact/gravity but removing balance failures, and a 1,218-episode find-navigate-interact benchmark evaluating nine frontier VLMs.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across nine VLMs, none solves the benchmark—the best (Gemini-3.1) achieves only 16.8% success—with the bottleneck identified as "embodied self-awareness" (knowing where the body is, whether it has arrived, or whether it collided) rather than perception, which is largely intact once a target renders in view.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究视觉-语言模型（VLM）的"动作智能"，即其能否通过物理身体执行动作，跨居于具身AI、多模态推理与人形机器人控制领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于先前的具身环境（VirtualHome、ALFRED、Habitat）、端到端VLA模型（RT-2、OpenVLA）及可控运动生成（MoMask）等研究；新思路是提出HumanCLAW框架，将高层决策与底层运动执行解耦，同时让身体在物理世界中自由行动。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了具身任务失败时难以区分是VLM决策错误还是运动执行问题（如失去平衡）的难题，因为现有基准要么抽象掉物理运动，要么将决策与特定机器人执行纠缠在一起。
4. 📊 结果与评估: 在九个VLM中无一能解决该基准——最佳模型（Gemini-3.1）成功率仅16.8%——瓶颈被识别为"具身自我意识"（知道身体位置、是否到达目标、是否发生碰撞），而非感知能力（在目标渲染入视野后感知基本完好）。

Paper 3

CodeNib: A Multi-View Data System for Serving Repository Context to Coding Agents

Published: 2026-07-27

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.25431

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents CodeNib, a multi-view data system for serving repository context to coding agents, situated at the intersection of software engineering, AI-driven code agents, and data management systems.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on materialized views, code intelligence (LSP, LSIF, SCIP, Glean, Zoekt, Sourcegraph), lexical/dense/structural retrieval, and agent interfaces (OpenHands, SWE-agent, Serena). Its new idea is to treat repository context as a commit-indexed data plane with independent lexical, dense, and structural views that share repository-relative source addresses while preserving explicit, operation-specific validity boundaries instead of collapsing different retrieval/navigation/maintenance operations into one abstraction.
3. ❓ Problem: Coding agents repeatedly search, navigate, and retain context from evolving repositories, but disconnected indexes, language servers, and task-local histories force repeated per-task discovery and obscure build, update, query, and delivery costs across the repository-context lifecycle.
4. 🛠️ Methods: CodeNib (1) compiles and materializes lexical, dense, and structural views per commit, linking them via a manifest; (2) maintains them incrementally through Git/LSP-assisted graph repair and content-addressed vector reuse, with offline output equivalence checks against independent rebuilds; (3) exposes a runtime that serves ranked retrieval plans, static/live symbol navigation through one location interface, and bounded context policies (grep/read, eager injection, eager+compact history rewrite) via stdio MCP and bound skills.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across 100 snapshots, graph and vector updates achieve median 8.67× and 25.44× speedups on transitions matching independent rebuilds (15/33 and 28/31 source-changing transitions). Static navigation reproduces live LSP normalized locations on 632/1,000 requests with a median 4.72× per-request live/static latency ratio. Selected context policies across five models use 50–87% fewer trajectory tokens than paired grep/read while preserving the −0.05 localization margin.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文介绍 CodeNib，一个用于为编码智能体提供代码仓库上下文的多视图数据系统，处于软件工程、AI 编码智能体与数据管理系统的交叉领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本文基于物化视图、代码智能技术（LSP、LSIF、SCIP、Glean、Zoekt、Sourcegraph）、词法/向量/结构化检索以及智能体接口（OpenHands、SWE-agent、Serena）等研究。其新思路是将仓库上下文视为以 commit 索引的数据平面，将相互独立的词法、向量与结构视图通过仓库相对源码地址对齐，并在显式、操作特定的合法性边界下分别服务于检索、导航与维护，而非将不同操作合并为单一抽象。
3. ❓ 问题: 编码智能体需要在持续演化的代码仓库中反复搜索、导航并保留上下文，但分散的索引、语言服务器和任务局部历史导致重复发现，并掩盖了仓库上下文全生命周期（构建、更新、查询、交付）的成本。
4. 🛠️ 方法: CodeNib（1）通过视图编译器为每个 commit 物化词法、向量与结构视图，并通过清单（manifest）链接；（2）通过 Git/LSP 辅助的图修复与基于内容寻址的向量复用进行增量维护，并以独立重建做离线的输出等价性校验；（3）在运行时通过统一的位置接口提供排序检索方案、静态/动态符号导航，并通过 stdio MCP 与绑定技能支持有界上下文策略（grep/read、eager 注入、eager+compact 一次性历史重写）。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在 100 个快照上，图与向量更新在与独立重建匹配的转换上达到中位数 8.67× 和 25.44× 的加速（分别为 33 次中 15 次和 31 次中 28 次源变化转换）。静态导航在 1,000 次请求中的 632 次上复现了实时 LSP 的归一化位置，且在这些匹配请求上中位数 live/static 延迟比为 4.72×。在五个模型上，所选上下文策略在维持 −0.05 定位质量边际的同时，使用的轨迹 token 比配对的 grep/read 少 50%–87%。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read Paper 2 (HumanCLAW) first—it provides a striking diagnostic of why current VLMs fail at embodied tasks, pinpointing 'embodied self-awareness' as the real bottleneck rather than perception. This framing naturally motivates Paper 1 (TurboVLA), which sidesteps LLM-centric VLA limitations with a lean V+L→A architecture, showing that decoupling language reasoning from action execution can still match or beat larger models. Paper 3 (CodeNib) is thematically adjacent in spirit—efficient, structured context serving—but independent in domain, best read as a complement if you're broadly interested in agent infrastructure.
建议先阅读第二篇（HumanCLAW），它清晰地诊断了当前 VLM 在具身任务中失败的根本原因——不是感知，而是"具身自我意识"的缺失。这一洞见自然引出第一篇（TurboVLA）：通过精简的 V+L→A 架构绕开 LLM 中心化 VLA 的瓶颈，在参数大幅减少的同时仍可媲美或超越更大模型。第三篇（CodeNib）在主题上较为独立，关注的是面向编码代理的高效上下文服务，但如果你对智能体基础设施的整体设计感兴趣，可作为补充阅读。
