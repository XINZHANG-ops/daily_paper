PDRVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Physical Dynamics Representations

Published: 2026-10-26

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.37381

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper addresses robot manipulation and embodied AI by developing a vision-language-action model with compact representations of physical dynamics.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on VLA models and video world models, it proposes a lightweight Physical Dynamics Module trained through direct future RGB/depth/optical-flow prediction and frozen world-model supervision, avoiding video generation during deployment.
3. ❓ Problem: Existing VLA policies have limited ability to anticipate physical scene changes, while incorporating online video prediction into the control loop creates substantial latency.
4. 🛠️ Methods: PDRVLA extracts 50 physical tokens from layer-8 VLM features, conditions a diffusion-transformer action expert on these tokens and final VLM features, and jointly trains cross-embodiment policies using public, synthesized, subtask-annotated, and ECoT-annotated data.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: PDRVLA achieves 99.3%, 89.9%, 93.6%, and 81.5% success on LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, RoboTwin 2.0, and SimplerEnv respectively; world-model supervision reduces held-out future-feature MSE by 1.32%, whereas subtask and object-pose supervision provide only 0.30- and 0.19-point gains, and inference-time ECoT generation shows no consistent benefit.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文聚焦机器人操作与具身智能，提出了一种使用紧凑物理动力学表征的视觉-语言-动作模型。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在VLA模型和视频世界模型的基础上，作者提出轻量级物理动力学模块，通过直接预测未来RGB、深度和光流特征并结合冻结世界模型监督进行训练，从而避免部署阶段生成视频。
3. ❓ 问题: 现有VLA策略难以预判物理场景变化，而将在线视频预测引入控制回路又会带来显著延迟。
4. 🛠️ 方法: PDRVLA从VLM第8层特征中提取50个物理令牌，将它们与最终层VLM特征共同用于条件化扩散Transformer动作专家，并利用公开、合成、子任务标注和ECoT标注的跨本体数据联合训练策略。
5. 📊 结果与评估: PDRVLA在LIBERO、LIBERO-Plus、RoboTwin 2.0和SimplerEnv上分别达到99.3%、89.9%、93.6%和81.5%的成功率；世界模型监督使留出集未来特征MSE降低1.32%，而子任务与物体位姿监督仅带来0.30和0.19个百分点的提升，推理时生成ECoT也未表现出稳定收益。

Paper 2

Hy-Nomas: Towards Free-form Interactive World Generation

Published: 2026-10-26

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36729

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: Hy-Nomas is an 8B-parameter video-based world model for free-form interactive world generation, supporting multi-turn text instructions and continuous camera control through autoregressive segment-wise video synthesis.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on the HunyuanVideo 1.5 diffusion transformer, Self Forcing-style causal rollout, and prior world models such as Genie 3 and HappyOyster, the paper introduces action-aware causal attention that keeps descriptive context visible across segments while confining action instructions to their originating time windows, combined with continuous relative camera-pose cross-attention and a VLM Prompt Enhancer.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper tackles the difficulty of executing free-form text and continuous camera commands within one continuous session while preserving history, since naive causal masking causes within-segment action misalignment and cross-segment action repetition and autoregressive errors compound into appearance drift and scene breakdown.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The approach combines structured JSON video captions with subject identifiers and timestamped actions, segment-wise and action-aware causal attention with a KV cache, frame-aligned camera cross-attention using relative extrinsics, a five-stage curriculum spanning latent adaptation, causal pre-training, high-quality annealing, segment forcing on self-generated histories, and causal diffusion distillation to four NFEs, plus PE-based inference, LoRA adaptation, and Ulysses/FSDP2 infrastructure supporting 360K-token sequences.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: On the 280-case Hy-Nomas-Bench, human evaluation shows strong wins on multi-turn prompt following (65%–75%) and interleaved control (75% prompt following, 73.3% physical plausibility) but weaker spatial memory than Genie 3 (13.3% win, 30% loss), while the distilled model runs at four NFEs per segment and generates a 100-second 720p rollout on a single RTX 4090 with a 20.53 GiB peak memory footprint.
1. 📘 主题与领域: Hy-Nomas 是一个 8B 参数的基于视频的交互式世界模型，通过分段自回归视频合成支持多轮自由文本指令与连续相机控制。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文以 HunyuanVideo 1.5 的扩散 Transformer、Self Forcing 式因果自回归以及 Genie 3、HappyOyster 等已有世界模型为基础，提出"动作感知因果注意力"，使描述性上下文可跨段共享而动作指令仅在其原始时间窗口内生效，并结合连续相对位姿相机交叉注意力与 VLM 提示增强器。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文致力于解决在单一连续会话中同时执行自由文本与连续相机指令并保持历史一致性的难题，因为简单因果掩码会导致段内动作错序与跨段动作重复，且自回归误差会累积为外观漂移与场景崩坏。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 方法结合了含主体标识与时间戳动作的结构化 JSON 视频字幕、分段与动作感知因果注意力及 KV 缓存、基于相对外参的帧对齐相机交叉注意力、涵盖潜空间适配、因果预训练、高质量退火、基于自生成历史的分段强制与因果扩散蒸馏（降至 4 次 NFE）的五阶段课程训练，以及 PE 推理、LoRA 适配和支持 360K token 序列的 Ulysses/FSDP2 训练基础设施。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在包含 280 个用例的 Hy-Nomas-Bench 上，人工评测显示多轮文本跟随（胜率 65%–75%）与交错控制（提示跟随 75%、物理合理性 73.3%）表现突出，但空间记忆弱于 Genie 3（胜率 13.3%、败率 30%），蒸馏后模型每段仅需 4 次 NFE，并可在单张 RTX 4090 上以 20.53 GiB 峰值显存生成 100 秒 720p 视频。

Paper 3

The Trinity:Capability-Driven Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents

Published: 2026-10-25

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36077

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper presents The Trinity, a capability-driven recursive self-improvement framework for multimodal agents that co-evolves verifiable environments and modular agent harnesses.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on recursive harness self-improvement, agent–environment co-evolution, curriculum learning, and agent-as-a-router research, it replaces task-specific or monolithic harnesses with a shared capability tree, selectively inherited specialized harnesses, and capability-aware routing.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses the high cost of constructing heterogeneous verifiable environments and the poor scalability of manually maintained harnesses, which can overfit individual tasks or inefficiently reconcile conflicting requirements across diverse tasks.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The framework induces a capability tree from verified trajectories, uses Creator to generate simplified and screened simulation worlds through Capability-Driven Real2Sim2Real, uses Explorer to evolve a selectively inherited module-based Harness tree, consolidates validated Creator updates into a shared Base Harness, and routes tasks using public information and verified experience.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across 28 training and 18 unseen benchmarks, the final Harness raises mean scores from 39.06 to 73.16 and from 37.45 to 63.61, capability-aware routing approaches oracle performance at 71.27/60.96 versus 73.16/63.61, and evolved configurations achieve up to 80% simulated and 60% real-robot success.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文提出 The Trinity，一种面向多模态智能体的能力驱动递归自改进框架，通过共同演化可验证环境与模块化智能体 Harness 来提升能力。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 在递归 Harness 自改进、智能体—环境协同进化、课程学习和 Agent-as-a-Router 等工作的基础上，论文不再按具体任务或单一全局配置组织 Harness，而是构建共享能力树、选择性继承的专用 Harness 以及能力感知路由机制。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决异构可验证环境构建成本高昂以及人工迭代 Harness 无法随任务规模和复杂度扩展的问题，因为任务特定 Harness 容易过拟合，而全局 Harness 又难以兼顾相互冲突的任务需求。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该方法从已验证轨迹中归纳能力树，由 Creator 通过能力驱动的 Real2Sim2Real 生成并筛选简化仿真世界，由 Explorer 演化带选择性继承的模块化 Harness 树，将 Creator 的有效更新整合为共享 Base Harness，并依据公开任务信息和验证经验进行能力感知路由。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在28个训练基准和18个未见基准上，最终 Harness 将平均分分别从39.06和37.45提升至73.16和63.61，能力感知路由取得71.27/60.96、接近Oracle的73.16/63.61，演化后的配置在仿真和真实机器人任务中最高分别达到80%和60%的成功率。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read PDRVLA first: it offers a focused, practically validated approach to giving robot policies compact physical-dynamics representations while avoiding the latency of online video prediction. Together, the papers form a progression from latent dynamics for control (PDRVLA), to explicit interactive video world generation (Hy-Nomas), to recursively improving agents and their environments (The Trinity).
建议优先阅读 PDRVLA：它以紧凑的物理动力学表征增强机器人策略，同时避免在线视频预测带来的延迟，方法集中且实验结果扎实。三篇论文可视为递进关系：PDRVLA 面向控制中的隐式动力学，Hy-Nomas 探索显式交互式世界生成，The Trinity 则研究智能体与可验证环境的递归协同进化。
