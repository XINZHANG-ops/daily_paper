Paper 1

AI4AI at Test-Time: Strong-to-Weak Capability Transfer via Harnesses

Published: 2026-08-11

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12307

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper lies in the domain of large language model capability transfer, specifically studying test-time scaffolding (inference-time harnesses) to help weaker models perform better without retraining, with a focus on Theory-of-Mind (ToM) reasoning benchmarks.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work in model distillation (knowledge distillation, on-policy distillation, instruction tuning, RLHF) and inference-time reasoning methods (chain-of-thought, self-consistency, least-to-most prompting, tool use, programmatic reasoning). The new idea is "strong-to-weak scaffolding"—transferring capability at test time by having a strong builder model construct reusable inference-time harnesses (prompts, routing, deterministic solvers, format enforcement) for a weaker target model, rather than updating the target's parameters.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses whether strong-to-weak capability transfer can occur at test time without any parameter updates to the weaker target model, specifically investigating when, why, and how inference-time harnesses improve weaker model performance, and what design principles make them effective.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The method involves a strong builder model iteratively constructing and refining an inference-time scaffold using only a 5% validation set across four ToM benchmarks (BigToM, Hi-ToM, MMToM-QA, MuMA-ToM), where the builder can implement any combination of task routing, prompt templates, deterministic solvers, few-shot exemplars, verification passes, and format enforcement. The final scaffold is evaluated on a hidden test set without builder intervention, across multiple builder models, platforms (Cursor, Claude Code, GPT Codex), and target models (GPT-5.4-mini, Gemini-3.5-flash).
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: The best scaffold raised GPT-5.4-mini's macro-average accuracy from 0.488 to 0.912 (+0.423 uplift, 86.7% relative), with 100% of runs exceeding the baseline. Gains come primarily from offloading unstable reasoning into deterministic code, benchmark-specific routing, and strict answer-format enforcement rather than from encouraging more target-model reasoning. Key findings include: (1) weaker targets benefit most (headroom law, r=0.75), (2) builder reasoning effort improves scaffolds monotonically (Spearman r=0.77), (3) platform effects are second-order relative to builder identity, (4) top scaffolds are near-Pareto-improving (fix 83% of baseline errors while breaking only 7%), and (5) residual errors concentrate in harder regimes like deep belief recursion and Bayesian goal inference where tasks resist compilation into rules.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文属于大语言模型能力迁移领域，具体研究测试时支架（推理时外壳）如何在不重新训练的情况下帮助较弱模型表现更好，重点关注心理理论（ToM）推理基准测试。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文建立在模型蒸馏（知识蒸馏、在线策略蒸馏、指令微调、RLHF）和推理时推理方法（思维链、自我一致性、最少到最多提示、工具使用、程序化推理）的基础上。新思路是"强弱支架"——通过让强构建模型为弱目标模型构建可重用的推理时外壳（提示、路由、确定性求解器、格式强制）来在测试时迁移能力，而非更新目标模型的参数。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决的问题是强弱能力迁移能否在测试时无需对较弱目标模型进行任何参数更新的情况下发生，具体研究推理时外壳在何时、为何以及如何改善较弱模型的性能，以及使其有效的设计原则是什么。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 方法涉及一个强构建模型仅使用5%验证集在四个ToM基准（BigToM、Hi-ToM、MMToM-QA、MuMA-ToM）上迭代构建和精炼推理时支架，其中构建器可以实现任务路由、提示模板、确定性求解器、少样本示例、验证步骤和格式强制的任意组合。最终支架在没有构建器干预的情况下在隐藏测试集上评估，跨多个构建模型、平台（Cursor、Claude Code、GPT Codex）和目标模型（GPT-5.4-mini、Gemini-3.5-flash）进行实验。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 最佳支架将GPT-5.4-mini的宏平均准确率从0.488提升至0.912（提升+0.423，相对86.7%），100%的运行超过基线。增益主要来自于将不稳定推理卸载到确定性代码、特定基准的路由以及严格的答案格式强制，而非鼓励目标模型进行更多推理。关键发现包括：（1）较弱目标受益最大（提升空间定律，r=0.75），（2）构建器推理努力单调提升支架质量（Spearman r=0.77），（3）平台效应相对于构建器身份是次要因素，（4）顶级支架近乎Pareto改进（修复83%的基线错误，仅破坏7%），（5）剩余错误集中在较难情境中，如深度信念递归和贝叶斯目标推断，在这些情况下任务难以被编译为规则。

Paper 2

Spark-to-Paper: End-to-End Research Paper Generation as a Composable Skill

Published: 2026-08-11

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11924

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on autonomous end-to-end research paper generation, situated at the intersection of natural language processing, LLM-based autonomous agents, and scientific writing automation.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds on prior autonomous research systems (AI Scientist/v2, AutoResearchClaw, Kosmos/Robin, Agent Laboratory, CycleResearcher, ARS) and lightweight skill-based writing assistants (Idea2Story, GPT Researcher), and introduces the new idea of implementing paper generation as thirteen composable skills inside an existing coding assistant (Claude Code), eliminating the need for a separate agent platform or orchestration infrastructure.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the gap between systems that automate the full research pipeline but require heavy standing infrastructure, and those that stay lightweight but only handle a narrow slice of the process; specifically, it tackles fabrication, citation hallucination, lack of experiment grounding, inconsistent long-horizon claims, and the production of non-editable bitmap figures.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The system uses thirteen composable skills organized into eight stages (Input Routing, Planning, Citation, Writing, Refinement, Review, Figure Generation, Assembly, Experiment Execution), separating model-based judgment from deterministic operations, combining deterministic integrity gates with Self-Review and Adversarial Review, employing pre-committed experiment design with evidence-guided claim revision, bounding a "Self-Refutation Loop" with a cap of seven cycles, and producing editable vector figures via programmatic plotting or HTML-based code reconstruction.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across eight controlled research topics, Spark-to-Paper achieves 99.5% citation validity and 96.4% figure editability; an ablation shows fabrication detection rising from 14% (single-pass draft) to 92% (full integrity and review stack) with 74% adversarial-review precision, at an average cost of 11.9M tokens, $8.1 per manuscript, and 3.2 hours wall-clock time.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于端到端的自动化科研论文生成，隶属于自然语言处理、基于大语言模型的自主智能体以及科研写作自动化这一交叉领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于此前自主科研系统（AI Scientist/v2、AutoResearchClaw、Kosmos/Robin、Agent Laboratory、CycleResearcher、ARS）以及轻量级技能化写作助手（Idea2Story、GPT Researcher）的研究，并提出新思路：在现有编程助手（Claude Code）内部以十三个可组合技能实现论文生成，无需独立的智能体平台或编排基础设施。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决现有系统中"自动化全流程但需重型基础设施"与"保持轻量但仅覆盖局部流程"之间的差距，具体应对引用捏造、文献幻觉、实验缺乏支撑、长链路主张不一致以及生成不可编辑位图图形等问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 系统采用十三个可组合技能，按八个阶段组织（输入路由、规划、引用、写作、精炼、评审、图形生成、装配、实验执行），将模型判断与确定性操作分离，结合确定性完整性闸门与自评审、对抗性评审，采用预先承诺的实验设计与基于证据的主张修订，将"自反驳循环"上限设为七轮，并通过程序化绘图或基于 HTML 的代码重建生成可编辑矢量图形。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在八个受控研究主题上，Spark-to-Paper 达到 99.5% 引用有效率与 96.4% 图形可编辑率；消融实验显示虚假主张检测率从单次生成的 14% 提升至完整堆栈的 92%，对抗性评审精度为 74%，平均消耗 1190 万 token、单篇成本 8.1 美元、平均耗时 3.2 小时。

Paper 3

On-Policy Self-Distillation without Any Supervision

Published: 2026-08-08

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.06296

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on unsupervised on-policy self-distillation for large language models, specifically applied to mathematical reasoning tasks.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on on-policy distillation (OPD) and on-policy self-distillation (OPSD), which still depend on external supervision like ground-truth solutions or stronger teacher models. The new idea is to replace ground-truth supervision with the model's own majority-vote consensus over multiple sampled rollouts, enabling genuine unsupervised self-distillation.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the dependency of existing on-policy self-distillation methods on external supervision (ground-truth labels, environmental feedback, or larger teacher models), which limits scalability to unlabeled problems and applicability where supervision is costly or unavailable.
4. 🛠️ Methods: U-OPSD samples multiple rollouts from the model, uses majority voting under a self-consistency threshold to construct a pseudo-solution, conditions the teacher distribution on this pseudo-solution, and distills it into the student on the disagreeing completions—correcting the model precisely where it is confidently wrong.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: U-OPSD improves over base models by 8.5% (4B) and 10.7% (8B) on math reasoning benchmarks in non-thinking mode, outperforms supervised OPSD by 3.2% and 2.3%, and matches or surpasses GRPO, all without using any ground-truth labels.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于大语言模型的无监督在线自蒸馏方法，应用于数学推理任务。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于在线蒸馏（OPD）和在线自蒸馏（OPSD）研究，这些方法仍依赖外部监督（如真实标签或更强的教师模型）。新思路是用模型自身多次采样的多数投票共识替代真实监督，从而实现真正的无监督自蒸馏。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了现有在线自蒸馏方法对外部监督（真实答案、环境反馈或更大模型）的依赖问题，这种依赖限制了在无标签问题上的可扩展性以及在监督成本高昂或不可用领域的应用。
4. 🛠️ 方法: U-OPSD 首先对每个问题采样多个输出，通过自一致性阈值下的多数投票构建伪解，将教师分布条件化于该伪解，并在与共识不一致的输出上对学生进行蒸馏，从而精确纠正模型自信但错误的判断。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在非思考模式下，U-OPSD 使基础模型在数学推理基准上分别提升 8.5%（4B）和 10.7%（8B），比有监督的 OPSD 高出 3.2% 和 2.3%，并在不使用任何真实标签的情况下与 GRPO 持平或超越。
