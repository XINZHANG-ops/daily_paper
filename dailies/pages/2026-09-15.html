Atria Dawn: The Dawn of Agentic Superintelligence

Published: 2026-09-13

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15818

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper is in artificial intelligence, specifically focusing on agentic language models and human-AI collaboration in AI research and development (R&D).
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds on prior work in LLM tool use (e.g., Toolformer, SWE-bench), automated AI research (e.g., AlphaEvolve, AI co-scientist), and agent benchmarks (e.g., BFCL, MLE-bench), while introducing the novel "Verifiable Experience Pipeline" for training and a case study of human-AI collaboration during real model development.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses how to train an effective agentic foundation model for real-world research and engineering tasks, and how to understand the evolving division of labor between humans and AI agents in recursive self-improvement of AI.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors train a 744-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model using a Verifiable Experience Pipeline that links tasks, tool-mediated trajectories, and externally verified outcomes, and analyze 769 task records from 56 participants alongside agent logs.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Atria Dawn Preview achieves the highest reported score on 5 of 16 benchmarks; participants rated 33.2% of completed AI-assisted tasks as infeasible without AI, humans made the final decision in 85.5% of method/parameter choices while AI proposed 55.4%, and the daily median agent actions per human prompt rose from 11.0 to 28.5.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文属于人工智能领域，专注于智能体语言模型（agentic language models）以及AI研发中人机协作的演变。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于LLM工具使用（如Toolformer、SWE-bench）、自动化AI研究（如AlphaEvolve、AI联合科学家）以及智能体基准（如BFCL、MLE-bench）等先前工作，创新性地提出"可验证经验流水线"（Verifiable Experience Pipeline）训练方法，并通过真实模型开发过程进行人机协作的案例研究。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决两个问题：如何训练面向真实科研与工程任务的智能体基础模型，以及如何理解AI研发中人与AI智能体之间不断演变的分工，特别是面向递归自我改进的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者使用744亿参数的混合专家（MoE）模型，通过将任务、工具调用轨迹与外部验证结果相连接的"可验证经验流水线"进行训练，并结合56名参与者的769条任务记录与智能体日志进行分析。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Atria Dawn Preview在16个基准中5个取得最高报告分数；参与者认为33.2%的已完成AI辅助任务在无AI时不可行；在方法/参数决策中人类做出最终决策占85.5%，AI提出方案占55.4%；每条人类提示对应的智能体动作数中位数从11.0增至28.5。

Dream-RSI: Recursive Self-Improvement through Evolving Worlds

Published: 2026-09-13

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.14858

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) for AI agents in long-horizon scientific and algorithmic discovery, spanning algorithm engineering, mathematical optimization, and GPU kernel engineering. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** The paper builds on model-based reinforcement learning and World Models (Ha & Schmidhuber, Dreamer by Hafner et al.) and AI-driven discovery systems (AlphaEvolve, SimpleTES, OpenEvolve, EvoX); its new idea is to treat accumulated discovery history as a "replay simulator" that enables fast, low-cost off-policy evaluation of exploration policies without rerunning the actual discovery agent. 3. **❓ Problem:** The paper addresses the bottleneck that existing exploration strategies in RSI are either fixed (cannot adapt to scaling search spaces) or require expensive online meta-optimization with delayed, costly feedback over long horizons. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** Dream-RSI employs a three-stage closed loop: (1) Online Exploration with an executable exploration policy controlling branching, parallelism, and stopping; (2) Construction of a replay simulator pool from the recorded discovery tree; and (3) Dreaming-based Policy Improvement, where candidate policies are evaluated cheaply via replay and the best is redeployed online. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Across 8 tasks in 3 domains, Dream-RSI matched or outperformed strong baselines (e.g., 162× fewer agent calls than SimpleTES on Lasso, 50× budget savings on math optimization, and 1.79×–2.43× fewer generations or up to 2.09× higher kernel performance on KernelBench) while reducing discovery cost significantly.

PhysBrain 1.5: From Vision-Language Models to Physical Foundation Models

Published: 2026-09-13

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.14973

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper introduces PhysBrain 1.5, a unified embodied foundation model spanning physical AI, vision-language models, and robotics, targeting understanding, action generation, and future-state prediction within physical environments.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Built on Qwen3-VL-Instruct and prior embodied models (e.g., PhysBrain 1.0, HY-Embodied, UniVLA, Cosmos), it proposes the new idea of unifying language, end-effector motion, and visual states as discrete tokens under a single autoregressive next-token prediction objective across the physical loop.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses how to jointly learn heterogeneous supervision signals (semantic understanding, motion trajectories, and dense visual predictions) from diverse embodiments and data sources within a single physical foundation model.
4. 🛠️ Methods: It extends the VLM vocabulary with ActionPiece action tokens and VQ-VAE visual tokens, trains autoregressively with masked next-token prediction in two stages (physical-aware pre-training on human videos and embodied SFT on human/robot/simulated data), and conditions action generation on recent action history for embodiment-specific continuation.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: PhysBrain 1.5 (8B) achieves an average score of 72.5 across 28 embodied benchmarks, setting a new open-source SOTA, ranking first on 14 benchmarks, approaching GPT-6-Astra (73.3) and Gemini 3.6 Flash (73.0), while also demonstrating action trajectory and future RGB/depth/mask prediction.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文介绍了PhysBrain 1.5，一个统一的具身基础模型，横跨物理AI、视觉语言模型和机器人领域，旨在实现物理环境中的理解、动作生成与未来状态预测。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于Qwen3-VL-Instruct和先前的具身模型（如PhysBrain 1.0、HY-Embodied、UniVLA、Cosmos），提出在物理循环中将语言、末端执行器运动和视觉状态统一为离散令牌、通过单一自回归下一令牌预测目标联合学习的新思路。
3. ❓ 问题: 解决了如何在单个物理基础模型中联合学习来自不同本体和数据源的异质监督信号（语义理解、运动轨迹和密集视觉预测）的难题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 通过ActionPiece动作令牌和VQ-VAE视觉令牌扩展VLM词汇表，采用掩码下一令牌预测进行两阶段自回归训练（在人类视频上进行物理感知预训练，在人类/机器人/模拟数据上进行具身SFT），并以近期动作历史为条件进行本体特定的动作延续生成。
5. 📊 结果与评估: PhysBrain 1.5（8B）在28个具身基准测试中取得72.5的平均分，创下新的开源SOTA，在14个基准测试中排名第一，接近GPT-6-Astra（73.3）和Gemini 3.6 Flash（73.0），同时展示了动作轨迹预测以及未来RGB/深度/掩码预测能力。
