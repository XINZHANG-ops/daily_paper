CoworkOS:Scaling Agent Coworkers for Real-world Automation

Published: 2026-11-02

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03178

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents CoworkOS, a general-purpose AI-agent harness for long-horizon, human-in-the-loop automation of real-world productivity work. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on agent workflows, assistants, and multi-agent cowork systems, CoworkOS newly separates model-driven task strategy from runtime-managed execution through hierarchical agents, shared editable artifacts, and persistent state. 3. **❓ Problem:** It addresses the difficulty of completing complex productivity tasks while simultaneously sustaining long-term monitoring, decision-making, context continuity, and human collaboration across sessions and interruptions. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The system combines a Main Agent, Coordinator Runtime, and Subagents with synchronous or asynchronous delegation, native and MCP application integrations, revision-aware shared resources, hierarchical workspaces, semantic memory, checkpointing, and persistent Jobs. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Across 12 benchmarks and 24 cases in six application categories, CoworkOS is competitive with OpenHands, Codex, and DeepSeek Harness, while five rounds of automated post-training raise Qwen3.6-35B-A3B’s eight-benchmark average from 33.19 to 42.95.

CUA-Master: Scaling Computer-use Agents through Open-World Recursive Improvements

Published: 2026-11-02

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03126

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper introduces CUA-Master, a framework for scaling computer-use agents through open-world recursive data construction and model optimization in GUI and hybrid GUI–CLI environments.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Unlike unidirectional data pipelines and closed-world self-improvement methods, it proposes a nested double-loop flywheel combining external instructional-video knowledge, model-gap-driven exploration, controllable task synthesis, and resilient trajectory collection.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses insufficient diversity, controllability, and reliability of interaction data, as well as the lack of iterative feedback, limited ability expansion, and diminishing returns in existing recursive training approaches.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The framework mines instructional videos into seed tasks and trajectories through visual-semantic reconstruction, converts workflows into reusable primitives for verifiable task composition, performs feedback-guided snapshot-rollback retries, and trains through iterative SFT followed by verifier-based online RL.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: CUA-Master-27B scores 80.2 on OSWorld Verified, 36.9 on OSWorld 2.0, 43.9 on WeaveBench, and 25.0 full-pass on Agents’ Last Exam, while outperforming UI-Mate-27B on all four benchmarks and demonstrating strong efficiency, rollout yield, and cross-platform generalization.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文提出 CUA-Master，通过开放世界递归数据构建与模型优化，在 GUI 及 GUI–CLI 混合环境中扩展计算机使用智能体。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 不同于单向数据流水线和依赖自生成经验的闭环自我改进，该工作提出嵌套双环飞轮，将外部教学视频知识、模型缺口驱动的探索、可控任务合成与可靠轨迹收集结合起来。
3. ❓ 问题: 该工作解决交互数据多样性、可控性和可靠性不足，以及传统训练缺乏迭代反馈、自生成式递归改进扩展能力有限且收益递减的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 该框架通过视觉—语义双重路径从教学视频中挖掘种子任务与轨迹，将工作流抽象为可复用原语以合成可验证任务，利用反馈引导的快照回滚机制收集轨迹，并依次采用迭代式监督微调和基于验证器的在线强化学习。
5. 📊 结果与评估: CUA-Master-27B 在 OSWorld Verified、OSWorld 2.0、WeaveBench 和 Agents’ Last Exam 上分别取得 80.2、36.9、43.9 和 25.0 的全通过率，同时在四项基准上优于同规模 UI-Mate-27B，并展现出较高的执行效率、轨迹产出率及跨平台泛化能力。

Reviso: Not All Visual Evidence Deserves Equal Attention in Video Captioning

Published: 2026-11-02

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.03602

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: Reviso is an agentic video-captioning framework that applies priority-guided active visual perception to multimodal large language models. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Whereas prior captioning and video-agent methods mainly improve representations, rewards, or query-driven temporal inspection, Reviso newly learns video-specific observation priorities and tool-selection strategies for open-ended captioning. 3. **❓ Problem:** Existing models produce detailed captions that may overemphasize minor details while omitting key events and context, and they struggle to identify what to inspect, which tool to use, and when to adapt their observations. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** Reviso generates a Context–Focus–Points plan from a low-frame-rate preview, performs iterative high-frame-rate revisits, cropping, and OCR, and learns this behavior through verified-trajectory SFT followed by GRPO with key-fact, plan-alignment, and unproductive-tool-call rewards. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Reviso outperforms the evaluated open-source baselines across four captioning benchmarks, improves its backbone’s F1 by 4.4 points on DREAM-1K and 15.4 on MCTS-VCB, and raises caption-based QA accuracy by 4.9 points on LVBench and 2.1 on MMVU, with ablations confirming the contributions of active tools and all three rewards.
