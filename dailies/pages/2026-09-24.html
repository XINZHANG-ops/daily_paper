Paper 1

RULER: Instance-aware Rubric Rewards for SVG Generation

Published: 2026-09-20

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25270

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on open-ended Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG) code generation from natural-language instructions, situated at the intersection of visual code generation, multimodal large language models, and reinforcement learning.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds on prior work in SVG generation (StarVector, OmniSVG, IconShop), reinforcement learning for visual content (rendering-aware RL, GRPO), and rubric-based evaluation/rewards (LLM-Rubric, RaR, universal rubric scoring). Its new idea is to introduce *instance-aware* rubric rewards—rubrics that are dynamically generated per instruction from text alone—and to repurpose them as fine-grained, multi-axis reward signals for RL, avoiding the reward hacking that plagues scalar metrics like CLIP and Aesthetic.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses two coupled problems in open-ended SVG generation: (1) unreliable evaluation metrics (CLIP, Aesthetic, HPS) that transfer poorly to stylized vector content and fail to align with human judgments, and (2) unfaithful supervision signals for training, where scalar rewards cause reward hacking and supervised fine-tuning overfits to dataset templates.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes RULER, which (i) empirically validates rubric-based scoring via a human alignment study, (ii) generates an instance-aware six-item rubric per instruction using a frontier model (Claude-Opus-4.6 or GPT-5.5) along three axes—semantic fidelity, visual quality, and rendering style, (iii) scores rendered SVG rollouts with a judge VLM (Qwen3-VL-8B) item-by-item, and (iv) optimizes the policy using Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with the weighted per-item satisfactions as the reward.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: RULER lifts the rubric score from 0.432/0.395 (Qwen3-8B baseline) to 0.693/0.683 on MMSVG-Illustration and MMSVG-Icon, surpassing dedicated SVG specialists (IconShop, OmniSVG, JanusCoder) and matching the much larger DeepSeek-V3. Blinded human preference shows win rates above 50% against all baselines (53.3%–96.5%), and the method generalizes across base model scales (4B/8B) and rubric generators.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文聚焦于从自然语言指令生成开放式的可缩放矢量图形（SVG）代码，研究领域涵盖视觉代码生成、多模态大语言模型以及强化学习。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 本文基于SVG生成（StarVector、OmniSVG、IconShop）、视觉内容强化学习（rendering-aware RL、GRPO）以及基于评分标准的评估/奖励（LLM-Rubric、RaR、通用评分标准）方面的研究。其新思路在于引入*实例感知*的评分标准奖励——仅从文本为每条指令动态生成评分标准——并将其重新用作强化学习的细粒度、多维度奖励信号，从而避免CLIP、Aesthetic等标量度量所引发的奖励黑客问题。
3. ❓ 问题: 本文解决开放式SVG生成中两个相互关联的问题：（1）评估指标不可靠（CLIP、Aesthetic、HPS等标量度量难以迁移到风格化矢量内容且与人类判断不一致）；（2）训练监督信号失真，即标量奖励导致奖励黑客行为，且监督微调会过拟合到数据集模板。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 本文提出了RULER方法，包括：（i）通过人类对齐研究实证验证基于评分标准的评分方式；（ii）利用前沿模型（Claude-Opus-4.6或GPT-5.5）为每条指令沿三个维度（语义保真度、视觉质量、渲染风格）生成包含六个项目的实例感知评分标准；（iii）使用评判VLM（Qwen3-VL-8B）逐项对渲染的SVG输出进行评分；（iv）利用Group Relative Policy Optimization（GRPO），以加权逐项满意度作为奖励来优化策略。
5. 📊 结果与评估: RULER在MMSVG-Illustration和MMSVG-Icon上将评分标准分数从0.432/0.395（Qwen3-8B基线）提升至0.693/0.683，超越了专门的SVG模型（IconShop、OmniSVG、JanusCoder），并与规模更大的DeepSeek-V3相当。盲测人类偏好显示对所有基线的胜率均超过50%（53.3%–96.5%），且该方法在不同基模型规模（4B/8B）和评分标准生成器下均具有泛化能力。

Paper 2

Transferring the Intelligence of VLMs to Robotic Control

Published: 2026-09-18

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.22966

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on robotics and embodied AI, specifically transferring the general-purpose intelligence of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to physical-world robotic control without task-specific robot training.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds upon prior Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and World-Action Models (WAMs) that require costly, embodiment-specific robot datasets, proposing instead a new paradigm of "intelligence transfer" using a lightweight semantic action interface and few-shot in-context demonstrations to unlock pretrained VLM capabilities for robotic control.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses whether pretrained VLM capabilities—primarily acquired in the digital world—can be directly transferred to physical robotic control, and how to avoid the degradation of general-purpose reasoning caused by large-scale robot data fine-tuning while achieving strong manipulation performance.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper proposes RoboDawn, featuring (1) a human-intuitive semantic action interface with discrete translation, rotation, and gripper commands, (2) a closed-loop VLM reasoning mechanism with execution feedback and memory, and (3) a two-level in-context learning scheme combining a command primer with task-level demonstrations to ground the VLM in interface usage and task-solving strategies.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: RoboDawn achieves state-of-the-art results on RoboTwin 2.0 C2R (53.2% zero-shot, 73.6% one-shot, surpassing π0.5 and HarnessVLA) and RoboDojo (35.67% zero-shot, 47.17% one-shot), and successfully transfers to real-world Franka robots for block-in-basket (9/10) and block stacking (5/10), all without task-specific parameter updates.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于机器人与具身智能领域，研究如何在不进行任务特定机器人训练的情况下，将视觉语言模型（VLM）的通用智能迁移到物理世界的机器人控制中。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于以往的视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型与世界-动作模型（WAM），这些方法需要昂贵且具身特定的机器人数据集；本文则提出了一种"智能迁移"的新范式，通过轻量级的语义动作接口和少量上下文示例，无需参数更新即可释放预训练VLM的机器人控制能力。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决的核心问题是：主要在数字世界中获得的预训练VLM能力能否直接迁移到物理机器人控制，以及如何在避免大规模机器人数据微调导致通用推理能力退化的同时，实现强大的操作性能。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文提出了RoboDawn框架，包含（1）由离散平移、旋转和夹爪命令组成的人类直观语义动作接口；（2）具有执行反馈和记忆的闭环VLM推理机制；（3）结合命令入门示例和任务级示例的两级上下文学习方案，使VLM能够理解接口用法和任务解决策略。
5. 📊 结果与评估: RoboDawn在RoboTwin 2.0 C2R上取得领先结果（零样本53.2%，单样本73.6%，超越π0.5和HarnessVLA），在RoboDojo上表现优异（零样本35.67%，单样本47.17%），并在真实Franka机器人上成功迁移（积木入篮9/10，积木堆叠5/10），且全程无需任务特定的参数更新。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Start with RoboDawn — its 'intelligence transfer via in-context learning' paradigm, which sidesteps task-specific robot fine-tuning, is conceptually striking and contrasts sharply with the Grounded Action Model (GAM), which tackles a similar robotics foundation-model problem via a fundamentally different route (frozen 3D grounding + flow matching). Reading the two robotics papers together highlights a clean methodological dichotomy between parameter-free in-context adaptation and parametric adaptation, both benchmarking against π0.5 and RoboTwin 2.0. RULER sits in an unrelated domain but is a strong follow-up if you're interested in rubric-based RL for visual code generation.
