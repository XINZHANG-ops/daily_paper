Published: 2026-11-18

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.26409

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper presents WeMM-Video, a Chinese-English bilingual family of open-source omni-modal foundation models for unified text, image, audio, and video understanding and reasoning. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on video-SALMONN 2 and Qwen3.5/Qwen3.6 backbones, the paper introduces hybrid instruct/thinking modes, timestamped audio-video token interleaving, and a unified pipeline for large-scale omni-modal training and specialized reasoning. 3. **❓ Problem:** It addresses the weak fine-grained perception, cross-modal temporal alignment, long-context reasoning, hallucination resistance, and repetitive generation of existing open-source models on complex real-world videos. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The models combine native visual encoders with a Whisper-based encoder and 0.1-second window Q-Former, then use three-stage progressive pre-training on 1.2T tokens followed by multi-teacher OPD, anti-repetition MrDPO, and specialist RL. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Across more than 120 benchmarks, the 35B-A3B model establishes a new open-source state of the art, beats Qwen3.5-Omni-Flash and Seed2.0-Mini-0428 on 16 of 17 general audio-visual benchmarks, approaches Gemini-3.0-Flash, and the 9B model surpasses the larger Qwen3-Omni-30B-A3B-Instruct while retaining strong audio, silent-video, image, and text abilities.

Coda-DaVinci: Reimagining Video Generation with Code2Video

Published: 2026-11-18

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.26595

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: Coda-DaVinci is a task-adaptive agentic Code2Video framework spanning video generation, coding agents, 3D proxy modeling, physics simulation, and controllable visual synthesis.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on direct video generation and Code2Video systems such as VideoCoCo and Code Video Model, it derives task-dependent policies for proxy granularity, physics simulation, and proxy-grounded prompting, then integrates them with adaptive routing, persistent scene assets, and multi-agent visual review.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses weak instruction following, cross-revisit scene and state consistency, physical plausibility, and natural articulated motion in video generation, while mitigating the risk that inaccurate or overly detailed 3D proxies degrade downstream quality.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors conduct controlled blinded evaluations on 94 samples comparing three proxy granularities, two physics settings, and three prompting strategies, then implement a harness that routes tasks between direct and 3D-guided generation, models environments and objects at medium granularity and humans coarsely, grounds prompts in inspected proxies, and repairs outputs through independent visual acceptance.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Coda-DaVinci achieves the best VTR checklist score of 91.42 and WER of 11.08, leads all R2M metrics, ranks second on physics behind Claude+Blender, and leads Code2Video methods in T2AV-Compass realism, while improving long-video structure and consistency at the cost of transition stability and setting adherence, with Blender providing better quality than Three.js at roughly twice the token cost.
1. 📘 主题与领域: Coda-DaVinci 是一个任务自适应智能体式 Code2Video 框架，涵盖视频生成、编程智能体、三维代理建模、物理模拟和可控视觉合成。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该研究在直接视频生成以及 VideoCoCo、Code Video Model 等 Code2Video 系统基础上，系统总结代理粒度、物理模拟和代理对齐提示的任务依赖规律，并将其与自适应路由、持久化场景资产和多智能体视觉审查相整合。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决视频生成在复杂指令遵循、镜头回访时的场景与状态一致性、物理合理性及自然关节运动方面的不足，同时降低错误或过度精细的三维代理损害最终视频质量的风险。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者在94个样本上开展受控盲评，比较三种代理粒度、两种物理设置和三种提示策略，并构建一个在直接生成与三维引导生成间路由、以中等粒度建模环境和物体、以粗粒度建模人物、依据代理检查结果生成提示并通过独立视觉验收修复视频的执行框架。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Coda-DaVinci 取得最佳 VTR 清单得分91.42和最低 WER 11.08，在全部 R2M 指标上领先，物理评测以60.77位列 Claude+Blender 之后，并在 T2AV-Compass 真实感上领先其他 Code2Video 方法，同时改善长视频结构与一致性但牺牲转场稳定性和场景设定遵循度，且 Blender 相比 Three.js 质量更高但令牌成本约为两倍。

Paper 3

HiUni: Harmonizing Understanding and Generation in Shared Visual Space

Published: 2026-11-18

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.26107

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper studies unified multimodal modeling for image understanding, generation, and editing through a shared visual representation space.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Unlike prior systems that separate VAE and ViT representations or use task-specific Mixture-of-Transformers backbones, HiUni proposes extending a pretrained VLM by sharing its language-aligned ViT and dense backbone while restricting task-specific computation to output modules.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses how to unify understanding and generation without task interference or separate backbones while overcoming unstable pixel-space optimization and the loss of fine spatial details caused by semantic visual abstraction and patch merging.
4. 🛠️ Methods: HiUni initializes from Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct, adds a simplified Pixel Diffusion Head with timestep conditioning and a lightweight HiGate that injects raw pixel residuals, and jointly optimizes language modeling and pixel-space diffusion through staged warmup, pretraining, and fine-tuning.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: HiUni preserves nearly all pretrained understanding ability with a 75.56 average versus 75.61 for Qwen3-VL-4B, achieves the highest Chinese Qwen-Image-Bench score among unified models at 47.98, ranks second among unified models in editing, and shows that shared dense backbones are viable but involve a generation-performance trade-off versus task-routed backbones.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文研究统一多模态模型，通过共享视觉表示空间统一支持图像理解、生成与编辑。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 不同于以往分离 VAE 与 ViT 表征或采用任务专属混合专家 Transformer 主干的方法，HiUni 提出复用预训练视觉语言模型的语义对齐 ViT 和稠密主干，并将任务特定计算限制在输出端。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在无需任务专属主干或严重任务干扰地统一理解与生成，同时解决像素空间优化不稳定以及语义抽象和补丁合并导致细粒度空间信息丢失的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: HiUni 基于 Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct 初始化，引入简化且具备时间步条件控制的像素扩散头与向扩散头注入原始像素残差的轻量级 HiGate，并通过分阶段预热、预训练和微调联合优化语言建模与像素空间扩散。
5. 📊 结果与评估: HiUni 以 75.56 的平均分基本保持 Qwen3-VL-4B 的 75.61 理解能力，在统一模型中取得最高的中文 Qwen-Image-Bench 分数 47.98，并在图像编辑中位列统一模型第二，同时证明共享稠密主干可行但相较任务路由主干存在生成性能权衡。

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

Read WeMM-Video first: it presents the broadest systems contribution and the strongest empirical case for unified omni-modal perception and reasoning, with open-source models, explicit temporal alignment, and state-of-the-art results across more than 120 benchmarks. Then read HiUni for its related investigation of whether a shared dense visual backbone can support understanding and generation without task interference, followed by Coda-DaVinci as a complementary study of controllability, physical plausibility, and consistency in video synthesis.
建议优先阅读 WeMM-Video：它以开源模型、显式时序对齐和120余项基准上的领先结果，系统展示了统一全模态感知与推理的能力。随后可读 HiUni，探讨共享稠密视觉骨干能否兼顾理解与生成且减少任务干扰；再读 Coda-DaVinci，从可控生成、物理合理性与一致性角度补充视频合成方向。
