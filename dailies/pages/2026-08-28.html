Paper 1

VGI-Bench: Probing Visual Intelligence in Video Generation Models

Published: 2026-08-25

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.19583

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on benchmarking visual reasoning capabilities of video generation models in the computer vision and generative AI domain.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds on prior video benchmarks like PhysGenBench, WorldSimBench, TiVi-Bench, V-ReasonBench, and VBVR-Bench, while introducing VGI-Bench's novel emphasis on photorealistic inputs, process-sensitive task design, and calibrated difficulty.
3. ❓ Problem: Existing video reasoning benchmarks suffer from visual domain mismatch (using abstract/line-art inputs), limited demand for rollout-based reasoning, and uncontrolled task difficulty that makes failures less diagnostic.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper constructs a two-level taxonomy (4 domains and 7 skill tags) with 27 tasks and 810 instances, uses human-in-the-loop image generation for photorealistic inputs, and employs a VLM-as-judge with dual metrics (Completeness × Rubric Score) to evaluate both goal completion and process validity.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Even the strongest model Seedance 2.0 achieves only 51.0% overall, with Structured Puzzles being the hardest domain and Topology/Temporal the weakest skills; analysis reveals high input sensitivity, bounded transfer from synthetic fine-tuning, and limited self-correction (below 1%) during denoising.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本文聚焦于计算机视觉与生成式AI领域中对视频生成模型视觉推理能力的基准评估。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于PhysGenBench、WorldSimBench、TiVi-Bench、V-ReasonBench和VBVR-Bench等先前视频基准研究，提出VGI-Bench在真实风格输入、过程敏感任务设计和难度校准三方面的新颖设计。
3. ❓ 问题: 现有视频推理基准存在视觉域不匹配（使用抽象线稿输入）、对展开推理需求有限以及任务难度未受控导致失败诊断性不足等问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 构建了包含4个领域和7个技能标签的双层分类法，涵盖27个任务和810个实例，通过人机协作生成真实风格输入图像，并采用VLM作为评判器结合完整性（Completeness）与评分表（Rubric Score）的双重指标来评估目标完成度与过程有效性。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 即使是表现最强的Seedance 2.0模型总体得分也仅为51.0%，结构化谜题是最困难领域，拓扑和时间技能最弱；分析显示模型对输入条件敏感、合成数据微调的迁移效果有限，且去噪过程中的自我纠正率低于1%。

PAWBench: How Far Are We from Probabilistically Aligned World Modeling?

Published: 2026-08-26

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27345

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on evaluating video generation models as world models, specifically examining whether they can capture the full distribution of possible futures under a fixed observation and action, within the domain of computer vision and AI world modeling.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on advances in video generation (e.g., Veo, Kling, Seedance) and existing benchmarks like VBench and WorldModelBench that evaluate individual-video plausibility, the new idea is formalizing "probabilistic alignment" as a distributional criterion and introducing PAWBench to test whether models recover the correct outcome distribution rather than just a single plausible sample.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the gap that current video world model evaluations assess single-rollout plausibility but do not test whether repeated generations correctly recover the conditional distribution over physically valid futures, leaving unclear how far existing models are from being probabilistically aligned world models.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper introduces PAWBench with 50 scenarios across 8 mechanism groups (PAW-Calibration with reference distributions, PAW-Coverage for support recovery), PAWEval as an outcome-level readout protocol using Gemini 3.5 Flash, and probes three intervention interfaces: prompt engineering, coupled initial noise sampling (C2C), and LoRA fine-tuning to reshape model distributions.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across 11 video generators, no model consistently matches reference probabilities and recovers valid outcome support; models underreact to causal physical changes but overreact to non-causal cues, language steering and noise coupling provide only limited control, and LoRA training shifts outcome frequencies without achieving scene-conditioned probability alignment.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于将视频生成模型作为世界模型的评估，特别考察它们是否能在固定观察和动作下捕捉所有可能未来的分布，属于计算机视觉和人工智能世界建模领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 基于视频生成的最新进展（如Veo、Kling、Seedance）以及评估单视频合理性的现有基准（如VBench、WorldModelBench），新思路是将"概率对齐"形式化为分布准则，并引入PAWBench来测试模型是否能恢复正确的结果分布，而非仅仅生成一个合理的样本。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了当前视频世界模型评估仅评估单次推演的合理性，而未测试重复生成是否能正确恢复物理有效未来的条件分布的问题，揭示了现有模型距离概率对齐世界建模的差距。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文引入了包含50个场景、跨8个机制组的PAWBench（PAW-Calibration含参考分布，PAW-Coverage用于支持恢复），使用Gemini 3.5 Flash作为结果级读出协议的PAWEval，并通过三个干预接口进行探测：提示工程、耦合初始噪声采样（C2C）以及LoRA微调以重塑模型分布。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在11个视频生成器中，没有模型能同时一致地匹配参考概率并恢复有效结果支持；模型对因果物理变化反应不足，但对非因果线索反应过度；语言引导和噪声耦合仅提供有限控制，LoRA训练改变了结果频率但未实现场景条件化的概率对齐。

Paper 3

VBVR-Pro: A Scalable and Verifiable Suite for Native Visual Reasoning

Published: 2026-08-25

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.26105

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper presents VBVR-Pro, a scalable and verifiable testbed in the domain of native visual reasoning through generative models (image, video, and interleaved text-image generation).
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The work builds on the VBVR suite and existing visual reasoning benchmarks but introduces a new closed-loop framework that unifies procedurally generated tasks, task-grounded verifiable reward scorers, and multi-task reinforcement learning to make native visual reasoning trainable, verifiable, and optimizable.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses the lack of scalable training data, reliable evaluation methods, and controlled comparison infrastructure for native visual reasoning, particularly the unreliability of the prevalent VLM-as-a-judge paradigm for tasks requiring precise verification.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors construct 300 procedurally generated visual reasoning tasks with aligned video/image/interleaved modalities, design deterministic task-specific verifiable reward scorers based on semantic extraction, conduct controlled benchmarking across 30+ generative models, and implement reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) using Coefficients-Preserving Sampling (CPS).
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Training on VBVR-Pro yields an average improvement of 0.290 across models and strong transfer (up to 20 percentage points) to seven external benchmarks; the verifiable scorers achieve higher human alignment than VLM judges at lower cost; and RLVR outperforms RLVLM (0.548 vs. 0.508 overall) with consistent gains on both in-domain and out-of-domain tasks.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 该论文提出了VBVR-Pro，一个用于原生视觉推理的可扩展且可验证的测试平台，属于通过生成模型（图像、视频和交错文本-图像生成）进行视觉推理的研究领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该工作基于VBVR套件和现有视觉推理基准，但引入了一个新的闭环框架，统一了程序生成的任务、基于任务的可验证奖励评分器以及多任务强化学习，使原生视觉推理变得可训练、可验证和可优化。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决了原生视觉推理缺乏可扩展训练数据、可靠评估方法以及跨生成模态受控比较基础设施的问题，特别是针对现有VLM作为评判范式在需要精确验证的任务中不可靠的问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者构建了300个程序生成的视觉推理任务，对齐视频/图像/交错模态，设计了基于语义提取的确定性任务特定可验证奖励评分器，对30多个生成模型进行受控基准测试，并使用系数保留采样（CPS）实现可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在VBVR-Pro上训练可使模型平均提升0.290，并在七个外部基准测试中实现强迁移（最多20个百分点）；可验证评分器在更低的成本下实现了比VLM评判器更高的人类一致性；RLVR优于RLVLM（整体0.548对比0.508），在域内和域外任务上均获得持续提升。
