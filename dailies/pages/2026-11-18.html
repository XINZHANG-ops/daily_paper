Paper 1

TeleChat to Xing4: Advancing Frontier Agentic Models on Domestic Chips and Frameworks

Published: 2026-11-16

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22115

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This technical report presents Xing 4, a family of sparse MoE foundation models and an Ascend/MindSpore training stack for long-context reasoning and agentic workloads.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Extending TeleChat and prior work on MLA, DSA, mHC, MTP, IndexShare, speculative decoding, FSDP, and Muon, it co-designs wide-and-shallow MoE architectures with topology-aware HSDP, owner-executed Muon, operator-level recomputation, DSA workload compaction, CPU isolation, and TeleGym-based asynchronous reinforcement learning.
3. ❓ Problem: It addresses how to scale high-capacity, million-context agentic models on domestic Ascend hardware while reducing KV-cache and activation memory, pipeline and communication overhead, host-scheduling jitter, and rollout stragglers in heterogeneous multi-turn RL.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The study evaluates an open 29B-total/4B-active MoE and a 285B-total/17B-active configuration using MLA+DSA+IndexShare, wider expert layouts, DSpark, HyperParallel HSDP, owner-based Muon, selective checkpointing, document-aware DSA balancing, and TeleGym/Slime partial-rollout training.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Simulation and cluster measurements report up to 4.68× prefill and 2.67–3.77× decoding speedups, a 1.14× gain over 1F1B pipelining, 9.1%, 6.9%, and 15.2% step-time reductions from three pretraining optimizations, and 5–10% large-cluster throughput gains from CPU isolation, although the attention results are partly simulation-based and comprehensive model-quality improvements are not demonstrated.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本技术报告介绍了Xing 4稀疏混合专家基础模型系列，以及面向长上下文推理和智能体任务的昇腾与MindSpore训练技术栈。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文在TeleChat及MLA、DSA、mHC、MTP、IndexShare、推测解码、FSDP和Muon等工作基础上，联合设计宽而浅的MoE架构、拓扑感知HSDP、所有者执行Muon、算子级重计算、DSA负载压缩、CPU隔离及基于TeleGym的异步强化学习。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文旨在解决在国产昇腾硬件上扩展高容量、百万上下文智能体模型时，KV缓存与激活内存、流水线和通信开销、主机调度抖动以及异构多轮RL中长尾 rollout 等问题。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 研究评估了公开的29B总参数/4B激活参数MoE和285B总参数/17B激活参数配置，并结合使用MLA+DSA+IndexShare、宽专家布局、DSpark、HyperParallel HSDP、所有者式Muon、选择性检查点、文档感知DSA负载均衡以及TeleGym/Slime部分rollout训练。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 仿真与集群测试报告了最高4.68×的预填充和2.67–3.77×的解码加速、相对1F1B流水线的1.14×提速、三项预训练优化分别带来的9.1%、6.9%和15.2%步时下降，以及大集群中CPU隔离带来的5–10%吞吐提升，但注意力结果部分来自仿真，且未充分展示全面的模型质量提升。

Paper 2

Weight-Space Programming Doesn't Need Transformers

Published: 2026-11-16

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22055

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This preprint studies weight-space programming, where frontier LLMs directly write target-model weights and architectures without gradient-based training, with emphasis on neural architecture design and associative memory.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: Building on prior WSP, Transformers-as-associative-memory analyses, neural architecture search, and model editing, the paper lets LLMs choose their architecture and introduces a fixed hashed sparse-table architecture that preserves line-by-line writable knowledge.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper asks whether Transformers remain appropriate for WSP when their gradient-optimization and dense-GPU advantages no longer apply, seeking models whose weights are traceable, locally editable, accurate, and efficient.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The authors gave three frontier LLMs ten labeled ARC examples and a sandbox, compared fixed written architectures with freely designed models and iterative lineages, and conducted locality, BM25, contamination, benchmark, pretraining, generation, vision, and RTX 3090 speed experiments.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: All writers converged on sparse fact tables, a 0.94M-parameter design beat the best 1.72B-parameter written released architecture using about 1,800× fewer parameters, the strongest lineage reached 54.4% ARC accuracy, and the authors’ fixed associative-memory model remained within about three points while running up to 17× faster than same-sized Transformers.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本预印本研究权重空间编程，即前沿大语言模型在无梯度训练的情况下直接编写目标模型的权重与架构，重点涉及神经架构设计和联想记忆。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文以既有权重空间编程、Transformer作为联想记忆的分析、神经架构搜索和模型编辑研究为基础，首次让大语言模型自主选择架构，并提出保留逐行知识编写能力的固定哈希稀疏表架构。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文考察当梯度优化和密集GPU优势不再存在时，Transformer是否仍适用于权重空间编程，并寻求权重可追溯、修改局部化、准确且高效的新架构。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 作者向三个前沿大语言模型提供十个带标签的ARC样例和沙箱，比较固定架构与自主设计模型及多代迭代，并开展局部性、BM25、污染、其他基准、预训练权重、生成、视觉和RTX 3090速度实验。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 三位编写者均选择了稀疏事实表，其中一个仅0.94M参数的模型以约1,800倍更少参数超过了最佳的1.72B参数已发布架构，最强谱系达到54.4%的ARC准确率，而作者提出的固定联想记忆模型精度仅低约三个百分点，却可比同规模Transformer快至多17倍。
