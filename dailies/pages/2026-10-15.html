Paper 1

Any Time, Any Space: A World-State-Centric Framework for Agentic Video Generation

Published: 2026-10-13

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.20571

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: The paper focuses on long-form agentic video generation, specifically multi-shot video synthesis that requires maintaining spatial and temporal consistency across nonconsecutive shots, situated in computer vision and generative AI.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: It builds upon text-to-video/image-to-video models, agentic video systems (e.g., VGoT, Mora, MovieAgent, ReCA), and explicit spatial memory methods (e.g., 3D Gaussian Splatting, neural rendering); its new idea is a world-state-centric framework that maintains two complementary persistent memories—a spatially registered scene memory and a time-indexed event memory—together with a validation-controlled update mechanism.
3. ❓ Problem: Existing agentic video systems cannot preserve spatial layout or replay historical events across non-local, non-consecutive shot requests, because they rely on prompts, recent frames, or text summaries that lack a shared spatial frame and event-time index, leading to inconsistent revisits of earlier scenes and events.
4. 🛠️ Methods: ATAS uses a world-state compiler that recursively decomposes screenplays into bounded shot requests with explicit scene, camera, and event-time references; retrieves registered static geometry from scene memory and dynamic observations from event memory; generates clips via a short-video generator; and updates persistent state only after passing film review, registration, and state-update admission checks.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Across four video backbones, ATAS improves all reported cross-shot consistency measures and achieves the highest aggregate benchmark score per backbone, outperforms compared agents and 3D-guided methods on all three spatial-revisit metrics (PSNRC 15.968, SSIMC 0.385), leads three of five event-recapture criteria, and receives the highest mean human rating on all six evaluation criteria.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 论文聚焦于长篇代理式视频生成，具体涉及跨多镜头的视频合成，需要在非连续镜头之间保持空间和时间一致性，属于计算机视觉与生成式人工智能领域。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 该研究建立在文生视频/图生视频模型、代理式视频系统（如VGoT、Mora、MovieAgent、ReCA）以及显式空间记忆方法（如3D高斯泼溅、神经渲染）的基础上；其新思路是采用以世界状态为中心的框架，维护两种互补的持久记忆——空间配准的场景记忆和按时间索引的事件记忆，并配合验证控制的状态更新机制。
3. ❓ 问题: 现有的代理式视频系统无法在非局部、非连续的镜头请求中保持空间布局或重放历史事件，因为它们依赖提示词、最近帧或文本摘要，缺乏共享的空间坐标系和事件时间索引，导致对早期场景和事件的回访不一致。
4. 🛠️ 方法: ATAS采用世界状态编译器，将剧本递归分解为带有显式场景、相机和事件时间引用的有界镜头请求；从场景记忆中检索已配准的静态几何信息，从事件记忆中检索动态观测；通过短视频生成器生成片段；仅在通过影片审查、配准和状态更新准入检查后才更新持久状态。
5. 📊 结果与评估: 在四个视频骨干网络中，ATAS改进了所有报告的跨镜头一致性指标，并在每个骨干上取得最高的聚合基准分数；在所有三个空间回访指标上优于对比的代理方法和3D引导方法（PSNRC 15.968，SSIMC 0.385）；在五个事件重捕标准中领先三项，并在全部六项评估标准上获得最高的人类评分均值。

Player-1: Learning to Play from Synthetic Experience

Published: 2026-10-13

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.20584

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on game-control agents, specifically building a post-training framework that combines supervised initialization with reinforcement learning using synthetic environments for general digital control through games.
2. 💡 Previous Research and New Ideas: The paper builds upon foundation models (GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini), computer-use agents (UI-TARS, Qwen-CUA), and game agents (Game-TARS, SIMA 2, NitroGen, Odysseus), as well as RL with verifiable rewards (RLVR) approaches. The new idea is using synthetic experience in 16 controllable environments with verifiable rewards to teach transferable game-control capabilities beyond supervised demonstrations, without requiring RL in each target game.
3. ❓ Problem: The paper addresses three challenges: (1) gameplay demonstrations only cover limited game states; (2) scalable interactive training environments for games are difficult to obtain; and (3) experience from diverse environments with different dynamics must be incorporated into a shared policy that generalizes beyond the training suite.
4. 🛠️ Methods: The paper extends the computer-use action space with relative pointer motion, sustained inputs, and composite actions; performs SFT on 6,044 curated gameplay trajectories with task instructions and visual reasoning; constructs PLAYER-1-GYM with 16 synthetic 2D/3D environments featuring verifiable rewards and randomized observations/control dynamics; and applies multi-environment PPO with per-environment advantage normalization.
5. 📊 Results and Evaluation: Player-1-9B achieves 38.33% success on MineDojo-300 (a 27.8 percentage-point gain over SFT and exceeding the strongest baseline at 12.63%), Player-1-35B-A3B reaches 41.04% (17.7 pp gain over SFT), and both models show further generalization gains on ViZDoom-10 and GameWorld outside the RL training suite; broader synthetic environment coverage improves transfer, and composite-action use rises from 64.7% to 93.2% during RL.
1. 📘 主题与领域: 本论文聚焦于游戏控制智能体，具体提出一种结合监督初始化与强化学习的后训练框架，通过合成环境实现面向通用数字控制（以游戏为载体）的智能体学习。
2. 💡 先前研究与新思路: 论文基于基础模型（GPT-4o、Claude、Gemini）、计算机使用智能体（UI-TARS、Qwen-CUA）以及游戏智能体（Game-TARS、SIMA 2、NitroGen、Odysseus）和带可验证奖励的强化学习（RLVR）。新思路是利用包含可验证奖励的16个可控合成环境的合成经验来教授可迁移的游戏控制能力，无需在每个目标游戏中进行在线强化学习。
3. ❓ 问题: 论文解决三大挑战：（1）游戏演示仅覆盖有限的游戏状态；（2）游戏的可扩展交互式训练环境难以获取；（3）来自不同动力学环境（动力差异）的经验必须被整合到一个能泛化到训练套件之外的共享策略中。
4. 🛠️ 方法: 论文扩展了计算机使用动作空间，加入相对指针运动、持续输入和复合动作；在6044条带任务指令和视觉推理的精选游戏轨迹上进行SFT；构建包含16个合成2D/3D环境的PLAYER-1-GYM，提供可验证奖励和随机化的观测/控制动力学；并采用带按环境优势归一化的多环境PPO进行训练。
5. 📊 结果与评估: Player-1-9B在MineDojo-300上达到38.33%的成功率（较SFT提升27.8个百分点，超过最强基线12.63%），Player-1-35B-A3B达到41.04%（较SFT提升17.7个百分点），两个模型在RL训练套件之外的ViZDoom-10和GameWorld上均表现出进一步的泛化提升；更广泛的合成环境覆盖改善了迁移能力，复合动作使用率在RL过程中从64.7%上升到93.2%。

Doc2Harness: Benchmarking LLMs on Long-Horizon Harness Engineering of Coding Agents

Published: 2026-10-13

Link: http://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.20344

1. 📘 Topic and Domain: This paper focuses on benchmarking large language models' ability to perform long-horizon harness engineering for coding agents, situated in the domain of software engineering evaluation and AI agent benchmarking. 2. **💡 Previous Research and New Ideas:** Building on prior SWE benchmarks (e.g., SWE-bench Pro, NL2Repo-Bench, Commit0) and harness benchmarks (e.g., Claw-SWE-Bench, Harness-Bench), the paper introduces the new idea of Doc2Harness, a deterministic two-stage benchmark that directly evaluates how well a model develops a harness rather than inferring harness quality from downstream task performance. 3. **❓ Problem:** The paper addresses the fact that existing benchmarks measure downstream task success under a fixed harness, leaving the critical capability of harness engineering itself unexplored and lacking a deterministic evaluation method. 4. **🛠️ Methods:** The paper employs a two-stage benchmark in which Stage 1 has a build model construct a complete candidate harness from a development document and an interface-only contract skeleton inside an isolated container, and Stage 2 evaluates the frozen candidate harness by driving it end-to-end with a scripted mock model, observing four channels (requests, files, exit codes, command output) and using positive controls to rule out vacuous passes. 5. **📊 Results and Evaluation:** Across nineteen frontier models evaluated on 328 test cases, GPT-6 Astra achieves the highest pass rate at 53.05% while the weakest reaches 27.44% (a gap of ~26 points); even the strongest model fails nearly half the test cases, and failures concentrate on module-level fragmentation of cross-cutting features.

Today's Reading Tips 今日阅读推荐

I'd recommend reading the Doc2Harness paper first: it introduces a clean, deterministic two-stage benchmark that exposes a fundamental gap in how we evaluate coding agents—measuring harness-engineering capability directly rather than inferring it from downstream performance—and its results show even GPT-6 Astra fails nearly half the cases, making it a strong conversation starter. It also connects naturally to the other two papers: Player-1 shares the spirit of verifiable, controlled evaluation through synthetic environments and verifiable rewards, while ATAS tackles a complementary 'persistent state' problem in a very different domain (multi-shot video generation), so reading Doc2Harness first gives you a framework for thinking about agent memory, state, and rigor that carries over to both.
建议先读 Doc2Harness：这篇论文提出了一个干净的、两阶段的确定性基准，揭示了当前编码智能体评估中的一个根本性盲区——即直接衡量"工具链工程能力"，而不是从下游任务表现间接推断，并且其结果显示即便是 GPT-6 Astra 也有近一半用例无法通过，非常适合作为讨论的切入点。它与其他两篇论文也有自然的联系：Player-1 同样强调通过合成环境和可验证奖励实现可控、可验证的评估；而 ATAS 则在多镜头视频生成这一完全不同的领域中处理了一个互补的"持久状态"问题，因此先读 Doc2Harness 能帮你建立一个关于智能体记忆、状态与评估严谨性的思考框架，自然地迁移到另外两篇。
